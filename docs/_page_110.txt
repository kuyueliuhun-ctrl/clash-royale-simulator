Surround your enemies with this pile of bones!\u201d</b></i></div>\n<p>The Skeletons is a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Common_Cards\" title=\"Category:Common Cards\"><span style=\"color: #9ECAFF; font-weight: bold;\">Common</span></a> <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> that is unlocked from the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Bone Pit (Arena 2)</a>. It spawns three fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> with very low hitpoints and very low damage. Their deployment is in a triangle formation. They appear as small undead creatures with a skull that lacks a lower jaw and has a scar on the left eye and wield short swords. They are also the same as those spawned by the <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Tombstone\" title=\"Tombstone\">Tombstone</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Barrel\" title=\"Skeleton Barrel\">Skeleton Barrel</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Army\" title=\"Skeleton Army\">Skeleton Army</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Super_Witch\" title=\"Super Witch\">Super Witch</a>. A Skeletons card costs 1 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> to deploy.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Strategy\">Strategy</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a title=\"Sign in to edit\" class=\"mw-editsection-visualeditor\" data-tracking-label=\"log-in-edit-section\" data-action=\"edit-section\" href=\"https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fclashroyale.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkeletons%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en&amp;metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section\" data-testid=\"log-in-edit-section\" aria-label=\"Sign in to edit\"><svg class=\"wds-icon wds-icon-tiny\" aria-hidden=\"true\" focusable=\"false\"><use xlink:href=\"#wds-icons-pencil-tiny\"></use></svg></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Skeletons_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Skeletons Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>The Skeletons are most effectively used as a disposable distraction for medium to high-hitpoint units, such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Dragon\" title=\"Inferno Dragon\">Inferno Dragon</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"P.E.K.K.A.\">P.E.K.K.A.</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mighty_Miner\" title=\"Mighty Miner\">Mighty Miner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Mini_P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"Mini P.E.K.K.A.\">Mini P.E.K.KA.</a>, since they can stop charged up abilities and distract enemies. Against <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">building</a>-targeting <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> unable to fight them, they provide a high amount of damage per second for 1 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a>, and they can stall single-targeting troops for a relatively long time considering their Elixir cost. Although they will often not counter units by themselves, the support and time they provide for other units is valuable. However, they will quickly be defeated by ranged area damage units like the <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard\" title=\"Wizard\">Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bowler\" title=\"Bowler\">Bowler</a>, Executioner, <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber\" title=\"Bomber\">Bomber</a>, so the player should try to defeat or distract these types of units beforehand.</li>\n<li>Generally, it is risky to use a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spell</a> on Skeletons, as their 1 Elixir cost means any spell will be a negative Elixir trade. Plus, even a Level 1 <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Tower_Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Tower Troop Cards\">Crown Tower</a> can fully take them down without taking any damage. However, if the opponent is relying on Skeletons in order to defend, a quick spell can allow for major damage to the opposing Crown Tower. If the player knows that the opponent will use Skeletons against a remaining Prince, they can use a <a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a> spell as soon as they see the Skeletons, potentially allowing the Prince to deal damage it would not have done otherwise or forcing the opponent to use another <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, making up for the Elixir deficit.</li>\n<li>The Skeletons can be used to counter a lone <a href=\"/wiki/Princess\" title=\"Princess\">Princess</a>. If played just after the Princess fires, the Skeletons will eliminate her quickly. Be careful, as if they are deployed with bad timing, the Princess will easily eliminate the Skeletons with one hit. The Princess player may also quickly react with a spell on the Skeletons, allowing her to continue firing at the Tower, so be prepared to follow up if necessary.</li>\n<li>With the help of a Crown Tower, Skeletons can counter medium-health troops like the <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Night_Witch\" title=\"Night Witch\">Night Witch</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a>, Miner, Bandit, <a href=\"/wiki/Executioner\" title=\"Executioner\">Executioner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Cage\" title=\"Goblin Cage\">Goblin Brawler</a>, and Mini P.E.K.K.A.. Against single-targeting ranged troops, one should place the Skeletons on top of the troop to negate the range advantage, Against melee troops, the Skeletons should be placed in the center so that the troop has to walk farther, giving the Crown Tower more time to chip it down and the Skeletons time to deploy.</li>\n<li>Their 1 Elixir cost makes them an effective distraction against a <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>, leaving the player with more Elixir to deploy other units to destroy her. Skeletons can also be deployed directly on top of her to surround her, which will counter her for a 5 Elixir advantage with the help of a Crown Tower. However, an adept Sparky player may preemptively Zap on top of their own Sparky as it crosses the bridge. Players relying on Skeletons to surround and counter her in this situation this will suffer major damage to their Crown Tower, so the player should switch up their tactics.</li>\n<li>If the opponent places an <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Tower\" title=\"Inferno Tower\">Inferno Tower</a> to counter a tank, Skeletons can stall the Inferno Tower for a short time, giving the defender time to place another card to counter it or reset its damage ramp-up. This also works against the <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Dragon\" title=\"Inferno Dragon\">Inferno Dragon</a>.</li>\n<li>Since it costs only 1 Elixir, it can shuffle the player's <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">deck</a> quickly and add a significant damage boost to an attack. Better yet, since they are also easily defeated by many cards and Crown Towers, not many players are willing to play cards to only take out Skeletons, given that their 1 Elixir cost will almost always result in a negative Elixir trade for the player countering them. They are very good to be split behind the <a href=\"/wiki/King%27s_Tower\" title=\"King&#39;s Tower\">King Tower</a> while shuffling the player's deck, as it can discourage the opponent from immediately attacking either lane slightly.</li>\n<li>Skeletons can provide a cheap, but threatening boost to a small attack due to their Fast move speed and high combined damage. For example, a <a href=\"/wiki/Miner\" title=\"Miner\">Miner</a> accompanied by Skeletons will be much more threatening as the opponent will take much more damage if they ignore it, as opposed to ignoring a single Miner.</li>\n<li>Skeletons can be used along with certain other cards like <a href=\"/wiki/Knight\" title=\"Knight\">Knight</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a>, or Mini P.E.K.K.A. to easily counter high damage single-targeting troops like <a href=\"/wiki/Elite_Barbarians\" title=\"Elite Barbarians\">Elite Barbarians</a>, Mighty Miner, P.E.K.K.A, and Prince. Use the Skeletons to pull them into the center while absorbing some of the damage then place down Knight or Mini P.E.K.K.A. to finish them off. This can then assist in a counterattack.</li>\n<li>If the player thinks their opponent will try and send a <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Barrel\" title=\"Goblin Barrel\">Goblin Barrel</a> far behind their Crown Tower, they can try and activate their King's Tower. The Skeletons should be split behind the player's King's Tower a few tiles before the barrel's shadow crosses the river. 1 Skeleton should go in the lane of the barrel while the 2 go in the other lane. If the opponent does send the barrel to the back of the player's Crown Tower, the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a> will defeat the Skeletons and 1 Goblin will head towards the King's Tower afterwards instead of towards the Crown Tower. Even if the opponent does not send the barrel behind the Tower, the player will have sufficient Elixir to counter the regular placement.</li>\n<li>Against an offensive <a href=\"/wiki/Mortar\" title=\"Mortar\">Mortar</a> on the center of the bridge, meet the Mortar vertically and play the Skeletons on the first tile in front of the King's Tower slightly before the Mortar's deploy timer is three fourths of the way finished. The Mortar will fire its shell at the Skeletons just as they are deployed, and the area damage effect will clip the King's Tower, activating it.</li>\n<li>Skeletons can fully counter a <a href=\"/wiki/Bandit\" title=\"Bandit\">Bandit</a>. Before the Bandit is about to dash, place the Skeletons on the third tile vertically from the King's Tower and the third tile horizontally from the Crown Tower. The front Skeleton should appear to be slightly more forward than the Crown Tower. If executed correctly, the Bandit will be in range of both Crown Towers after her dash, resulted in her being whittled down quickly and getting no hits on the player's Tower.</li>\n<li>Skeletons have very high DPS/Cost ratio. A strategy is to place an <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Golem\" title=\"Ice Golem\">Ice Golem</a> to distract a troop, and Skeletons behind the attacking troop to severely cripple them, if not, destroy them.</li>\n<li>Skeletons can be used as a cheap way to kite air troops placed at the center of the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Arena</a>. Split the Skeletons so that one goes into the lane the air troops is staggered towards, and two go into the other lane. The single Skeleton will tank an attack and buy time for the two Skeletons in the other lane to distance themselves from the air unit, allowing them to live longer. However, this will not work against the <a href=\"/wiki/Flying_Machine\" title=\"Flying Machine\">Flying Machine</a>, as its sight range is equal to its attack range, meaning that if an enemy walks out of its attack range, it won't try to follow them. To counteract this, players should place Skeletons in front of the Flying Machine to distract it.</li>\n<li>Placing Skeletons and then placing down a single grounded unit on the same tile right afterwards will cause them to spread further apart from each other while deploying, allowing them to be targeted individually by certain area damage attackers like the <a href=\"/wiki/Baby_Dragon\" title=\"Baby Dragon\">Baby Dragon</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Dragons\" title=\"Skeleton Dragons\">Skeleton Dragons</a>, making them more effective at stalling these units.\n<ul><li>This tactic is most effective if the grounded unit is a ranged attacker, as it will not continue to collide with the Skeletons upon deployment and will not be susceptible to area damage damage until after all of them are defeated.</li>\n<li>This tactic is ineffective against cards with a large enough area damage radius, such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a> and the <a href=\"/wiki/Dark_Prince\" title=\"Dark Prince\">Dark Prince</a> with their jump and charge attacks respectively.</li></ul></li></ul>\n<center><div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\"><h2 \"<i>\u03c3\u03ba\u03b5\u03bb\u03b5\u03c4\u03cc\u03c2</i>\" (<i>skelet\u00f3s</i>), meaning dried up or withered.\n<ul><li>The Skeletons, <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber\" title=\"Bomber\">Bomber</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Minions\" title=\"Minions\">Minions</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Archers\" title=\"Archers\">Archers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarians\" Breakers\">Wall Breakers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bowler\" title=\"Bowler\">Bowler</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Cannon_Cart\" title=\"Cannon Cart\">Cannon Cart</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Phoenix\" title=\"Phoenix\">Phoenix</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Lava_Hound\" title=\"Lava Hound\">Lava Hound</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Archer_Queen\" title=\"Archer Queen\">Archer Queen</a>.\n<ul><li>It is the only troop that is only available as a sub-troop in Clash of Clans.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Skeletons are 1 of 3 troops which can be spawned by a troop (Skeletons/<a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Army\" title=\"Skeleton Army\">Skeleton Army</a>), a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spell</a> (<a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a>), and a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">building</a> (Tombstone).
