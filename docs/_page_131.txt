It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troop</a> with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. When the Mother Witch attacks a troop, that troop will have a curse placed on it for 5 seconds. If the troop dies during the curse's duration, a Cursed Hog will spawn, which has moderate hitpoints and very low damage. She appears to be an elderly <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a> wearing a lighter-colored cloak with a pink outline and a team-colored patch on its front and back, shorter purple gloves with team-colored cuffs, and a belt with a skull face in the middle, has glowing blue eyes and purple nail paint, wields a staff with a cauldron placed on top, and carries a wooden spoon. The Cursed Hog resembles a <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Hogs\" title=\"Royal Hogs\">Royal Hog</a> without its helmet and has a team-colored band on its tusks and a skull logo on the side of its back. A Mother Witch card costs 4 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> to deploy.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Modifiers\">Modifiers</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span Mother Witch\u201d</b></i></div>\n<p>Curses a troop to spawn in another Mother Witch instead of a cursed Hog.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Strategy\">Strategy</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Mother_Witch_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Mother Witch Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>Any <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> that spawn sub-troops (smaller variants) upon death will not spawn a Cursed Hog. For example, <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Golem\" title=\"Elixir Golem\">Elixir Golems</a>, Elixir Golemites, <a href=\"/wiki/Lava_Hound\" title=\"Lava Hound\">Lava Hounds</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golems</a> do not turn into Cursed Hogs (they do not even show the tint of being cursed), but Elixir Blobs and Golemites do. Only a troop's final stage can turn into a Cursed Hog. As such, the <a href=\"/wiki/Cannon_Cart\" title=\"Cannon Cart\">Cannon Cart</a> can never get cursed since its final form is a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">building</a>. The <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Barrel\" title=\"Skeleton Barrel\">Skeleton Barrel</a> also cannot turn into a Cursed Hog, but the <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarians\" title=\"Barbarians\">Barbarians</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a> which spawn after its destruction can be turned into Cursed Hogs.\n<ul><li>The spawned sub-troops are not automatically cursed when they spawn from their parent troops, even if the parent troops were killed or damaged by Mother Witch. The sub-troops need to specifically be targeted and damaged/killed by Mother Witch post-spawn in order for them to spawn a Cursed Hog on death.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Giant\" title=\"Goblin Giant\">Goblin Giant</a> is an exception to this rule, as both he and his 2 <a href=\"/wiki/Spear_Goblins\" title=\"Spear Goblins\">Spear Goblins</a> can be turned into Cursed Hogs.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Phoenix\" title=\"Phoenix\">Phoenix</a> is another exception to this rule, as it can turn into a Cursed Hog despite spawning a Phoenix Egg on death. Both the initially deployed Phoenix and the Phoenix respawned from an egg are capable of turning into Cursed Hogs. However, the Phoenix Egg is itself immune to the curse.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Any <a href=\"/wiki/Clone\" title=\"Clone\">Cloned</a> troops that the Mother Witch defeats will turn into Cloned Cursed Hogs. A Cloned Mother Witch will spawn regular Cursed Hogs if attacking non-cloned troops.</li>\n<li>Cursed Hogs cannot be inflicted with a curse, but the Mother Witch herself is vulnerable to being cursed.</li>\n<li>The cursed effect does not stack. If two Mother Witches attacked a single unit, and it triggered the curse's effect, only one Cursed Hog would spawn. However, it can stack with the curse effect of the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Curse\" title=\"Goblin Curse\">Goblin Curse</a>, allowing the troop to spawn both a Cursed Hog and a <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblin</a> out of it.</li>\n<li><a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Spirit\" title=\"Ice Spirit\">Ice Spirit</a> and the like, along with the <a href=\"/wiki/Wall_Breakers\" title=\"Wall Breakers\">Wall Breakers</a> will not transform into Cursed Hogs if they are destroyed as part of their attack, even if they are cursed.</li>\n<li>Effects that activate after death will still activate even if the Mother Witch successfully converts a troop into a Cursed Hog. For example, the <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Golem\" title=\"Ice Golem\">Ice Golem</a> will still deal its full death damage, and it will slow down nearby units, as well as the Cursed Hog that spawns after it is defeated. As such, she is slightly less effective against these units.</li>\n<li>Any ranged attacks that have already initiated after the ranged troop was killed will continue until they travel to their maximum distance or their target. For example, the <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a>'s bullets will still fire and deal damage to the Cursed Hog that spawns, destroying it immediately, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Executioner\" title=\"Executioner\">Executioner</a>'s axe will still fly the full distance even after being converted into a Cursed Hog.</li>\n<li>Mother Witch is good as support for a tank such as <a href=\"/wiki/Giant\" title=\"Giant\">Giant</a> or Golem, because she denies fragile troops being used to defeat the tank. Otherwise, the opponent would have to deal with Cursed Hogs in addition to the current push.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Giant\" title=\"Electro Giant\">Electro Giant</a> synergizes with the Mother Witch, as his reflected damage can allow the Mother Witch's curse to take effect more easily. However, they cost 11 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> together.</li>\n<li>Unlike the <a href=\"/wiki/Ram_Rider\" title=\"Ram Rider\">Ram Rider</a>, she cannot switch her attack to other troops, meaning she can be easily distracted by a tank or a mini tank.</li>\n<li>The Mother Witch is good against swarm units because she has a fairly fast attack speed and can easily take them out and curse all of them to create a huge army of Cursed Hogs. She is particularly effective against Skeletons, <a href=\"/wiki/Bats\" title=\"Bats\">Bats</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Spear_Goblins\" title=\"Spear Goblins\">Spear Goblins</a> as she is able to one-shot them (i.e., immediately create Cursed Hogs). However, she still fares well against <a href=\"/wiki/Minions\" title=\"Minions\">Minions</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a>, which will get two-shot, but the Cursed Hogs may be damaged down more due to the swarm staying alive for longer. Conversely, she gets countered by mini-tanks like the <a href=\"/wiki/Knight\" title=\"Knight\">Knight</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mighty_Miner\" title=\"Mighty Miner\">Mighty Miner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Lumberjack\" title=\"Lumberjack\">Lumberjack</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mini_P.E.K.K.A\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"Mini P.E.K.K.A\">Mini P.E.K.K.A</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Monk\" title=\"Monk\">Monk</a>, Executioner, <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Ghost\" title=\"Royal Ghost\">Royal Ghost</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Dark_Prince\" title=\"Dark Prince\">Dark Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Golden_Knight\" title=\"Golden Knight\">Golden Knight</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_King\" title=\"Skeleton King\">Skeleton King</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a> as she cannot do enough damage.</li>\n<li>The best way of preventing <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Tower_Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Tower Troop Cards\">Crown Tower</a> damage against a lone Mother Witch with swarm cards is to simply wait for her to lock onto the Crown Tower before deploying the swarm, so that she does not curse them. This prevents much more damage than trying to have the swarm tank for the Crown Tower, as Cursed Hogs will quickly accumulate. The player should make sure to place the swarm behind her, rather than in front of her, to prevent accidentally pushing her away from the Crown Tower and retargeting onto the swarm. However, if the swarm is large or bulky enough, as is the case with a <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Army\" title=\"Skeleton Army\">Skeleton Army</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Minion_Horde\" title=\"Minion Horde\">Minion Horde</a>, Royal Recruits, or Barbarians, then placing it on top of her will prevent more damage despite the curse effect as the Cursed Hogs will also be defeated quickly.</li>\n<li>The Mother Witch is a perfect counter to <a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a>, as not only will she help the Tower take down the Skeletons, but she will curse a lot of Skeletons, translating the defense into a potentially massive counterattack. Also, the spawned Skeletons will spawn too slow enough to kill the Cursed Hogs.</li>\n<li>The Mother Witch wastes her potential when matched against tanks because it takes a long time to counter them for just one Cursed Hog.</li>\n<li>The Mother Witch is not very effective when paired with area damage units, as she herself does not have area damage. Against swarms, she will only be able to curse one or two units as the area damage unit will defeat them too quickly. However, she can be useful when paired with a <a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Snowball\" title=\"Giant Snowball\">Giant Snowball</a>, to allow her to OHKO Minions or Goblins.</li>\n<li>The Mother Witch is able to be fully defeated by an equally-levelled <a href=\"/wiki/Fireball\" title=\"Fireball\">Fireball</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Poison\" title=\"Poison\">Poison</a>. Therefore, it may be beneficial to bait out the Fireball/Poison for the Mother Witch with other Fireball/Poison-vulnerable <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">cards</a> like the <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarians\" title=\"Barbarians\">Barbarians</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Dragons\" title=\"Skeleton Dragons\">Skeleton Dragons</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Recruits\" title=\"Royal Recruits\">Royal Recruits</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard\" title=\"Wizard\">Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Flying_Machine\" title=\"Flying Machine\">Flying Machine</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Zappies\" title=\"Zappies\">Zappies</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Wizard\" title=\"Electro Wizard\">Electro Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Wizard\" title=\"Ice Wizard\">Ice Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Archer\" title=\"Magic Archer\">Magic Archer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Little_Prince\" title=\"Little Prince\">Little Prince</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Goblinstein\" title=\"Goblinstein\">Goblinstein</a> so that the opponent cannot take her out as easily when she is deployed. Conversely, she can act as the bait herself for the aforementioned cards in certain match-ups where her abilities are less impactful compared to the other cards.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Bomb_Tower\" title=\"Bomb Tower\">Bomb Tower</a> is an amazing counter against the Mother Witch. Since it is a building, it cannot be cursed by the Mother Witch, and its splash damage makes short work of the Cursed Hogs. Other buildings can work similarly, however they are less effective when the Mother Witch has already accumulated a large number of Cursed Hogs.</li></ul>\n<center><div id=\"unit-statistics\"
