It can only be deployed on the player's own side. It appears as a long, spiked piece of wood. Chop!</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h3>\n<div class=\"quote-block\"><i><b>\u201cSpawn lumberjack\u201d</b></i></div>\n<p>The Log spawns a <a href=\"/wiki/Lumberjack\" title=\"Lumberjack\">Lumberjack</a> after expiring.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Strategy\">Strategy</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_The_Log_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the The Log Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>As a cheap <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spell</a>, The Log can be used to damage, push back or even defeat multiple ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> that are approaching your side, such as <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarians\" title=\"Barbarians\">Barbarians</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Army\" title=\"Skeleton Army\">Skeleton Army</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Gang\" title=\"Goblin Gang\">Goblin Gang</a>.</li>\n<li>The Log deals enough damage to OHKO a <a href=\"/wiki/Princess\" title=\"Princess\">Princess</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Rascals\" title=\"Rascals\">Rascal Girl</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Dart_Goblin\" title=\"Dart Goblin\">Dart Goblin</a> of the same Level. However, it is not able to defeat them if they are at a higher Level than The Log.</li>\n<li>Unlike most other cards with a pushback effect (with the exception of the <a href=\"/wiki/Monk\" title=\"Monk\">Monk</a> or the <a href=\"/wiki/Little_Prince\" title=\"Little Prince\">Little Prince</a>'s ability), The Log can affect all ground troops. If a troop is locked into a target, the pushback caused by The Log will reset their attack animation. This is in contrast with the <a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Lightning\" title=\"Lightning\">Lightning</a>, as it will only pause the attack instead of fully resetting it, with the exception of <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>. There are many implications where the knockback can be useful.\n<ul><li>The Log's pushback can be used to force enemy troops directly in the center of the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Arena</a> to switch lanes by deploying The Log on the side of the troop opposite to where the player would like to redirect it. This can be useful to weaken the current defense or to weaken a counterattack. A scenario where this would be useful is if the opponent places down a <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golem</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Giant\" title=\"Giant\">Giant</a> against your <a href=\"/wiki/X-Bow\" title=\"X-Bow\">X-Bow</a>. The Log's knockback will send the tank out of range, letting your X-Bow connect to the <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Tower_Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Tower Troop Cards\">Crown Tower</a>.</li>\n<li>If a unit is walking right on the edge of the river on a player's side, the player can use The Log to push the unit over the river back to the opponent's side. This is a good way to delay slow-moving units like Giant, <a href=\"/wiki/Rune_Giant\" title=\"Rune Giant\">Rune Giant</a>, Golem, <a href=\"/wiki/Mighty_Miner\" title=\"Mighty Miner\">Mighty Miner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Skeleton\" title=\"Giant Skeleton\">Giant Skeleton</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_King\" title=\"Skeleton King\">Skeleton King</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Golem\" title=\"Elixir Golem\">Elixir Golem</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Machine\" title=\"Goblin Machine\">Goblin Machine</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"P.E.K.K.A.\">P.E.K.K.A.</a>.</li>\n<li>If the opponent places a <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Giant\" title=\"Royal Giant\">Royal Giant</a> in the extra space of the player's territory once a Tower has been destroyed, they may be able to use The Log to push him to the <a href=\"/wiki/King%27s_Tower\" title=\"King&#39;s Tower\">King's Tower</a> lane, protecting their frailer Tower. It could also be used to cause him to retarget to another <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">building</a> if the player places their building late.</li>\n<li>If a <a href=\"/wiki/Miner\" title=\"Miner\">Miner</a> is placed on the corner of the Crown Tower closest to the King's Tower, The Log can push the Miner towards the King's Tower, activating it.</li>\n<li>The Log's pushback can push the <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a> further away from his target, reducing his damage potential significantly.</li>\n<li>The Log's pushback can be used to push troops closer to the inside of the Arena. This can be helpful in situations such as when a <a href=\"/wiki/Bowler\" title=\"Bowler\">Bowler</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Archer\" title=\"Magic Archer\">Magic Archer</a> is pushed closer to an enemy troop so that their piercing attacks can reach the King Tower and activate it.</li>\n<li>While a <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a> is charging his jump attack, pushing back the Mega Knight while he is charging does not prevent him from jumping on his target. The same holds true for the <a href=\"/wiki/Bandit\" title=\"Bandit\">Bandit</a> and her dash attack.</li></ul></li>\n<li>It can be a cheaper alternative to <a href=\"/wiki/Arrows\" title=\"Arrows\">Arrows</a>, due to the very similar damage and area of effect.\n<ul><li>The main differences are that The Log costs 1 less <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> while being unable to affect air troops. As such, it might be advisable to bring other splash damage cards to deal with the likes of <a href=\"/wiki/Minions\" title=\"Minions\">Minions</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bats\" title=\"Bats\">Bats</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Minion_Horde\" title=\"Minion Horde\">Minion Horde</a>.</li>\n<li>It can also be an alternative to Zap, as both of them have the same Elixir cost. They can both disarm the charge attacks of <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Dark_Prince\" title=\"Dark Prince\">Dark Prince</a> if you have low Elixir or if your small units are out of rotation. However, they have slightly different effects when used. The Log will not reset a <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>, but a Zap will.</li>\n<li>Barring air troops, there are only 5 units that it cannot OHKO compared to Arrows, those being <a href=\"/wiki/Wall_Breakers\" title=\"Wall Breakers\">Wall Breakers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber\" title=\"Bomber\">Bomber</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Archers\" title=\"Archers\">Archers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Firecracker\" title=\"Firecracker\">Firecracker</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Golem\" title=\"Elixir Golem\">Elixir Blobs</a>. They will still be countered with the help of the Crown Tower.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Log is effective on offense when combined with grounded units. Any swarms placed on top of The Log will be immediately hit, and in most cases, completely eliminated. However, the player should be aware of the slightly staggered deploy time on all cards comprised of more than one unit besides <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a> and Skeleton Army, and deploy their The Log so that it will roll over the units once their hitbox spawns.</li>\n<li>Remember that, despite it being a spell, it can only be placed on the player's side, which reduces its effectiveness in certain situations. Because of this, The Log should not be used in Miner or <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Barrel\" title=\"Goblin Barrel\">Goblin Barrel</a> decks as it will not be able to reach them in time to save them from enemy swarms.</li>\n<li>The Log can be an effective counter to <a href=\"/wiki/Tombstone\" title=\"Tombstone\">Tombstones</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Drill\" title=\"Goblin Drill\">Goblin Drills</a>. If the building is at around half health, The Log can be used to destroy the building and the troops spawned afterward. Similarly, it can also be used on a <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a> to deal additional damage to the spawned Barbarians. However, this will not work against a <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Giant\" title=\"Goblin Giant\">Goblin Giant</a>.</li>\n<li>The Log, combined with one <a href=\"/wiki/Cannoneer\" title=\"Cannoneer\">Cannoneer</a> shot, can fully counter the <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Archer\" title=\"Magic Archer\">Magic Archer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mother_Witch\" title=\"Mother Witch\">Mother Witch</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Wizard\" title=\"Ice Wizard\">Ice Wizard</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Little_Prince\" title=\"Little Prince\">Little Prince</a> for a +2 and +1 Elixir trade respectively.</li>\n<li>The Log is a great counter to a Goblin Barrel. Although it is slightly harder to defend a Goblin Barrel with it compared to Arrows, it costs 1 less Elixir. It is also more effective in scenarios where the Goblin Barrel is accompanied with a tank, as The Log will also deal damage to the tank, and in cases where the tank is a swarm like a Skeleton Army, The Log will be able to defeat them both at once.\n<ul><li>Playing a Goblin Barrel 3 tiles above a Crown Tower looks very similar to one that is placed on the center of it (this is colloquially referred to as a \"tricky barrel\"). It may cause The Log played too far forward to miss one or more of the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a>, allowing them to sneakily deal damage</li>\n<li>The Log is wider compared to the <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarian_Barrel\" title=\"Barbarian Barrel\">Barbarian Barrel</a>. As such, placing the Goblin Barrel 1 tile on the side of the Tower will not work against a player with The Log, as it will be able to hit all 3 Goblins.</li>\n<li>The Log can only prevent all damage from a center-placed <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Barrel/Evolution\" title=\"Goblin Barrel/Evolution\">Evolved Goblin Barrel</a> if the <a href=\"/wiki/Dagger_Duchess\" title=\"Dagger Duchess\">Dagger Duchess</a> is in use with at least 3 daggers available. Make sure that you deploy The Log on the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a> side, while the Dagger Duchess attacks the Decoy Goblins side.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Log pairs very well with other spells, like <a href=\"/wiki/Fireball\" title=\"Fireball\">Fireball</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Poison\" title=\"Poison\">Poison</a>. The extra damage that The Log does is enough to finish off units that would survive those two spells like <a href=\"/wiki/Night_Witch\" title=\"Night Witch\">Night Witch</a>, Hunter, <a href=\"/wiki/Berserker\" title=\"Berserker\">Berserker</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bandit\" title=\"Bandit\">Bandit</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a>. In the case of the Poison, it will push them into the area of the spell, staying there for longer. However, this is mostly a negative Elixir trade, so try to get additional value by hitting other units as well.\n<ul><li>If the player uses a Fireball next to the Crown Tower and manages to hit a troop that survives it, it is better to wait until the enemy troop reaches the bridge and use The Log there, in hopes of hitting other troops that are used as a meat shield for the injured troop. This should not be done however if the troop has already crossed the bridge, as it would be more Elixir-efficient to let the Tower finish it off.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Log can be combined with Spirit <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">cards</a> similarly to the Spirit + Zap combination. Compared to Zap, The Log does more damage, which allows it to defeat up to Elixir Blobs with any Spirit, as opposed to Zap which requires a <a href=\"/wiki/Fire_Spirit\" title=\"Fire Spirit\">Fire Spirit</a> to reach the same threshold. In addition, the combination of The Log + a Spirit card is enough to defeat <a href=\"/wiki/Guards\" title=\"Guards\">Guards</a>, however, unlike the other troops, this requires The Log to hit first before the Spirit.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Hog_Rider\" title=\"Hog Rider\">Hog Rider</a>, Mega Knight, <a href=\"/wiki/Ram_Rider\" title=\"Ram Rider\">Ram Rider</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Hogs\" title=\"Royal Hogs\">Royal Hogs</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Mother_Witch\" title=\"Mother Witch\">Cursed Hogs</a> can unintentionally jump over The Log as they cannot be struck by it while they use their jump abilities. However, this is rare and should not be relied on.</li>\n<li>When timed right, the Magic Archer paired with The Log can stop 2 of the <a href=\"/wiki/Three_Musketeers\" title=\"Three Musketeers\">Three Musketeers</a> when split, while also getting chip damage towards the Crown Tower. This will leave the player with 3 spare Elixir to use on the third <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a> in the other lane.</li></ul>\n<center>\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" On the same day, a bug was discovered where the Log was not able to be deployed on top of <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">buildings</a>. The bug that prevented the Log from being deployed on top of buildings was also fixed.</li>\n<li>On 12/12/2017, the <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Electrifying_Update_(12/12/2017)\" title=\"Version History\">Electrifying Update</a>, decreased The Log\u2019s <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Crown_Towers\" title=\"Category:Crown Towers\">Crown Tower</a> damage to 35% of the full damage (from 40%).</li></ul>\n<p>2018\n</p>\n<ul><li>On 25/4/2018, the <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Clan_Wars_Update_(25/4/2018)\" title=\"Version History\">Clan Wars Update</a>, allowed The Log to be placed on the river if a Crown Tower has been already destroyed.</li>\n<li>On 5/9/2018, the <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#September_2018_Update_(5/9/2018)\" title=\"Version History\">September 2018 Update</a>, changed it so that <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Legendary_Cards\" title=\"Category:Legendary Cards\"><span class=\"text-legendary\" style=\"font-weight: bold;\">Legendary</span></a> cards start at Level 9 and end at Level 13 (from 1 to 5).</li>\n<li>On 1/10/2018, a <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Balance_Changes_(1/10/2018)\" title=\"Version History\">Balance Update</a>, fixed a bug damage.</li></ul>\n<p>2020\n</p>\n<ul><li>On 7/4/2020, a <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Balance_Changes_(7/4/2020)\" title=\"Version History\">Balance Update</a>, added a card render to The Log's card info screen.</li>\n<li>On 4/8/2020, a <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Balance_Changes_(4/8/2020)\" title=\"Version History\">Balance Update</a>, decreased The Log's rolling distance to 10.1 tile (from 11.1 tiles), and decreased its Crown Tower damage to 30% of the full damage (from 35%).</li>\n<li>On 4/12/2020, a <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Maintenance_Break_(4/12/2020)\" title=\"Version
