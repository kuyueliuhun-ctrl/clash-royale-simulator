“In a chilling return to the Arena, Hero Ice Golem keeps enemies at bay with a brain-freezing blizzard! The only things he has to fear are fireballs and a hot summer's day.”

The Hero Ice Golem is a Rare card that is unlocked by unlocking 200  and choosing the Hero Ice Golem. The Heroic Ice Golem takes the appearance of a large creature made out of snow with glowing yellow eyes and golden ice shards on his head, with his hands being also colored golden and his nose and rocks on his spine being in the form of coal. He also dons a team-colored cape, two heavy golden armpads and gauntlets, and a gold headpiece with the Hero Shard in the center. This card costs 2to put on the arena and an additional 2  to activate his ability.

Ability: Snowstorm
“Conjures a blizzard that damages and slows enemies.”

The Hero Ice Golem has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. After a 1-second delay, the Hero Ice Golem activates his "Blizzard" ability, which generates a 4-tile aura radius that has 3 pulses, with each one slowing down enemy troops and dealing damage. The Snowstorm ability costs 2  to activate.

Strategy
Use your Ice Golem to distract enemy troops then pop the ability to freeze any defensive cards once it's at the tower.
All 3 blast are enough to kill Goblins.
Use the ability on defense, to stall any push.

History

2026

Hero Ice Golem was added to the game on 5/1/2026 with the January 2026 Update, along with Hero Wizard.
On 2/3/2026, a Balance Update, decreased Freeze duration to 1.5sec (from 2sec).
On 7/4/2026, a Balance Update, the pushback on the Hero Ice Golem was removed entirely.
On 4/8/2026, a Balance Update, the 3rd Blast Effect went from being a freeze effect to a slowdown effect, and the blast damage was increased by 82%. This also made the ability single use (from 17 sec cooldown).

Trivia
The word "Golem" occurs once in the Bible in Psalm 139:16, which uses the word "golmi", that means "my light form", "raw" material, connoting the unfinished human being. A golem is an animated, anthropomorphic being in Jewish folklore, which is entirely created from inanimate matter, usually clay or mud. The most famous golem narrative involves Judah Loew ben Bezalel, the late 16th-century rabbi of Prague.
The Ice Golem is 1 of 17 troops that inspired a troop in Clash of Clans, the others being Spear Goblins, Bomber, Bats, Skeleton Barrel, Minion Horde, Firecracker, Battle Ram, Zappies, Baby Dragon, Giant Skeleton, Ram Rider, Inferno Dragon, Miner, Royal Ghost, Ice Wizard, and Night Witch.
The Ice Golem is 1 of 7 cards associated with ice, with the others being Ice Spirit, Giant Snowball, Freeze, Ice Wizard, Super Ice Golem, and Wizard Trio.
In the Finnish language setting of the game, the Ice Golem's name translates to "Freezing Golem".
