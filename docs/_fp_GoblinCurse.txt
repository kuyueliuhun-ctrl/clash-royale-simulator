{"parse":{"title":"Goblin Curse","pageid":277638,"wikitext":"{{DISPLAYTITLE: <span style=\"color: #b300b3;\">Goblin Curse</span>}}\n{{MagicWords}}{{Protection}}\n\n{{Card Infobox|Cost=2|Rarity=Epic|Type=Spell|Arena=Serenity Peak|ReleaseDate=17 June 2024}}\n{{Quote|One does not simply reverse a curse! It is an area-damage, air-targeting [[:Category:Spell Cards|spell]] with a medium radius and very low damage that is dealt every second for 6 seconds. If those troops are defeated, they would convert into [[Goblins]]. It is a lime-colored liquid encased in a large vial, which is secured by a thin rope, with a large, rectangular wooden cap with three golden hairs, a circular mouth, two large ears, and two green, gem-shaped eyes on the front, resembling a [[Baby Goblins|Baby Goblin]]. A Goblin Curse card costs 2 [[Elixir]] to deploy.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Goblin Curse Card}}\n*The Goblin Curse has extremely high potential on both offense and defense for its [[Elixir]] cost. This is especially useful in [[Battle Decks|decks]] where the user will have a large push consisting of many high damage cards.\n*Be extremely careful when placing swarm units, like [[Bats]], [[Spear Goblins]], or [[Skeleton Army]], behind your [[:Category:Crown Towers|Crown Tower]] as the Goblin Curse will turn them into [[Goblins]] given enough time or with the assistance of a small spell. These Goblins will then attack your Crown Tower, dealing heavy damage or even destroying a Crown Tower if not answered properly.\n**In scenarios where the user’s swarm units are turned into Goblins, using spells like [[The Log]] and [[Barbarian Barrel]] are quick and very effective temporary solutions to them as they’ll do more than enough damage to OHKO Goblins. More permanent solutions involve cards with high area damage like [[Executioner]], Bowler, [[Wizard]], [[Sparky]], [[Firecracker]], and [[Bomber/Evolution|Evolved Bomber]] as they’ll stay in the [[Arenas|Arena]] after finishing them off.\n*The Goblin Curse's damage stacks with another Goblin Curse, but the curse effect does not stack. However, the curse effect does stack with the [[Mother Witch]]'s curse, allowing them to spawn both a Goblin and a Cursed Hog out of the same troop.\n*The Goblin Curse is easily one of the best counters for Graveyard, being able to almost always fully counter it with the help of the [[Tower Princess]], [[Dagger Duchess]] or [[Royal Chef]] for a +3 positive Elixir trade. Also, the Skeletons spawned from the Graveyard will turn into Goblins, creating a threatening counterattack under certain circumstances.\n*The Goblin Curse has incredible synergy with the [[Void]]. The damage of the Void is high enough that makes it more likely for singlular units to be destroyed by either spel. However, this synergy is completely ineffective against buildings, especially defensive buildings, as they unfortunately can’t be turned in Goblins like troops can.\n*The [[Goblin Giant]] is a great offensive combo in combination with the Goblin Curse due to the 2 Spear Goblins in the backpack. If light swarms, such as [[Goblin Gang]] or [[Skeleton Army]] are used to deal with the Goblin Giant, the Spear Goblins and Goblin Curse will do enough damage to turn these fragile troops into Goblins, adding to the push and increasing offensive pressure.\n*Due to the Goblin Curse’s very low damage over its duration, it’s advised to nearly always avoid using it for spell cycling onto the Crown Towers. Doing so, in most cases, will essentially result in a waste of 2 Elixir and ruin its potential to get more value against the opponent’s units.\n*The [[Tornado]] can be a valuable synergy with the Goblin Curse when used in large offensive pushes. The Tornado will keep opposing troops within the Goblin Curse’s radius for slightly longer, raising the chances of spawning Goblins out of it. However, this is a somewhat expensive combination, so it should ideally be used in Overtime, where Elixir is much more prevalent.\n*Under the right circumstances and with the help of the Tower Princess, the Goblin Curse can fully counter a [[Witch]] for a +3 positive Elixir trade. Make sure the Tower Princess is attacking the Skeletons first, turning them into Goblins and overwhelming the Witch before she can get any damage onto the Crown Tower.\n*The Goblin Curse can synergize well with the [[Rage]] when used against swarms. Playing the Goblin Curse first and then using Rage will turn all low health troop within the area of effect of both spells into Goblins, which are immediately boosted by the Rage spell and can quickly overwhelm the enemy Crown Tower if uninterrupted.\n\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\">\n{{Statistics}}{{StatusEffects}}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Goblin Curse Attributes}}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width: 100%; text-align: center;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |Cost<br />{{Icon|I=Elixir}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Radius<br />{{Icon|I=Radius}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Duration<br />{{Icon|I=Time}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Target<br />{{Icon|I=Targets}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Type<br />{{Icon|I=Type}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Rarity<br />{{Icon|I=Rarity}}\n|-\n|2||3||6 sec||Air & Ground||[[:Category:Spell Cards|Spell]]||{{Rarity|Epic}}\n|}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width:100%; text-align:center;\"\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Goblin Attributes}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |First Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Range<br />{{Icon|I=Range}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Target<br />{{Icon|I=Targets}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Transport<br />{{Icon|I=Transport}}\n|-\n|1.1 sec||0.4 sec||Very Fast (120)||Melee: Short (0.5)||Ground||Ground\n|}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Card Statistics}}\n<!--\nthis will not be shown\n================================================================\nATTENTION EDITORS: DO NOT MANUALLY UPDATE THE TABLE CELLS!
