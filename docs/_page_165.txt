Don't call her Princess.}}\n\nThe Dagger Duchess is a {{Rarity|Legendary}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from [[Arenas|Electro Valley (Arena 11)]]. It is a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|tower troop]] with moderate hitpoints and low damage. She attacks up to 8 times with a very fast hit speed by throwing daggers, until she depletes all of them, after which she reloads one dagger at a time with a slower reload speed. She resembles a woman who has cyan eyes, blonde eyebrows, a small mole on her left cheek, and blonde hair in a bun, wields daggers, wears a team-colored headband, a team-colored dress trimmed with white, white leggings, team-colored shoes, and a gilded breastplate, and carries a dagger case.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Dagger Duchess Card}}\n*The Dagger Duchess is most effective against small swarms, small pushes, and frailer [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]] due to her extremely fast initial attack speed and damage.\n*The Dagger Duchess is best utilized in [[Battle Decks|decks]] that struggle with other decks using a fast cycle, such as large-tank decks like [[Golem]] and [[Elixir Golem]]. Since fast paced decks rely on small pushes, the Dagger Duchess would be less likely to run out of daggers, thus increasing her effectiveness at destroying them before any major damage is done.\n*Unlike the [[Cannoneer]], the Dagger Duchess cannot fully counter [[Wall Breakers]] as her low damage allows one of to connect to her tower for a good amount of damage.\n**If the [[Wall Breakers/Evolution|Evolved Wall Breakers]] are used and ignored, the Dagger Duchess's DPS will allow one Wall Breaker and one Runner to connect.\n*She will have a hard time dealing with [[Lava Hound]] decks, as not only it will keep making her go into dagger cooldown, but also the Lava Pups take three daggers to be killed.\n*Against a [[Furnace]], she will easily deal with the [[Fire Spirit|Fire Spirits]] as they will be destroyed in just 3 daggers, causing nothing to connect to the Dagger Duchess. She will also replenish her daggers in between the waves, ensuring she never runs out. \n**Similar to the Furnace, the [[Goblin Hut]] is also weak to the Dagger Duchess as she will quickly destroy Spear Goblins with 2 daggers, allowing few connections per wave.\n*Frailer support cards, such as [[Little Prince]], Magic Archer, [[Goblinstein]], [[Ice Wizard]], Fisherman, [[Firecracker/Evolution|Evolved Firecracker]], [[Witch]], [[Electro Wizard]], [[Berserker]], Evolved Bomber, [[Electro Dragon]], [[Mega Minion]], [[Hunter]], [[Dart Goblin/Evolution|Evolved Dart Goblin]], [[Musketeer]], [[Wizard]], Princess, [[Flying Machine]], [[Skeleton Dragons]], and [[Archers/Evolution|Evolved Archers]], are very weak to her as they will take around 8 daggers to be destroyed, causing them to get little to no damage on the Dagger Duchess when left alone.\n*The [[Knight/Evolution|Evolved Knight]] is extremely effective against the Dagger Duchess as his damage reduction while moving allows him to easily nullify her attacks, forcing her into the dagger cooldown without dealing much damage to him. However, he can still be countered with nearly any tanky troop or building to disable his shield.\n*The Dagger Duchess is very weak against troops with high hitpoints, like the [[Giant]], [[Golem]], [[Lava Hound]], [[Giant Skeleton]], [[Mega Knight]], [[Goblin Giant]], [[Electro Giant]], and [[P.E.K.K.A.|P.E.K.K.A]], and very large pushes, due to her charging dagger mechanic slowing down her DPS once all 8 initial daggers have been used. \n*A full burst of daggers from the Dagger Duchess is enough to destroy any unit with as many hitpoints as a [[Fisherman]] before she needs to reload them again. Keep this in mind for when to drop any defending units to defeat any incoming enemy troop and for when to let her soak up the damage herself.\n*Unlike other tower troops, it is not recommended to utilize scouting strategies against the Dagger Duchess because the Dagger Duchess will be able to nullify the attempt by defeating or heavily crippling the troop used to scout the opponent's deck. An exception can be applied to scouting cards that can outrange her, like the [[Princess]], or cards with high hitpoints, like the [[Prince]], [[Mini P.E.K.K.A]], [[Dark Prince]], Hog Rider, [[Elite Barbarians]], Ram Rider, [[Baby Dragon]], or the [[Lumberjack]].\n*With the assistance of properly placed [[Goblins]] or [[Guards]], the Dagger Duchess can fully counter a [[Hog Rider]] and [[Ram Rider]] for a +2 and +3 positive [[Elixir]] trade respectively.\n*By herself, the Dagger Duchess can cripple the [[Battle Ram]] and [[Battle Ram/Evolution|Evolved Battle Ram]], and with the help of almost any card, the Ram can be taken out. However, this will use up all her daggers and put her into dagger cooldown, allowing the [[Barbarians]] to deal significant damage if ignored.\n*The [[Bandit]] is very effective against the Dagger Duchess, as she will become invisible and immune to the damage during her dash, allowing her to soak up a lot of daggers, forcing her to attack the Bandit during her cooldown. When facing a Bandit, make sure that distractions are placed close enough that she cannot dash to completely absorb any dagger.\n*Kiting opposing troops into the other lane with any card, such as an [[Ice Golem]], Ram Rider, or [[Goblin Giant]], is especially powerful with the Dagger Duchess as the other Crown Tower will usually have full daggers, destroying or severely damaging anything that was kited much faster as both of them will be attacking the kited troops. This can also buy the Crown Tower in the initial lane enough time to reload daggers for any incoming future push.\n*The Dagger Duchess has lower hitpoints compared to the [[Tower Princess]]. This makes her weaker to strategies that can outrange her.\n**The [[X-Bow]] and [[Mortar]] both can outrange her and because of her lower hitpoints, they will need less attacks in order to fully destroy her. This may require placing the [[cards]] fewer times, saving more [[Elixir]] for later within the [[Basics of Battle|battle]].\n**While a [[Magic Archer]] cannot outrange her initially, if he is lined up with an incoming troop, he can deal notable damage to the Dagger Duchess while also slowly chipping down the troop. A similar strategy works with the [[Firecracker]], [[Bowler]], and [[Bomber/Evolution|Evolved Bomber]].\n*She is quite effective against dual lane pushes. Due to them being weaker by having the troops split into both lanes, she will have an easier time dealing with them than if they were all in the same lane. This can allow for the player to ignore the attacks completely or respond to them with cheaper cards than they otherwise would use if they were not split. However, this is less effective if one Crown Tower has been already destroyed.\n*Against the [[Goblin Barrel/Evolution|Evolved Goblin Barrel]], she will only be hit once by the Decoy Goblins, which allows the player to largely ignore them and focus most of their Elixir into countering the side with the regular Goblins. If that side is ignored as well, she will only be hit thrice.\n*The quick unload of her daggers makes her great at defending passive buildings from incoming [[Miner|Miners]] and [[Goblin Barrel|Goblin Barrels]]. These cards will have dealt little damage to the passive buildings, allowing them to gain more value from the deployment, whether that is waves of troops spawned, or in the [[Elixir Collector]]'s case, Elixir. A similar principle can be applied to protecting frail ranged cards from the same attacking cards.\n*Aggressively deploying tanks or mini-tanks at the bridge can stop enemy tanks from crossing the river and expending the Dagger Duchess's ammunition, saving them for a higher-priority target like an incoming Hog Rider. Even if the tank is destroyed, it will have bought enough time to deal with the opponent's main offensive units first.\n*Be especially careful during Double Elixir and Overtime. The higher Elixir generation will allow your opponent to aggressively re-rush the same lane over and over again, preventing Dagger Duchess from being able to reload her daggers. In this case, it's highly recommended to bring strong defensive cards during Overtime and avoid taking too much Crown Tower damage.\n*When using the [[Monk]]'s ability against the Dagger Duchess, make sure that it is timed so that they are not reflected during her cooldown time. This will do more damage than if the ability was used during her cooldown time.\n\n<center>\n\n<div class=\"table-back\">{{Statistics}}\n{{StatusEffects}}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Dagger Duchess
