If you get too close, she's not afraid of pitching in with her mean-looking battle staff.\u201d</b></i></div>\n<p>The Night Witch is a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Legendary_Cards\" title=\"Category:Legendary Cards\"><span class=\"text-legendary\" style=\"font-weight: bold;\">Legendary</span></a> <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> that is unlocked from the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Serenity Peak (Arena 14)</a>. It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troop</a> with moderate hitpoints and high damage. Every 5 seconds, the Night Witch will passively summon a group of two <a href=\"/wiki/Bats\" title=\"Bats\">Bats</a> surrounding her. However, her first wave of Bats will spawn 1 second after she is deployed. When defeated, she will also spawn a single Bat. She appears similar to the <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a>, but has glowing blue eyes, wears a black cloak, top and gloves with silver armlets, a silver belt with an axe-shaped buckle and a glowing blue crystal in the center, and wields a long, darker axe-shaped staff with a glowing blue crystal on the top, which appears similar to her belt. class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a title=\"Sign in to edit\" class=\"mw-editsection-visualeditor\" data-tracking-label=\"log-in-edit-section\" data-action=\"edit-section\" href=\"https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fclashroyale.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FNight_Witch%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en&amp;metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section\" data-testid=\"log-in-edit-section\" aria-label=\"Sign in to edit\"><svg class=\"wds-icon wds-icon-tiny\" aria-hidden=\"true\" focusable=\"false\"><use xlink:href=\"#wds-icons-pencil-tiny\"></use></svg></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Night_Witch_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Night Witch Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>The Night Witch's high damage per attack in addition to her continuous spawns make her potent at defeating high-hitpoint units, whether it be on offense or defense.</li>\n<li>Her damage is enough to be able to OHKO any <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troop</a> with as many hitpoints as <a href=\"/wiki/Archers\" title=\"Archers\">Archers</a> or a <a href=\"/wiki/Firecracker\" title=\"Firecracker\">Firecracker</a>. Keep this in mind when deploying her on top on single frail troops to preserve as many of her hitpoints as possible.</li>\n<li>She cannot perfectly counter some troops that the <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a> can, as the Night Witch cannot distract ground-targeting troops with her <a href=\"/wiki/Bats\" title=\"Bats\">Bats</a>. However, the Night Witch can defeat nearly all <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">cards</a> that cannot target air, like <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Lumberjack\" title=\"Lumberjack\">Lumberjack</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/P.E.K.K.A\" class=\"mw-redirect\" title=\"P.E.K.K.A\">P.E.K.K.A</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarians\" title=\"Barbarians\">Barbarians</a>, etc.\n<ul><li>The Night Witch's role in a <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">deck</a> is fairly different compared to&#160;her counterpart, the Witch. The Night Witch does not do splash damage, only spawns two Bats at a time, is very fragile without the help of a tank&#160;(unlike how the Witch could protect herself using her spawned Skeletons), and cannot target air troops. On the other hand, the Night Witch's higher melee damage, increased health and her faster Bats make her more threatening on a counterpush.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Night Witch cannot attack air troops by herself, so <a href=\"/wiki/Minions\" title=\"Minions\">Minions</a>, Bats, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Dragon\" title=\"Electro Dragon\">Electro Dragon</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Minion\" title=\"Mega Minion\">Mega Minion</a>, Flying Machine, <a href=\"/wiki/Phoenix\" title=\"Phoenix\">Phoenix</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Dragons\" title=\"Skeleton Dragons\">Skeleton Dragons</a>, or the <a href=\"/wiki/Baby_Dragon\" title=\"Baby Dragon\">Baby Dragon</a> will be effective at taking her out since they can also easily take out her summoned Bats. However, if these units are distracted with something else, the Bats may heavily damage or even defeat them.</li>\n<li>The Night Witch is very dangerous to the <a href=\"/wiki/Cannoneer\" title=\"Cannoneer\">Cannoneer</a> as she will distract him with her Bats for a long time, although unlike the Witch, she will not distract him indefinitely, due to spawning fewer Bats.</li>\n<li>Upon deployment, the Night Witch's first wave of Bats spawns more quickly that all subsequent spawns, appearing 1 second after she is deployed. When she is placed directly behind the <a href=\"/wiki/King%27s_Tower\" title=\"King&#39;s Tower\">King's Tower</a>, 1 Bat will fly into the other lane, which can be useful if the player wants to lightly protect the other lane.</li>\n<li>The Night Witch pairs well with most large building-targeting tanks, because they can tank for the Bats spawned by her and deal massive damage to <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Crown_Towers\" title=\"Category:Crown Towers\">Towers</a>, while the Bats deal with threats to the tank, either with attacks or simply by distracting cards like the <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Tower\" title=\"Inferno Tower\">Inferno Tower</a>.\n<ul><li>The Giant is a cheap high-hitpoints tank that works well with the Night Witch, allowing him to be played either to setup a push in the back or by placing them both at the bridge for a rush attack.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golem</a> is similar to the Giant, except that he comes with higher hitpoints and damage, as well as two Golemites that will start tanking for the Night Witch once the Golem is defeated. If the Night Witch is placed behind the King's Tower, when she reach the Crown Tower, the Golem can be placed right in front. The Night Witch will be blocked, staying there until the Golem starts moving. This can let the player generate more Elixir to support this push afterwards.</li>\n<li>She also pairs well with the <a href=\"/wiki/Lava_Hound\" title=\"Lava Hound\">Lava Hound</a>. As an air troop, the Bats will assist the Lava Hound with distracting counters to it, while the Night Witch continues her way to her target. If defeated, the Night Witch will tank for the Lava Pups while also spawning more Bats to increase the swarm count and overwhelm the opponent.\n<ul><li>Conversely, she is an effective counter to a Lava Hound. By placing her in the back while the Lava Hound proceeds towards the Tower, she will spawn enough Bats to have them distracted by the Lava Hound, and defeat him. Note that the Lava Pups can OHKO the Bats so prepare with an additional cheap swarm to finish them off.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Giant\" title=\"Goblin Giant\">Goblin Giant</a> is slightly faster than the other tanks, which in this case, allows the Bats to stay closer to him and assist him against counters, while also preventing the Night Witch from being too close and provide splash value.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Golem\" title=\"Elixir Golem\">Elixir Golem</a> is the cheapest tank to use alongside the Night Witch. Like the Golem, he will also spawn smaller forms that will tank further for the Night Witch, with her Bats distracting and dealing more damage.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Night Witch can be a situational support troop for fast attackers like the <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Lumberjack\" title=\"Lumberjack\">Lumberjack</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Berserker\" title=\"Berserker\">Berserker</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Ram_Rider\" title=\"Ram Rider\">Ram Rider</a>. Her spawned Bats can catch up quickly with the fast attacker, while also punishing attempts at using a <a href=\"/wiki/Fisherman\" title=\"Fisherman\">Fisherman</a> to activate the King Tower with them, while the Night Witch stays away from them to avoid spell value.</li>\n<li>When the Night Witch is <a href=\"/wiki/Clone\" title=\"Clone\">cloned</a>, her clone will also spawn cloned Bats. If the opponent lacks a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spell</a> or proper area damage unit, this will often overwhelm them and destroy their Crown Tower in seconds.</li>\n<li>Ranged splash damage troops that can target air like the <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard\" title=\"Wizard\">Wizard</a> or Witch are very effective against the Night Witch since they can defeat her and her spawned Bats quickly without receiving much damage.\n<ul><li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Wizard\" title=\"Ice Wizard\">Ice Wizard</a> combines area-damage with a slow effect, forcing the Night Witch to take longer to spawn the next wave of Bats. While he deals lower damage than the previous options, it is still enough to OHKO the Bats, while he also costs 1 less <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> than the Night Witch, making him a cheap counter to her.</li>\n<li>Area denial units like the <a href=\"/wiki/Executioner\" title=\"Executioner\">Executioner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Archer\" title=\"Magic Archer\">Magic Archer</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Firecracker\" title=\"Firecracker\">Firecracker</a> will be able to immediately defeat the spawned Bats as they are spawned and continue to attack the Night Witch with little issue. In addition, when they land the fatal hit on the Night Witch, the singular Bat will be instantly defeated as well.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Dragon\" title=\"Electro Dragon\">Electro Dragon</a> may not be able to deal area damage but its chain attack is very effective at dealing damage to the Night Witch while also defeating her Bats. It will also stun her, delaying her from spawning additional Bats. The <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Spirit\" title=\"Electro Spirit\">Electro Spirit</a> works in a similar way for only 1 Elixir, but remember that it will only do that once.</li>\n<li>Unlike the regular <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a>, the <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie/Evolution\" title=\"Valkyrie/Evolution\">Evolved Valkyrie</a> is an effective counter to the Night Witch. The Evolved Valkyrie will attack her with her regular attacks while also dealing the tornado damage. The combination of the regular attacks and the tornado is enough to 3HKO the Night Witch, while the tornado alone can OHKO any Bats that are spawned. However, the death spawn Bat will stay alive to continue attacking the Evolved Valkyrie, so try to destroy it with another card like an <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Wizard\" title=\"Electro Wizard\">Electro Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Dart_Goblin\" title=\"Dart Goblin\">Dart Goblin</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a> to ensure that the Evolved Valkyrie can remain for a counterattack.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Because of her melee range and medium movement speed, she can be countered by pulling her to the center with a ranged card that can target air, even single-target ones like the <a href=\"/wiki/Archers\" title=\"Archers\">Archers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mother_Witch\" title=\"Mother Witch\">Mother Witch</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Archer_Queen\" title=\"Archer Queen\">Archer Queen</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Wizard\" title=\"Electro Wizard\">Electro Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Dart_Goblin\" title=\"Dart Goblin\">Dart Goblin</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Zappies\" title=\"Zappies\">Zappies</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Little_Prince\" title=\"Little Prince\">Little Prince</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Spear_Goblins\" title=\"Spear Goblins\">Spear Goblins</a>. However, this should not be done if she is supported with a tank, as they will target the tank in this case, which will allow the Night Witch to spawn more Bats and overwhelm them.</li>\n<li><a href=\"/wiki/Poison\" title=\"Poison\">Poison</a> is the most effective spell against the Night Witch. If timed correctly, not only will the Poison cripple the Night Witch, it will also eliminate all the spawned Bats. However, it is not enough to destroy her by itself, so it may have to be combined with a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:2-Elixir_Cards\" title=\"Category:2-Elixir Cards\">2-Elixir</a> spell like <a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Snowball\" title=\"Giant Snowball\">Giant Snowball</a> to fully defeat her.\n<ul><li><a href=\"/wiki/Fireball\" title=\"Fireball\">Fireball</a> deals less damage than Poison, so a combination of the spell with Zap or <a href=\"/wiki/Rage\" title=\"Rage\">Rage</a> is not enough to defeat her. In this case, a <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarian_Barrel\" title=\"Barbarian Barrel\">Barbarian Barrel</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/The_Log\" title=\"The Log\">The Log</a> would be necessary for the job, which is less effective due to its inability to hit the Bats.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Just like the regular Witch, if the Night Witch summons Bats while under&#160;<a href=\"/wiki/Rage\" title=\"Rage\">Rage</a>, her Bats are also affected. Not only that, but Rage will also allow the Night Witch to spawn more Bats faster.</li>\n<li>The Night Witch can be used to effectively counter the <a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a> since her Bats can quickly defeat the Skeletons. However, a spell like Poison can easily defeat her and allow the Skeletons to deal massive damage to the Tower.\n<ul><li>Conversely, she is also a great synergy with Graveyard. The Night Witch will tank for the Graveyard, while the <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a> and Bats deal massive damage while staying relatively spread out.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Night Witch pairs well with the <a href=\"/wiki/Miner\" title=\"Miner\">Miner</a>, as he can tank for her and her Bats, allowing her to spawn them more safely and cause a considerable damage to a Crown Tower, being more useful if the opponent has low Elixir. The combo, however, is still weak to swarms or spells, due to the fact that since Night Witch targets the Tower, she will ignore every enemy surrounding her.\n<ul><li>Alternatively, in larger pushes, a Miner can distract ranged area attackers that can threaten the Night Witch, allowing her to stay alive and spawn more Bats to assist the push.</li></ul></li>\n<li>A Night Witch, even with the assistance of a Crown Tower, cannot prevent a Balloon from connecting to the Tower. Her first wave of Bats don't spawn and attack quickly enough to prevent the Balloon's first attack, even when she is placed slightly in advance.</li>\n<li>Against a <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Barrel\" title=\"Goblin Barrel\">Goblin Barrel</a>, place her behind the bottom <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblin</a>. The Night Witch will attack it while her first wave of Bats will go for the other two Goblins. This will prevent the most damage from the Goblin Barrel.</li>\n<li>A Night Witch can be countered by kiting her into the other lane with an <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Golem\" title=\"Ice Golem\">Ice Golem</a>. Since the Night Witch and her spawned Bats are melee, they will follow the Ice Golem completely, while the Tower attacks them. If necessary, the Ice Golem's death damage will wipe out some Bats and slow down the Night Witch further, buying more time for the other <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Tower_Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Tower Troop Cards\">tower troop</a> to finish her off.</li>\n<li>The Night Witch can fully stop a <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a> with the help of the <a href=\"/wiki/Tower_Princess\" title=\"Tower Princess\">Tower Princess</a>. Place her in such a way that the Mega Knight does not jump on her. She spawns Bats fast enough that will keep up with the Mega Knight, as well as a singular one after death, which is enough to defeat him before he lands on the Tower Princess. After that, the player can make use of those Bats for a small counterattack.</li>\n<li>While a <a href=\"/wiki/Mother_Witch\" title=\"Mother Witch\">Mother Witch</a> does not deal area damage, she will immediately defeat each Bat in one hit, spawning Cursed Hogs from them. These Cursed Hogs will distract the Night Witch and any remaining Bats for a long time, allowing the Mother Witch to curse the Night Witch, eventually defeating her and spawning a Cursed Hog out of her.</li>\n<li>A Night Witch is actually able to slightly outrange some troops because of her staff, similar to the <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Guards\" title=\"Guards\">Guards</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Recruits\" title=\"Royal Recruits\">Royal Recruits</a>. Because of this, she can poke at a distracted <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a> without taking any damage, setting you up for a counterpush.</li>\n<li>The Night Witch is very effective at baiting spells from the opponent. She can either bait weaker spells like <a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Snowball\" title=\"Giant Snowball\">Giant Snowball</a> that are used against her Bats or she can bait stronger spells like <a href=\"/wiki/Lightning\" title=\"Lightning\">Lightning</a> and Poison that are used to directly target her. This allows the user to deploy other cards that are weak to those spells such as <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Barrel\" title=\"Skeleton Barrel\">Skeleton Barrel</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Flying_Machine\" title=\"Flying Machine\">Flying Machine</a> respectively.</li>\n<li>Like any spawner card, she pairs well with the <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_King\" title=\"Skeleton King\">Skeleton King</a>. The Bats that she spawns will be able to accumulate his ability, allowing him to spawn more Skeletons from it, while they also attack opposing air troops that can threaten the duo.</li>\n<li>The Night Witch is very effective in <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Modes\" title=\"Category:Modes\">game modes</a> where there are no Crown Towers to attack the Bats, such as <a href=\"/wiki/Touchdown\" title=\"Touchdown\">Touchdown</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Heist\" title=\"Heist\">Heist</a>. The lack of Crown Towers means that each Bat will have to addressed individually, and since they are air troops, it will necessitate the use of cards that can target them, which can allow other allied href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Balance_Changes_(11/8/2017)\" title=\"Version History\">Balance Update</a>, decreased the Night Witch's damage by 9%, decreased her range to 1.65 tiles (from 1.85 tiles), Bats' attack time interval to 1.1 seconds (from 1 second), and increased their first attack time interval to 0.6 seconds (from 0.5 seconds).</li>\n<li>On 20/6/2018, the <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Summer_2018_Update_(20/6/2018)\" title=\"Version History\">Summer 2018 Update</a>, moved the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Arena</a> to unlock the Night Witch <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Legendary_Cards\" title=\"Category:Legendary Cards\"><span class=\"text-legendary\" style=\"font-weight: bold;\">Legendary</span></a> cards start at Level 9 and end at Level href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Balance_Changes_(9/12/2020)\" title=\"Version History\">Balance Update</a>, decreased the Bats' first attack time interval to 0.6 seconds (from 0.8 the Night Witch from Spell Valley to Serenity Peak.</li>\n<li>On 6/9/2021, the <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#2021_Quarter_3_Update_(6/9/2021)\" title=\"Version History\">2021 Quarter 3 Update</a>, decreased the first spawn time interval to 1 second (from 3.5 seconds), decreased her spawn time interval to 5 seconds (from 7 seconds), and removed the Night Witch's death spawn.</li>\n<li>On 27/10/2021, the <a href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Champions_Update_(27/10/2021)\" title=\"Version History\">Champions Update</a>, increased the Level cap of all cards to 14 (from 13).
