class=\"quote-block\"><i><b>\u201cThree unarmored ranged attackers. Who the heck taught these guys to throw spears!? Who thought that was a good idea?!\u201d</b></i></div>\n<p>The Spear Goblins is a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Common_Cards\" title=\"Category:Common Cards\"><span style=\"color: #9ECAFF; font-weight: bold;\">Common</span></a> <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> that is unlocked from the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Goblin Stadium (Arena 1)</a>. It spawns three very fast, single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> with low hitpoints and low damage. Their deployment is in a triangle formation. They resemble normal <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a>, but are taller and thinner, have sharper elven ears and side hair, wear team-colored bandanas and belts, have team-colored spear cases tied to their backs with a rope, and always have a wooden spear in their hands with a team-colored rope tied on top. They are also the same as those spawned by the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Hut\" title=\"Goblin Hut\">Goblin Hut</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Gang\" title=\"Goblin Gang\">Goblin Gang</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Giant\" title=\"Goblin Giant\">Goblin Giant</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Party_Hut\" title=\"Party Hut\">Party Hut</a>. A Spear Goblins card costs 2 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> to deploy.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Strategy\">Strategy</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Spear_Goblins_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Spear Goblins Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>Spear Goblins provides anti-air and ground distraction for a cheap cost, but they will be defeated by any damaging <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spell</a> beside the <a href=\"/wiki/Freeze\" title=\"Freeze\">Freeze</a> spell. Being overly reliant on them to defend or distract pushes can backfire if the opponent predicts the move and spells them immediately. The player should be aware of when the opponent can or can't quickly defeat Spear Goblins on their side of the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Arena</a>.</li>\n<li>Mini tanks pair well with Spear Goblins, they can help in distracting the enemy units and absorbing hits from them while the Spear Goblins deal damage from distance. Additionally, they can also assist in protecting them from distractible splash <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Dragons\" title=\"Skeleton Dragons\">Skeleton Dragons</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard\" title=\"Wizard\">Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Wizard\" title=\"Ice Wizard\">Ice Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Archer\" title=\"Magic Archer\">Magic Archer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Firecracker\" title=\"Firecracker\">Firecracker</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Princess\" title=\"Princess\">Princess</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Heal_Spirit\" title=\"Heal Spirit\">Heal Spirit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Spirit\" title=\"Ice Spirit\">Ice Spirit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Fire_Spirit\" title=\"Fire Spirit\">Fire Spirit</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber\" title=\"Bomber\">Bomber</a>, or even against bulkier splash troops such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Baby_Dragon\" title=\"Baby Dragon\">Baby Dragon</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Executioner\" title=\"Executioner\">Executioner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Dark_Prince\" title=\"Dark Prince\">Dark Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_King\" title=\"Skeleton King\">Skeleton King</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bowler\" title=\"Bowler\">Bowler</a>, Valkyrie, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Demolisher\" title=\"Goblin Demolisher\">Goblin Demolisher</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Ghost\" title=\"Royal Ghost\">Royal Ghost</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a>.</li>\n<li>Compared to other cheap swarms like <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bats\" title=\"Bats\">Bats</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Minions\" title=\"Minions\">Minions</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Archers\" title=\"Archers\">Archers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Guards\" title=\"Guards\">Guards</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a>, Spear Goblins can stall for a longer period of time, as their range allows them to stay in place instead of approaching the unit intended for distraction.</li>\n<li>Due to their low <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> cost, Spear Goblins are easily disposable and can be used to block an enemy <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>'s charge. In fact, Spear Goblins, with the help of the two <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Tower_Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Tower Troop Cards\">Crown Towers,</a> can take out a Prince. The player should place the Spear Goblins in the center of their side. When the Prince attacks the Spear Goblins, the second Crown Tower will also attack the Prince, taking him out.</li>\n<li>Spear Goblins are decent support behind higher hitpoint troops such as <a href=\"/wiki/Giant\" title=\"Giant\">Giant</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Lava_Hound\" title=\"Lava Hound\">Lava Hound</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Skeleton\" title=\"Giant Skeleton\">Giant Skeleton</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Giant\" title=\"Royal Giant\">Royal Giant</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"P.E.K.K.A.\">P.E.K.K.A</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Giant\" title=\"Electro Giant\">Electro Giant</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golem</a>. They can deal considerable damage to defending units and <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">buildings</a>, and are less susceptible to splash troops behind a tank due to their range.</li>\n<li>When playing Spear Goblins at the bridge for chip damage, the player can place them in the bypass placement for ground troop. This will result in the second Spear Goblin to throw an extra spear before dying to the Crown Tower. Do be aware that doing so gives the opponent more time to react. Even if the opponent reacts to them in time, it will still allow the player to scout the opponent's <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">deck</a> based on what <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> they used to counter the Spear Goblins.</li>\n<li>Spear Goblins combined with a Crown Tower's damage can prevent a <a href=\"/wiki/Balloon\" title=\"Balloon\">Balloon</a> from landing its powerful melee attack onto it, only letting the death damage slip by. This is not a bad trade, as the defender will be up 3 Elixir.</li>\n<li>Spear Goblins can prevent major damage from <a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a>, as one Spear Goblin can one shot a Skeleton. However, their slow hit speed may cause them to stutter and allow a few hits on the Crown Tower, and they are susceptible to spells like <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Snowball\" title=\"Giant Snowball\">Giant Snowball</a>, Zap, or <a href=\"/wiki/Poison\" title=\"Poison\">Poison</a> commonly paired with Graveyard.</li>\n<li><a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a>, Giant Snowball, <a href=\"/wiki/Rage\" title=\"Rage\">Rage</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarian_Barrel\" title=\"Barbarian Barrel\">Barbarian Barrel</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/The_Log\" title=\"The Log\">The Log</a> are all&#160;effective in eliminating Spear Goblins due to their equivalent cost. However, the player should be cautious of other cards the opponent may be running, as the opponent may abuse the lack of efficient counter with cards such as <a href=\"/wiki/Minion_Horde\" title=\"Minion Horde\">Minion Horde</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Barrel\" title=\"Goblin Barrel\">Goblin Barrel</a>.</li>\n<li>They can tank three hits from a <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a>. By placing them almost horizontal to her, their triangular formation will cause the Valkyrie to only be able to take out one Spear Goblin at a time, buying time for the player's tower to take her out.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Miner\" title=\"Miner\">Miner</a> pairs well with the Spear Goblins on a counterattack, as the Miner's health pool will allow the Spear Goblins to rack up major damage if left unattended. It can be awkward to counter if the opponent does not have a cheap spell in hand.</li>\n<li>The Spear Goblins paired with Goblins can replicate the effect of the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Gang\" title=\"Goblin Gang\">Goblin Gang</a> for a slightly more expensive cost. However, there is 1 more Goblin than there is in the Goblin Gang, and their lower individual costs allow the player to defend more efficiently to several attacks (i.e, they are not forced to overcommit with 4 Elixir to a single attack, like with the Goblin Gang's 3 Elixir cost).</li></ul>\n<center>\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\"><h2 style=\"display: block; text-align:
