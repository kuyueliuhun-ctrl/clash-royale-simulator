{"parse":{"title":"Goblin Cage","pageid":246694,"wikitext":{"*":"{{DISPLAYTITLE:<span style=\"color: #ffa31a;\">Goblin Cage</span>}}\n{{Protection}}{{MagicWords}}\n\n{{Card Infobox|Cost=4|Rarity=Rare|Type=Building|Arena=Goblin Stadium|ReleaseDate=17 May 2019}}{{SubpageNavBox|Evolution=yes}}{{Quote|When the Goblin Cage is destroyed, a Goblin Brawler is unleashed into the Arena! Goblin Brawler always skips leg day.}}\n\nThe Goblin Cage is a {{Rarity|Rare}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from the [[Arenas|Goblin Stadium (Arena 1)]]. It spawns a [[:Category:Building Cards|building]] with moderate hitpoints. When defeated, it will spawn a Goblin Brawler, which is a fast, single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] with high hitpoints and high damage. It appears to be a wooden cage with four metal supports, a lock on the front, and a pair of bars at each side, while the Goblin Brawler appears as a larger, buff [[Goblins|Goblin]] with a unibrow, white bands tied on his hands, and tattoos printed on his back and arms. He is also the same as that spawned by the [[Party Hut]]. A Goblin Cage card costs 4 [[Elixir]] to deploy.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Goblin Cage Card}}\n*While not immediately obvious, the Goblin Cage's hitbox is identical to a spawner [[:Category:Building Cards|building]], such as the [[Goblin Hut]], rather than a defensive building such as [[Cannon]]. This means it can pull building targeting units from a slightly farther distance than defensive buildings can. This also makes it safer to place in the middle as a passive play, as the Goblin Cage will deter most attacks while also being unrecommended to destroy it with [[:Category:Spell Cards|spells]].\n**The Goblin Cage is not a replacement for any spawner building, as their playstyles are completely different. Goblin Cage is supposed to be used to absorb hits at all times, serving the role of a distraction, while spawner buildings are better to avoid taking damage in most situations so that they can spawn more units.\n*If an enemy [[Hog Rider]] or [[Ram Rider]] is deployed, the Goblin Cage should be placed down in front of them to mitigate as much damage as possible. This placement is more effective than the general center placement as the Goblin Brawler will start attacking immediately and will not have to chase them.\n*If placed in the center of the player's side of the map, it can completely stop a [[Mega Knight]] with the help of both [[:Category:Crown Towers|Crown Towers]], allowing for a large positive [[Elixir]] trade.\n*Like a [[Mini P.E.K.K.A.|Mini P.E.K.K.A]], [[Royal Ghost]], [[Dark Prince]], Elite Barbarian, [[Valkyrie]], [[Lumberjack]], [[Mega Minion]], [[Phoenix]], [[Bandit]], Golden Knight, [[Cannon Cart]], [[Night Witch]], [[Monk]], and [[Prince]], the Goblin Brawler himself dishes out significant damage left alone but has only average hitpoints, so [[Skeletons]] placed in the center of the [[Arenas|Arena]] can buy the player's Crown Tower enough time eliminate him before he deals any damage to it. Similarly, spells that can slow him down can prevent him from connecting to the Crown Tower.\n*The Goblin Cage's hitpoints are enough to still survive 2 hits from heavy hitters like the [[Elite Barbarians]], as well as a single hit from a Mini P.E.K.K.A. However, If the player is doing this move as a last resort, they should place the Cage as far away as possible, but directly in front of them. This will give the player an extra 1.5 seconds to defend.\n*If placed 1 tile from the other side of the Arena, it can pull an entire [[Minion Horde]]. Once the Goblin Brawler spawns, the remaining [[Minions]] will be kited and the Goblin Brawler will get 2 hits on the foe's Crown Tower.\n*Against ranged [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]], the Goblin Cage should be placed on top on them. This will ensure that when the Goblin Cage is destroyed, the Goblin Brawler will start attacking them immediately, instead of having to walk towards them, which would cause him to take unnecessary damage. This tactic should also be used against the [[Royal Giant]], to prevent more damage on the Crown Tower.\n*The Goblin Cage is great at absorbing attacks from troops that destroy themselves with their attack.\n**The Goblin Cage will be able to absorb the [[Battle Ram]]'s attack, regardless on whether the Battle Ram is charging or not. After that, it will withstand a few extra hits from the spawned [[Barbarians]], and when it is destroyed, the Goblin Brawler will further continue with defending against the Barbarians. A similar tactic applies to the [[Skeleton Barrel]] and [[Suspicious Bush]].\n**For a slight negative Elixir trade, it can lure both [[Wall Breakers]] and take their attack directly, saving a more potent target from being hit by them. This also works against the [[Wall Breakers/Evolution|Evolved Wall Breakers]]. However, the Goblin Cage will be destroyed in the process, forcing the Goblin Brawler to be released prematurely.\n**Although it cannot directly attack it, the Goblin Cage can be a decent tank against the spawned [[Fire Spirit|Fire Spirits]] from a [[Furnace]], and it less of an issue for it to take damage, due to its complete passivity. However, the Goblin Cage has a shorter duration than the Furnace, which will force the Goblin Brawler to absorb any remaining Fire Spirits if he travels towards the same lane, removing any opportunity for a counterattack.\n**While a large negative Elixir trade, the stun effect from the [[Ice Spirit/Evolution|Evolved Ice Spirit]]'s Ice Blast has no effect on the Goblin Cage, making it a good response for it when used in larger pushes.\n**In a pinch, the Goblin Cage can take the [[Golden Knight]]'s Dashing Dash, saving a more potent target from it. After it is destroyed, the Goblin Brawler will further assist with defending against the Golden Knight, countering him for a +1 Elixir trade.\n*The Goblin Brawler is among the few units that can escape the [[Giant Skeleton]]'s death bomb. Deploy the Goblin Cage behind the Giant Skeleton and it will absorb a few attacks from the Giant Skeleton before spawning the Goblin Brawler to defeat him.\n*The Goblin Brawler is very weak to dual lane units such as [[Royal Recruits]], split [[Royal Hogs]], split [[Zappies]], and split [[Three Musketeers]] because he does not have high enough hitpoints to withstand such as push and he only targets 1 unit at a time.\n*Additionally, the Goblin Brawler is weak to swarms so pairing him with spells such as [[Zap]], [[The Log]], [[Arrows]], [[Fireball]], or [[Poison]] can synergize with him and allow him to advance towards the Crown Tower.\n*The Goblin Cage can absorb 2 of the 3 strikes from the [[Lightning]] spell, so it can be used to tank for Lightning-vulnerable [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]] like the [[Musketeer]], Zappies, [[Electro Wizard]], [[Little Prince]], [[Ice Wizard]], [[Wizard]], [[Magic Archer]], [[Goblinstein]], [[Flying Machine]], [[Princess]], [[Firecracker]], [[Bomber]], and [[Mother Witch]].\n*The [[Earthquake]] does enough damage to destroy a Goblin Cage by itself and release the Goblin Brawler.\n*The Goblin Cage is a good counter to the [[Electro Giant]]. Due to being unable to attack, the Electro Giant will have to break it with his regular attack, and after it breaks, the Goblin Brawler will cripple if not outright destroy the Electro Giant.\n*When used against the [[Mortar]] and [[X-Bow]], the player should place the Goblin Cage in the same lane as them, to ensure that the Goblin Brawler will defend against those buildings' defenders. This is more effective than placing it in the other lane, as the Goblin Brawler will not distract them and instead they will retarget to something else after they destroy the Goblin Cage.\n*If the player has already destroyed one Crown Tower, they can place the Goblin Cage on the opponent's side. This can be helpful in situations where an attacking troop is proceeding towards the other Crown Tower and the player wants their Goblin Cage to distract said Crown Tower.\n\n<center><div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\">{{Statistics}}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Goblin Cage Attributes}}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width:100%; text-align:center;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |Cost<br />{{Icon|I=Elixir}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Deploy Time<br />{{Icon|I=Deploy Time}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Lifetime<br />{{Icon|I=Time}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Type<br />{{Icon|I=Type}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Rarity<br />{{Icon|I=Rarity}}\n|-\n|4||1 sec||20 sec||[[:Category:Building Cards|Building]]||{{Rarity|Rare}}\n|}\n\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Goblin Brawler Attributes}}\n{{StatusEffects}}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table-secondary\" style=\"width:100%;text-align:center;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |First Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Range<br />{{Icon|I=Range}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Target<br />{{Icon|I=Targets}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Count<br />{{Icon|I=Troop Count}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Transport<br />{{Icon|I=Transport}}\n|-\n|1.1 sec||0.2 sec||Fast (90)||Melee: Short (0.8)||Ground||x1||Ground\n|}\n\n<!--\nthis will not be shown\n================================================================\nATTENTION EDITORS: DO NOT MANUALLY UPDATE THE TABLE CELLS!
