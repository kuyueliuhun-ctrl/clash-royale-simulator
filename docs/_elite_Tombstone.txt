Strategy
For the purposes of determining Elixir trades, the Heroic Tombstone and its activated Ability count as 8 Elixir, as the player must play the 3 Elixir Heroic Tombstone and then spend a further 5 on his Ability.
Do not place a Heroic Tombstone in a position where you would never place a regular Tombstone. This is because it can easily reveal to the opponent that the ability will be used, incentivizing them to destroy it before the ability can be used.
