{"parse":{"title":"Cannoneer","pageid":277339,"wikitext":{"*":"{{Protection}}{{MagicWords}}\n\n{{Card Infobox|Rarity=Epic|Type=Tower Troop|Arena=Frozen Peak|ReleaseDate=1 January 2024}}\n{{Quote|Slow but powerful. What could be better than a giant cannon on your Tower?}}\n\nThe Cannoneer is an {{Rarity|Epic}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from [[Arenas|Frozen Peak (Arena 8)]].\u00a0It is a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|tower troop]] with moderate hitpoints and high damage. The Cannoneer resembles a short and stout man wearing goggles, a team-colored cloak, brown gloves and sandals, has a loose tooth, carries a sponge rammer and a backpack with a crown emblem in the center filled with cannonballs, and wields a large cannon with team-colored crown paint and a control lever.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Cannoneer Card}}\n*The Cannoneer's massive single-target damage makes him very effective at taking out low to medium-health, low-count [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]], such as [[Archers]], Bomber, [[Wizard]], [[Musketeer]], [[Phoenix]], Firecracker, [[Berserker]], [[Archer Queen]], Ice Wizard, [[Mother Witch]], Dart Goblin, [[Little Prince]], Fisherman, [[Baby Dragon]], [[Hunter]], Goblinstein, [[Electro Dragon]], Magic Archer, [[Electro Wizard]], [[Skeleton Dragons]], [[Mega Minion]], and [[Flying Machine]].\n*The Cannoneer's damage is similar to a [[Bandit]]'s dash. Thus, he will defeat troops, such as [[Dart Goblin]] and Firecracker in one hit.\n*The Cannoneer's hit speed is very slow at 2.2 seconds, hindering his ability to deal with most swarms effectively, allowing for large swarms, such as [[Skeleton Army]], to destroy the Cannoneer if left alone. Smaller swarm cards like [[Bats]], [[Minions]], Archers, [[Spear Goblins]], [[Skeletons]], and [[Goblins]] can accumulate a large amount of chip damage if left ignored. It's recommended to use multiple [[cards]] with splash damage when using the Cannoneer in battle.\n*Scouting strategies work differently against the Cannoneer compared to the [[Tower Princess]]. Singular troops like the Dart Goblin and Spirit cards are ineffective, while cheap swarms like Goblins and Bats work significantly better.\n*The [[Electro Spirit]] has amazing synergy with [[Battle Decks|decks]] using the Cannoneer due to its ability to handle light swarms, especially Skeletons and Bats, very effectively for a cheap cost.\n*The Cannoneer\u2019s 7.5 tile range allows him to attack opposing troops kited into the other lane or troops attacking the [[King's Tower|King Tower]] in the opposite lane.\n*The Cannoneer has lower hitpoints compared to other [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|tower troops]]. This makes him weaker to strategies that can outrange him.\n**The [[X-Bow]] and [[Mortar]] both can outrange him and because of his lower hitpoints, they will need less attacks in order to fully destroy him. This may require placing the cards fewer times, saving more [[Elixir]] for later within the [[Basics of Battle|battle]].\n**While a [[Magic Archer]] cannot outrange him initially, if he is lined up with an incoming troop, he can deal notable damage to the Cannoneer while also slowly chipping down the troop. A similar strategy works with the Firecracker and [[Bomber/Evolution|Evolved Bomber]].\n*The Cannoneer is excellent at dealing with high-hitpoint units, such as [[Golem]], [[Giant]], [[Mega Knight]], [[P.E.K.K.A]], Goblin Giant, [[Giant Skeleton]], [[Royal Giant]], [[Lava Hound]], and [[Knight/Evolution|Evolved Knight]], due to his high damage.\n*The Cannoneer is especially powerful against [[Electro Giant]] as the high damage and slow hit speed causes the Cannoneer to get hit by the counterattack less frequently compared to other tower troops while dealing more damage per shot.\n*The Cannoneer has great synergy with cards that can pull enemies towards the center of the player's side. These cards allow one or potentially both of the Cannoneers to deal more damage to the incoming troops.\n**Ranged troops like the Archers and Spear Goblins can utilize their range from the center, while the Cannoneer attacks the troops. This works best against slow melee troops like the [[Valkyrie]], [[Mighty Miner]], and [[Battle Healer]]. Note that this is not as effective against a P.E.K.K.A or a [[Giant Skeleton]] due to their lower sight range.\n**The player can also utilize kiting strategies with troops like the [[Ice Golem]] and [[Goblin Giant]], to lure them towards the other lane, while the Cannoneer attacks them. This may even result in the kiting troop surviving, setting up for a counterattack.\n**Against building-targeting troops, defensive [[:Category:Building Cards|buildings]], such as a [[Cannon]] or [[Tesla]], are necessary. While the Cannoneer deals damage to them, the building lures them deep enough into the other lane for the other Cannoneer to attack.\n**The [[Tornado]] and [[Fisherman]]'s pulling mechanics can also bring them to the area where both Cannoneers can attack. The Fisherman will also further defend against them with his regular attacks. The Cannoneer's high damage will save more hitpoints on the Fisherman, allowing him to counterattack afterwards more safely.\n*A [[Tombstone]] placed anywhere on the opponent's side will always take out the Cannoneer if left alone. This is due to Cannoneer's slow firing rate, and the spawned Skeletons coming out of the Tombstone too quickly for the Cannoneer to keep up.\n**Conversely, a Tombstone placed optimally against opposing high hitpoint units can greatly increase the effectiveness of the Cannoneer, allowing both of them to target the tank as the Tombstone distracts and adds extra damage with the spawned [[Skeletons]]. A [[Goblin Hut]] or [[Barbarian Hut]] can work in a similar way.\n*On the other hand, due to spawning only one troop per wave, the [[Furnace]]'s spawned [[Fire Spirit|Fire Spirits]] will be instantly defeated by the Cannoneer, preventing all damage from it, even from a distance. As such, the player can focus in the other lane without having to worry about the Furnace.\n*The [[Skeletons/Evolution|Evolved Skeletons]] can easily take out the Cannoneer. This is due to his attack speed being so slow that Evolved Skeletons placed at the bridge will reach the Cannoneer, causing them to destroy him outright if ignored. This will also allow them to accumulate enough troops to destroy the [[King's Tower|King Tower]] afterwards as well.\n*The Cannoneer is fairly effective at taking out [[Balloon]], as ignoring a Balloon with the Cannoneer will only have the Balloon get 1 hit.\n*The Cannoneer is very weak to the [[Graveyard]]. The Graveyard will spawn the Skeletons fast enough that the Cannoneer will not be able to keep up with them. Due to this weakness, against a Graveyard + tank push, it is most recommended to ignore the tank, and let the Cannoneer handle it, while prioritizing other card counters to the Graveyard.\n*Against a [[Goblin Barrel]], the Cannoneer is able to OHKO the Goblins. Therefore, using spells that are unable to OHKO Goblins, such as Zap and Rage is a waste of Elixir, as it would not help the Cannoneer with reducing the damage dealt to him. Instead, it is better to use these spells only for their additional effects, or to use spells that can OHKO the Goblins, like the [[Barbarian Barrel]].\n*The [[Witch]] is very dangerous to the Cannoneer as she will distract him with her Skeletons for a long time. In fact, she can spawn Skeletons faster than it takes for the Cannoneer to destroy the previous wave of Skeletons, distracting him indefinitely. The [[Night Witch]] is slightly less effective due to spawning fewer Bats, but can threaten the Cannoneer similarly.\n*Because of his high damage but slow attack, he pairs well with cards that can stun or slow down enemies, like the [[Ice Wizard]], Electro Wizard, Electro Dragon, [[Goblinstein]], [[Ice Spirit]], Electro Spirit, and [[Zappies]]. The additional effects of these cards will buy more time for the Cannoneer to deal damage to enemy troops.\n\n<center>\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" Cannons were invented in China during the twelfth century. It was most likely developed in parallel or as an evolution of an earlier gunpowder weapon called the fire lance. Cannons were used for warfare by the late thirteenth century in the Yuan dynasty and spread throughout Eurasia in the fourteenth century. During the late Middle Ages, large and small cannons were developed for siege and field battles. The cannon replaced prior siege weapons such as the trebuchet.
