Carries a bomb that blows up when the Giant Skeleton dies.}}\n\nThe Giant Skeleton is an {{Rarity|Epic}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from the [[Arenas|Frozen Peak (Arena 8)]]. It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] with very high hitpoints and high damage. When defeated, he drops a bomb which explodes after 3 seconds, dealing damage, which is double against [[:Category:Crown Towers|Crown Towers]], and inflicting knockback. He appears to be a colossal [[Skeletons|Skeleton]] that wears a team-colored ushanka, has an underbite, and carries a large gunpowder barrel on his back and a black bomb on his hand. A Giant Skeleton card costs 6 [[Elixir]] to deploy.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Giant Skeleton Card}}\n*The Giant Skeleton is effective to use as a meat shield for other [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]]. This makes him a good [[Cards|card]] to use as a buffer for spawner [[:Category:Building Cards|buildings]], as any unit that takes out the Giant Skeleton will be taken out by his bomb afterwards while the spawner cooks up troops. They will continue unopposed to the [[:Category:Crown Towers|Crown Tower]]. However, he is not very interchangeable with a [[Giant]], as the Giant Skeleton can be distracted with any ground unit, has lesser hitpoints, and costs 1 more [[Elixir]].\n*The Giant Skeleton's death bomb is useful for weakening or destroying troops that are close together. The death damage is enough to defeat any unit up to a [[Magic Archer]], [[Zappies]], or [[Mother Witch]] on its own. Combined with [[The Log]] or a [[Barbarian Barrel]], the combo can finish off [[Musketeer]], [[Wizard]], [[Electro Wizard]], and the like. [[Tornado]] also works well with the Giant Skeleton, helping his death bomb and support troops to hit more units, and also allowing [[Zap]] and [[Giant Snowball]] to finish off the weakened troops.\n**If a Giant Skeleton or [[Mega Knight]] is used behind your Giant Skeleton, a Tornado placed close to the Tower will cause them to push your Giant Skeleton closer to the Tower. While the Giant Skeleton himself will not connect, his bomb will.\n*[[Clone]] can forcibly push an allied Giant Skeleton forward, allowing his death damage to hit the Crown Tower reliably. The cloned bomb can also help clean up the troops that the opponent used to defend the first Giant Skeleton. If the Giant Skeleton was already attacking the Crown Tower before being cloned, it is almost a guarantee that his clone's bomb will also detonate on top of the Tower, dealing much more damage for just 3 extra Elixir.\n*Since he moves quite slowly and has a low damage per second, players may need to back him up with splash damage troops, such as the [[Witch]], [[Princess]], [[Firecracker]], Bomber, or Wizard. This gives the Giant Skeleton a higher chance to connect to the Tower.\n*[[:Category:Spell Cards|Spells]] with an unrestricted placement also support a Giant Skeleton well once he has crossed the bridge, decimating swarms placed to destroy him.\n*The Giant Skeleton's high hitpoints and death bomb can cause the opponent to overcommit their defenses on him solely, allowing other troops (especially quick ones like the [[Lumberjack]] and the [[Berserker]]) to pass by unharmed.\n*Fast troops can push the Giant Skeleton forward and allow him to reach the Tower more quickly. However, depending on the unit that is used, the main weakness of the push may become amplified (eg. a [[Mini P.E.K.K.A.]], [[Elite Barbarians]], or a Prince combined with a Giant Skeleton is still weak to swarms and air units), so often times more than 1 layer of offense will be necessary.\n**On the other hand, a Giant Skeleton can bodyblock friendly units. For example; a Giant Skeleton can block a friendly [[Hog Rider]] from travelling towards a building, allowing him to connect to the Tower.\n*As the Giant Skeleton is easily countered by swarms, a good way to ensure the Giant Skeleton reaches the Tower is to deploy him with the [[Skeleton Army]]. The Skeleton Army will surround the Giant Skeleton and defeat any swarms and troops sent to surround him. Furthermore, they will also help damage the Tower, and some Skeletons can even remain to help deal some extra damage on the [[King's Tower|King Tower]]. This however, is easily countered by spells or splash-damaging troops such as the Wizard, [[Bomber]], [[Valkyrie]], Baby Dragon, [[Executioner]], [[Bowler]], and the Mega Knight.\n*Pairing the Giant Skeleton with another threatening troop can cause the opponent to split their defenses thin, allowing one or the other to connect. For example, a Giant Skeleton paired with a [[Balloon]] can block air units and protect the Balloon long enough for it to reach the Tower. Additionally, the defending units will almost certainly die to one or both of the death bombs, eliminating or weakening the opponent's opportunity to counterpush. However, this push is extremely weak to air units (such as the [[Inferno Dragon]] or the [[Minion Horde]]), so it may be more beneficial to play anti-air cards or anti-air spells along with the push.\n*The Giant Skeleton is a good way to stop a large push, since he can group the enemies up and his death bomb will cripple the push, allowing other cards to finish everything off. It is a good idea to combine him with distracting troops like [[Skeletons]] to allow the bomb to explode on the Giant Skeleton's targets.\n*Despite being a negative Elixir trade, the [[P.E.K.K.A.]] is a good counter to the Giant Skeleton, since she can easily defeat him and not let the Crown Tower take any death damage while also surviving with many of her hitpoints remaining.\n*The [[Inferno Tower]] is an effective counter to the Giant Skeleton. Not only does it cost 1 less Elixir, but is also less vulnerable to most enemy spells. If needed, the Giant Skeleton can also be pulled away with another cheap card such as Skeletons.\n*On defense, mini tanks such as [[Knight]], [[Lumberjack]], Valkyrie, Mini P.E.K.K.A, [[Battle Healer]], [[Prince]], [[Royal Ghost]], [[Golden Knight]], and [[Dark Prince]] can counter a lone Giant Skeleton, with the help of any [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|tower troop]] and have some hitpoints for a counterattack.\n*If the player has nothing else to defend with, a Giant can counter a Giant Skeleton that has reached the Tower. Deploying the Giant in front of the Crown Tower pushes the Giant Skeleton, thus causing it to retarget, and since it is slow, it will lure the Giant Skeleton away from the Tower, taking away with it and saving the Tower from the enormous death damage that the Giant Skeleton can deal.\n*Despite not dealing high damage, the Giant Skeleton can stop a Mega Knight on his own, thanks to his higher attack speed and decently high hitpoints. With the help of any tower troop, he will also be left with half his hitpoints for a counterattack.\n*Although situational, the Giant Skeleton can be used as a distraction for enemy air troops, as his hitpoints are high enough to tank many shots from units such as [[Baby Dragon]], [[Electro Dragon]], [[Phoenix]], [[Skeleton Dragons]], or [[Flying Machine]].\n*When using a [[Fisherman]], the player should not play him behind the King Tower, as this is an opportunity for the Giant Skeleton to be pulled by the Fisherman and landing next to the Crown Tower. Instead it should be used as a reaction when the Giant Skeleton is a low health in order to pull him away from the Tower and drop the bomb in a spot away from the Tower.\n*A [[Mighty Miner]] or [[Monk]] can counter a Giant Skeleton for a positive Elixir trade. If the Mighty Miner was misplaced, it may be a good idea to use his ability, to allow him to escape the Giant Skeleton's bomb, which is still a positive Elixir trade.\n*The Giant Skeleton can be a good tank for pushes in [[Global Spell|Rage Battles]], as his increased speed will force the opponent to put more focus on him to prevent colossal damage to Towers. However, this benefit comes with a catch - a decreased viability on defense, since enemy troops will be able to quickly leave his death bomb's range before it detonates.\n\n<center>\n\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\">{{Statistics}}\n{{StatusEffects}}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Giant Skeleton Attributes}}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width:100%; text-align:center;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |Cost<br />{{Icon|I=Elixir}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |First Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Deploy Time<br />{{Icon|I=Deploy Time}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Range<br />{{Icon|I=Range}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Target<br />{{Icon|I=Targets}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Count<br />{{Icon|I=Troop Count}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Transport<br />{{Icon|I=Transport}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Type<br />{{Icon|I=Type}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Rarity<br />{{Icon|I=Rarity}}\n|-\n|6||1.3 sec||0.3 sec||Medium (60)||1 sec||Melee: Short (0.8)||Ground||x1||Ground||[[:Category:Troop Cards|Troop]]||{{Rarity|Epic}}\n|}\n\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Bomb Attributes}}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width:100%; text-align:center;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |Death Damage Splash Radius<br />{{Icon|I=Radius}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Deploy Time<br />{{Icon|I=Deploy Time}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Target<br />{{Icon|I=Targets}}\n|-\n|3||3 sec||Air & Ground\n|}\n\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Card Statistics}}\n<!--\nthis will not be shown\n================================================================\nATTENTION EDITORS: DO NOT MANUALLY UPDATE THE TABLE CELLS!
