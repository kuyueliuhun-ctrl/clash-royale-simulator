Pancakes take longer to cook when he's attacking, and he serves Troops who haven't eaten yet}}\n\nThe Royal Chef is a {{Rarity|Legendary}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from [[Arenas|Rascal's Hideout (Arena 13)]]. It is a single-target, air-targeting, long-ranged [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|tower troop]] with moderate hitpoints and low damage. Around every 23 seconds, he will throw a pancake at an allied troop on the Arena, increasing their Level by 1. The Royal Chef appears to be a man with aqua eyes, a gold tooth, blue hair and moustache, wears a white toque which covers his eyes and uniform with team-colored cuffs, a brown apron with gold buttons, a belt with a crown emblem, and team-colored pants, carries a pan and a spatula, and has a peg leg.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Royal Chef Card}}\n*The Royal Chef and his pancakes prioritizes higher-hitpoint troops over the lower ones. This makes him great to be used in decks with large tanks, such as [[Giant]], [[Golem]], [[Electro Giant]], [[Goblin Giant]], [[Lava Hound]] and [[Royal Giant]] decks.\n*The Royal Chef will try to spread out the pancakes into multiple troops instead of concentrating all of them into one troop. For example, in a [[Goblin Giant]]-[[Sparky]] push, if the Goblin Giant has already received a pancake, then the Sparky will receive the next pancake.\n*The Royal Chef will ignore troops with less than 33% of their maximum health for pancake-giving purposes. He will also ignore any [[:Category:Building Cards|building]].\n*Aside for regular stats, the pancakes of the Royal Chef will affect most unique effects of the troops that are fed.\n**Troops with shields can be fed and their shield will inherit the stat boost from the extra Level, just like their regular hitpoints do.\n**Similarly any troops with overheal hitpoints will have their overhealed hitpoints increased as well, in addition to their regular hitpoints.\n**Any troop that activates a special mode when they reach a certain hitpoint threshold will only be affected indirectly. The threshold will not change, but they will be able to activate it at higher hitpoints overall. In addition, any statistics inherited by the alternative mode will be affected by the feeding.\n**Fed units that spawn other troops, whether periodically (like the [[Witch]] or [[Night Witch]]) or upon death (like the [[Skeleton Barrel]], [[Goblin Giant]], [[Golem]], [[Elixir Golem]], [[Battle Ram]], or [[Lava Hound]]) will spawn leveled-up troops. This extends to {{Rarity|Champion}} cards with abilities that spawn other troops like the [[Skeleton King]] and [[Little Prince]].\n**A fed Goblin Giant will affect both the Goblin Giant and his Spear Goblins on his backpack, as well as when they spawn after death. A [[Ram Rider]] and [[Goblin Machine]] function in a similar manner.\n**Fed units can still cause various death effects.\n***Like the other spawning troops, a fed [[Mother Witch]] will spawn Cursed Hogs that match her own Level, regardless on the Level of the troop that she has cursed.\n***A fed [[Lumberjack]] will drop a leveled-up [[Rage]], allowing the spell to deal more damage. The [[Lumberjack/Evolution|Evolved Lumberjack]] will also spawn a leveled-up Lumberjack Ghost in addition to the Rage.\n***A fed Skeleton Barrel will drop a leveled-up container, which will increase its death damage, as well as the levels of the Skeletons.\n***However, in the case of items dropped by units such as [[Giant Skeleton]], [[Balloon]], and [[Mighty Miner]], the items cannot be fed, though their parent troops will still drop a leveled-up item if fed.\n**Since the troop fed by the pancake will have already spawned, fed [[Ice Wizard|Ice Wizards]], [[Electro Wizard|Electro Wizards]] and [[Mega Knight|Mega Knights]] will not have their spawn damage raised, and a fed [[Battle Healer]] will not have her spawn healing raised.\n*The cooking speed will change depending on if 0/1/2 attacks, so it's a good idea to pressure if someone is trying to build up the levels.\n*If one tower is destroyed, the cooking speed will slow down (like it's attacking). If both towers are destroyed, the cooking will stop completely.\n*The Royal Chef can level up troops past the Level cap. It's possible to level up a single troop multiple during a single match, though this would be tricky to pull off, since you would need to keep that troop above 33% of its max health as well as the fact that the Royal Chef prioritizes troops who haven't been fed yet. This can be done more easily by manipulating the Royal Chef to target the same troop with their cards in the deck being ones untargetable by the Royal Chef (such as using buildings and spells).\n*The pancake can cause a troop to gain enough hitpoints to survive certain spells, making placing these troops in the back safer compared to doing this with other [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|tower troops]].\n**A fed [[Dark Prince]] will allow his shield to survive a [[Barbarian Barrel]] and Earthquake, compared to a same Level one.\n**A fed [[Elixir Golem]] will spawn Elixir Blobs that can survive [[Arrows]], compared to a same Level one.\n**A fed [[Ice Wizard]] will be able to survive a [[Fireball]] or [[Poison]], compared to a same Level one. A fed [[Musketeer]], [[Electro Wizard]], [[Little Prince]], or [[Goblinstein]] will also be able to survive a Poison, compared to a same Level one.\n**A fed [[Electro Dragon]] will be able to survive a [[Void]], compared to a same Level one.\n**A fed [[Archer Queen]], Battle Ram, or [[Phoenix]] will be able to survive a [[Lightning]], compared to a same Level one.\n**A fed [[Mini P.E.K.K.A.|Mini P.E.K.K.A]] or Sparky will be able to survive a [[Rocket]], compared to a same Level one.\n*The player should be careful when using the Barbarian Barrel and [[Royal Delivery]] with this tower troop, as the [[Barbarians|Barbarian]] or [[Royal Recruits|Royal Recruit]] will cause the Royal Chef to throw a pancake on them which would remove the opportunity to throw it on a more potent troop. However, the player may want to have the Barbarian or Royal Recruit fed by the pancake if they want to defend more efficiently.\n*The Royal Chef's damage is the same as a [[Tower Princess]]', though he attacks slower and has less hitpoints. As such, most strategies used with the Tower Princess will work with the Royal Chef, although it's much less affordable to ignore troops that deal chip damage.\n<center>\n\n<div class=\"table-back\">{{Statistics}}\n{{StatusEffects}}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Royal Chef Attributes}}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width: 100%; text-align: center;\"\n!
