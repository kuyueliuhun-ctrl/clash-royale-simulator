It spawns a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troop</a> with moderate hitpoints and moderate damage. If there are ground units between 3.5 and 7 tiles of him, he will stop moving and begin charging a hook attack which takes 1.3 seconds to execute, and either pulls enemy troops towards him, or pulls himself towards <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">buildings</a>, and slow their movement and attack speed by 35% for 1.5 seconds. The Fisherman takes an appearance as a man with a large nose, blue eyes, blonde eyebrows and beard, wearing a team-colored hat which covers his eyes and overalls with a team-colored patch on it and a star shell behind it, and carries a hook tied on a rope and a deep indigo fish. class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a title=\"Sign in to edit\" class=\"mw-editsection-visualeditor\" data-tracking-label=\"log-in-edit-section\" data-action=\"edit-section\" href=\"https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fclashroyale.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFisherman%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en&amp;metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section\" xlink:href=\"#wds-icons-pencil-tiny\"></use></svg></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Fisherman_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Fisherman Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>The Fisherman's hook has a range of 7 tiles. Players should keep this in mind when deciding where to place him against enemy <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> to pull them.</li>\n<li>In 1-on-1 battles, the Fisherman is mediocre against any troop with high damage at close range, so players should avoid using the Fisherman alone to reel in troops that deal high amounts of damage to close targets such as a <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a>, Mini P.E.K.K.A., <a href=\"/wiki/Elite_Barbarians\" title=\"Elite Barbarians\">Elite Barbarians</a>, Prince, Mighty Miner, or a <a href=\"/wiki/P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"P.E.K.K.A.\">P.E.K.K.A.</a> unless they desperately need to pull him away from their <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Tower_Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Tower Troop Cards\">Crown Tower</a>. However, pairing the <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> with swarm troops like <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a>, Goblins, or <a href=\"/wiki/Guards\" title=\"Guards\">Guards</a> to distract the enemy troop can protect the Fisherman and allow him to stay on the board for a counterpush.</li>\n<li>On the contrary, the Fisherman can synergize well with close-range melee troops as the Fisherman can pull enemy troops closer to them, so the troop is able to hit more easily. A very powerful version of this synergy is the Fisherman + Hunter combo. The Fisherman's health pool can allow him to tank for the somewhat frail Hunter, and the Hunter will be able to land all of his shots thanks to the Fisherman hooking units towards them.</li>\n<li>Unlike the <a href=\"/wiki/Bandit\" title=\"Bandit\">Bandit</a>, the Fisherman is not immune to damage while in the process of grabbing, and can actually be defeated mid-hook. This is good to know for the case of saving your troop from any additional danger that could be caused by being pulled from the Fisherman.</li>\n<li>The Fisherman is best used as a defensive support card to separate a tank from support troops, primarily <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">building</a>-targeting units. Players should avoid cycling him in the back as opponent may take advantage of his hook and make him pull something unfavorable towards their Tower, like a <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Skeleton\" title=\"Giant Skeleton\">Giant Skeleton</a>, Mighty Miner, P.E.K.K.A, <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golem</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Monk\" title=\"Monk\">Monk</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Giant\" title=\"Goblin Giant\">Goblin Giant</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Giant\" title=\"Electro Giant\">Electro Giant</a>, Mega Knight, or a <a href=\"/wiki/Hog_Rider\" title=\"Hog Rider\">Hog Rider</a>.\n<ul><li>He can counter a Hog Rider completely, with or without a <a href=\"/wiki/King%27s_Tower\" title=\"King&#39;s Tower\">King Tower</a> activation. The player should hover the Fisherman on the third tile from the river and on the second tile horizontally in the opposite lane they think the Hog Rider will be deployed in. Once the Hog Rider has been deployed, drop the Fisherman as the Hog Rider's deploy timer is halfway finished. The Hog Rider will just barely be defeated before swinging at the Crown Tower.</li>\n<li>If timed correctly, the Fisherman can counter a same-level lone <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a> by pulling him towards the King Tower and having both Crown Towers target him. If you don't feel too safe doing this, you can place a <a href=\"/wiki/Mini_P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"Mini P.E.K.K.A.\">Mini P.E.K.K.A</a>, Prince, <a href=\"/wiki/Mighty_Miner\" title=\"Mighty Miner\">Mighty Miner</a>, Hunter, <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Dragon\" title=\"Inferno Dragon\">Inferno Dragon</a>, Elite Barbarians, or a <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Minion\" title=\"Mega Minion\">Mega Minion</a> to help finish him off.</li>\n<li>If the Fisherman is always played close to the river to hook away troops that target buildings, the attacking player can distract his hook with cheap swarms, allowing the building chaser to get to the Tower uninterrupted.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Fisherman can be used against most building chasers or melee area damage troops to activate the King's Tower by placing him in front of it, slightly into the opposite lane. Most of the time, the Crown Tower will be forced to take a bit of damage before the King's Tower is activated, so the player should only do this if their Tower is still healthy.\n<ul><li>Against continuous ranged splashers like the <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Archer\" title=\"Magic Archer\">Magic Archer</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber/Evolution\" title=\"Bomber/Evolution\">Evolved Bomber</a>, placing the Fisherman on the fourth tile vertically from the King's Tower and the third tile horizontally from the Crown Tower as the troop walks in his range will force a King activation after hooking them.</li>\n<li>The Fisherman can be deployed slightly earlier against building chasers so that he will hook them right as they are about to attack the Crown Tower, resulting in no damage. However, missing this timing will cause the King Tower activation to fail completely.</li>\n<li>Note that regardless of timing, all three Crown Towers will be distracted with the troop on the King's Tower for a short amount of time. The opponent may deploy frail, high damage troops on offense to take advantage of this vulnerability. If the player knows they won't have enough <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> to counter follow up troops, it may be better to defend the building chaser normally. Pulling powerful units like the Giant Skeleton to the King's Tower is not recommended more than twice, as the damage dealt could possibly result in a back door 3 Crown later in the match.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Players should take advantage of his hook and pull units away from their Tower. This is especially effective against a Giant Skeleton as the bomb won't detonate on top of the Tower and deal massive damage.</li>\n<li>If the player knows the opponent is going to send a <a href=\"/wiki/Miner\" title=\"Miner\">Miner</a> to the back of their Crown Tower, they can deploy the Fisherman behind the King's Tower in the same lane of the Miner. If timed correctly, the Miner will retarget to the King Tower and activate it.</li>\n<li>Cards with a continual stun are very good counters to a Fisherman, as he will be permanently stuck charging his hook until he is defeated. The <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Dragon\" title=\"Electro Dragon\">Electro Dragon</a> and Electro Wizard are the most reliable due to the former's chain lightning and the latter's split attack making it more likely for the Fisherman to be hit in larger pushes.</li>\n<li>If timed correctly, the Fisherman's hook can reset the <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ram_Rider\" title=\"Ram Rider\">Ram Rider</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Dark_Prince\" title=\"Dark Prince\">Dark Prince</a>'s charges; as well as being able to stop the Mega Knight from getting his jump off. Interestingly, the Fisherman can also stop the Bandit mid-dash with his hook and cancel it, despite the latter usually being invincible during it.</li>\n<li>If the Fisherman is deployed alone with no support, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a> can counter him. The Fisherman does not deal enough damage to OHKO a Goblin.</li>\n<li>The Fisherman's range allows him to pull away opposing heavy hitters, which can be useful if one is played in response to a push, allowing the push to remain mostly unharmed.</li>\n<li>The Fisherman can counter a <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard\" title=\"Wizard\">Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Wizard\" title=\"Electro Wizard\">Electro Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Archer_Queen\" title=\"Archer Queen\">Archer Queen</a>, or a Hunter just barely when pulled to your side of the Arena for a positive <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> trade. Likewise, this tactic works well against most ranged troops.</li>\n<li>Another positive Elixir trade is a Fisherman can counter a <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a> if it\u2019s placed near the centre of your side near the King Tower; both of your Crown Towers will be engaged to the Valkyrie along with the Fisherman\u2019s slowing affect and damage.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Giant\" title=\"Royal Giant\">Royal Giant</a> synergizes quite well with the Fisherman. The Fisherman will pull any high damage per second troops away from the Royal Giant, which will force the opponent to drop more Elixir. However, this can be easily predicted and your opponent may drop a distraction card to distract the Fisherman. You can prevent this by using <a href=\"/wiki/The_Log\" title=\"The Log\">The Log</a>. You can also use the Fisherman to snipe buildings by placing him at the middle of the river to finish of buildings like the <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Tower\" title=\"Inferno Tower\">Inferno Tower</a> to help your Royal Giant break through.</li>\n<li>The Mortar also synergizes well with the Fisherman. Any troop used to distract the <a href=\"/wiki/Mortar\" title=\"Mortar\">Mortar</a>, such as a Mini P.E.K.K.A. or <a href=\"/wiki/Lumberjack\" title=\"Lumberjack\">Lumberjack</a>, will be pulled into the Mortar\u2019s dead zone, allowing it to connect to the Crown Tower quicker. Buildings used to distract the Mortar may be damaged by the Fisherman, weakening them for the Mortar to connect. However, this combination gives spell value to the opponent. If they so choose, a <a href=\"/wiki/Rocket\" title=\"Rocket\">Rocket</a> fully counter this combination for a +1 Elixir trade if the Fisherman is too close to the Mortar.</li></ul>\n<center>\n<div
