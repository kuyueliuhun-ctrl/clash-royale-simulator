Does not affect flying units (it is an EARTHquake, after all).}}\n\nThe Earthquake is a {{Rarity|Rare}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from the [[Arenas|Spooky Town (Arena 12)]]. It is an area-damage, ground-targeting [[:Category:Spell Cards|spell]] with a medium radius and low damage, also doing 3.5 times damage to [[:Category:Building Cards|buildings]], that is dealt every second for 3 seconds. It also decreases movement speed of enemy [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]] by 50%. It is a murky brown liquid encased in a small oblong vial. An Earthquake card costs 3 [[Elixir]] to deploy.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Earthquake Card}}\n*Since the Earthquake does massive damage to most [[:Category:Building Cards|buildings]], it can be a good [[Elixir]] trade against structures such as the [[Elixir Collector]] and spawner buildings like the [[Goblin Hut]], as it is more cost-effective than a [[Fireball]] or [[Rocket]].\n**A lone Earthquake will fully stop a [[Tombstone]] by itself for a neutral Elixir trade. The first two ticks will destroy the Tombstone itself, while the third tick will kill the [[Skeletons]] that spawn after death. Use this in case you want to allow a building-targeting [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] to continue proceeding towards the [[:Category:Crown Towers|Crown Towers]] without having to worry about the Skeletons.\n**It may be a good idea to utilize both Earthquake and Fireball in a [[Battle Decks|deck]] in case of bait decks. Earthquake will handle those buildings while Fireball is saved for an attacking troop like the [[Three Musketeers]] or [[Wizard]].\n*The Earthquake deals slightly less Crown Tower damage than a Rocket and equal as much to [[Poison]], but compensates for its lower cost, making it good to use in [[:Category:Spell Cards|spell]] cycle decks.\n*It is generally not a good idea to use it on defense due to its low [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] damage, but in a pinch the 50% slowdown effect can prevent the opponent's push from decimating the Tower. The 50% slow effect does not stack with the slow from the [[Ice Wizard]], [[Ice Golem]], and the [[Giant Snowball]].\n*Against the [[Inferno Tower]], Earthquake combined with a [[Zap]] can severely cripple, if not take out the defensive building entirely. This is a great alternative to [[Lightning]], for 1 less Elixir. A lone Earthquake is not worth using on an Inferno Tower though, as the Inferno Tower will be able to kill any incoming tank before Earthquake destroys it.\n*It is also a great blanket counter to offensive building [[cards]] like [[X-Bow]] and [[Mortar]], though you will need something else to take them out fully or distract their targeting.\n*An Earthquake is able to damage a [[Tesla]] even if it has been concealed, making it one of the most efficient ways to eliminate the building at a positive Elixir trade.\n*The Earthquake does enough damage to destroy a [[Goblin Cage]] by itself and release the Goblin Brawler.\n*Against a [[Goblin Barrel]], place the Earthquake when the Goblin Barrel's shadow is 4 tiles from the river. This will prevent the most damage from the spell for a neutral Elixir trade.\n*It pairs best with the [[Hog Rider]] and the [[Royal Hogs]]. Both are fast moving troops that appreciate being able to take down buildings relatively quickly. The spell also does enough damage to OHKO [[Skeletons]] in one tick and to completely destroy [[Goblins]] with all three ticks, making trying to support the building with cheap follow up troops difficult.\n*Using the same principle, the [[Miner]] can pair well with Earthquake. The spell's 3 second duration gives it an area denial effect, and can clear away many of the swarms typically used to counter a Miner while dealing considerable Crown Tower damage. However, compared to Poison, Earthquake can't fully defeat troops like the [[Dart Goblin]], [[Princess]], [[Archers]], [[Firecracker]], [[Bomber]], or the [[Rascals|Rascal Girls]], nor can it target aerial troops such as [[Bats]] or [[Minions]].\n**When attacking a passive building with a Miner, it may be a good idea to feign the attack on the building (i.e. aiming for the Crown Tower instead) in order to make the opponent waste Elixir, which can allowing for Earthquake to deal damage on their unit, the Crown Tower, and the building.\n*It can also work well with large tanks like the [[Giant]], [[Electro Giant]], [[Goblin Giant]], [[Elixir Golem]], and [[Golem]]. Due to all of them being weak to buildings, Earthquake will assist them in breaking through, to allow them to proceed to the Crown Tower instead.\n*A full Earthquake eliminates Goblins up to 1 Level higher, and all Spirit cards of the same Level. Two ticks eliminate [[Spear Goblins]], while a single tick is enough to take out Skeletons.\n*The Earthquake can be combined with [[The Log]], in a similar way to other spell combinations in order to fully defeat certain troops. Together, the two spells are able to defeat anything up to an [[Elixir Golem|Elixir Blob]]. However, they are a negative Elixir trade, so try to use them when you hit other troops as well.\n*Since the Earthquake does its damage in 3 ticks, it can be a semi-decent counter to [[Graveyard]]. You must place the Earthquake after 2 waves of Skeletons spawn from the Graveyard in order to get its full effect.\n*When using the Earthquake against the [[Cannon Cart]], always wait for its shield to be destroyed first before deploying the Earthquake. This will allow the spell to deal its full damage to it, saving more of your attacking units' hitpoints.\n\n<center>\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\">{{Statistics}}\n{|class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width:100%; text-align:center;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |Cost<br />{{Icon|I=Elixir}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Radius<br />{{Icon|I=Radius}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Duration<br />{{Icon|I=Time}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Slowdown<br />{{Icon|I=Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Target<br />{{Icon|I=Targets}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Type<br />{{Icon|I=Type}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Rarity<br />{{Icon|I=Rarity}}\n|-\n|3||3.5||3 sec||-50%||Ground||[[:Category:Spell Cards|Spell]]||{{Rarity|Rare}}\n|}\n<!--\nthis will not be shown\n================================================================\nATTENTION EDITORS: DO NOT MANUALLY UPDATE THE TABLE CELLS!
