They appear as small machines having an upwards coil on a wooden chassis and wheels, with a spiked steel ram at the front and team-colored flags at the rear. class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a title=\"Sign in to edit\" class=\"mw-editsection-visualeditor\" data-tracking-label=\"log-in-edit-section\" data-action=\"edit-section\" href=\"https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fclashroyale.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FZappies%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&amp;uselang=en&amp;metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section\" xlink:href=\"#wds-icons-pencil-tiny\"></use></svg></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Zappies_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Zappies Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>Since Zappies can stun, they are effective counters against a lone&#160;<a href=\"/wiki/Fisherman\" title=\"Fisherman\">Fisherman</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ram_Rider\" title=\"Ram Rider\">Ram Rider</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mighty_Miner\" title=\"Mighty Miner\">Mighty Miner</a>, Sparky, <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Dark_Prince\" title=\"Dark Prince\">Dark Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Tower\" title=\"Inferno Tower\">Inferno Tower</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Dragon\" title=\"Inferno Dragon\">Inferno Dragon</a>.</li>\n<li>Zappies can be very effective against low hit speed <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Baby_Dragon\" title=\"Baby Dragon\">Baby Dragon</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Dragons\" title=\"Skeleton Dragons\">Skeleton Dragons</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Bowler\" title=\"Bowler\">Bowler</a> because they can cripple them with constant stuns, making them almost unable to attack. However, as most of such troops have attacks that can hit multiple troops, do not rely solely on the Zappies to defeat them.</li>\n<li>Since Zappies have low hitpoints, effective counters are <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spells</a> such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Fireball\" title=\"Fireball\">Fireball</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Poison\" title=\"Poison\">Poison</a>. Both spells can destroy Zappies for a neutral <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> trade.\n<ul><li>Split two Zappies on the lane with the healthier <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Tower_Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Tower Troop Cards\">Crown Tower</a> to minimize the spell value.</li>\n<li>Consider using other spell vulnerable troops with the Zappies such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Hogs\" title=\"Royal Hogs\">Royal Hogs</a> to try and bait out the opponent's spell so that they won't be able to take out the Zappies on offense. Alternatively, in matchups where Zappies would not have a great use, they can be used to bait out these spells so that the other spell vulnerable troops can attack unharmed.</li></ul></li>\n<li>They can be decent support behind a tank since they can cripple <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">buildings</a>, especially the <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Tower\" title=\"Inferno Tower\">Inferno Tower</a>, and with their stuns, they will leave the Crown Tower barely being able to shoot.</li>\n<li>Zappies crossing the bridge can be countered by the <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarian_Barrel\" title=\"Barbarian Barrel\">Barbarian Barrel</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Delivery\" title=\"Royal Delivery\">Royal Delivery</a> fairly well even when supported by a tank, so try to either have support <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">cards</a> to quickly eliminate the follow-up defenders, or simply not push when these counters are in cycle.</li>\n<li>Zappies can be good counters to small swarms like <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Archers\" title=\"Archers\">Archers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Minions\" title=\"Minions\">Minions</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Spear_Goblins\" title=\"Spear Goblins\">Spear Goblins</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bats\" title=\"Bats\">Bats</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Guards\" title=\"Guards\">Guards</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a>, and with the help of a Crown Tower, they can also counter larger swarms like the <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Army\" title=\"Skeleton Army\">Skeleton Army</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Minion_Horde\" title=\"Minion Horde\">Minion Horde</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Gang\" title=\"Goblin Gang\">Goblin Gang</a>. However, defensively, these swarms can also be used to counter the Zappies, by surrounding them with the said swarm.</li>\n<li>Zappies are most effective as defensive troops against single targeting units, especially slower ones as all three will focus their attacks onto them, making their stun effect much more effective at slowing them down. Placing them beside the Tower will make them line up vertically and efficiently stagger the timing of their stuns, leaving the enemy troop nearly immobile.</li>\n<li>Zappies can easily be stumped by proper ranged support troops like the <a href=\"/wiki/Executioner\" title=\"Executioner\">Executioner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard\" title=\"Wizard\">Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Firecracker\" title=\"Firecracker\">Firecracker</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber\" title=\"Bomber\">Bomber</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a>, or spell combos, so try and bait these out beforehand.</li>\n<li>The Zappies have an medium first attack, so placing them down on top of a unit to distract them is generally a bad idea. Instead, place them behind the Crown Tower so they have more time to charge their shots and stun approaching units.</li>\n<li>Zappies counter <a href=\"/wiki/Balloon\" title=\"Balloon\">Balloon</a> fairly well since their stalling makes it harder to reach the Balloon's goal. They can also survive many spells like <a href=\"/wiki/Arrows\" title=\"Arrows\">Arrows</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Snowball\" title=\"Giant Snowball\">Giant Snowball</a> commonly used with the Balloon, necessitating Fireball in order to defeat them.</li>\n<li>Zappies can synergize well with <a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a>. They can bait out the Graveyard's hardest counter, Poison. Additionally, even just a single Zappy supporting a Graveyard can be deadly, as it will survive to hit and stun the Tower once, making it retarget onto the spawned Skeletons, letting the Zappy stay alive and continue to slow down the tower's shots. The stun from the singular Zappy will also allow for more Skeletons to spawn before the existing ones are killed, further increasing the damage that the Graveyard will deal.</li>\n<li>When approached from a distance, <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a> will one shot the trio of Zappies before they can reset her attack due to her range. To properly counter the Sparky, wait for it to cross the bridge and surround her so that one or two Zappies will survive and leave her permanently stunned until death. This is also effective against area damage troops that lack a 360\u00b0 area damage effect.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a> is extremely effective against the Zappies. While his Spawn Damage is not enough to defeat them immediately, it will lower it to a point where one regular attack from him can finish them off. In addition, Zappies are also usually paired with other troops, so the Mega Knight gains additional value from his Spawn Damage by hitting more troops.</li>\n<li>If a Crown Tower is at a health range where a <a href=\"/wiki/Lightning\" title=\"Lightning\">Lightning</a> could finish it off, Zappies can be placed in the expected area to block the spell, as they will likely have more hitpoints than the Crown Tower. This can be an unexpected way to save your Crown Tower from being destroyed and preventing a loss, especially in a Sudden Death match.</li></ul>\n<center>\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" BCE where Ancient Egyptian texts described them as the \"protectors\" of all other fish. At around 600 BCE, Thales of Miletus made a series of observations on static electricity, from which he believed that friction rendered amber magnetic, in contrast to minerals such as magnetite, which needed no rubbing. Thales was incorrect in believing the attraction was due to a magnetic effect, but later science would prove a link between magnetism and electricity. Electricity would remain little more than an intellectual curiosity for millennia until 1600, when the English scientist William Gilbert wrote <i>De Magnete</i>, in which he made a careful study of electricity and magnetism, distinguishing the lodestone effect from static electricity produced by rubbing amber.\n<ul><li>The Zappies are 1 of 11 <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">cards</a> associated with electricity, with the others being <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Spirit\" title=\"Electro Spirit\">Electro Spirit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Tesla\" title=\"Tesla\">Tesla</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Dragon\" title=\"Electro Dragon\">Electro Dragon</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Giant\" title=\"Electro Giant\">Electro Giant</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Lightning\" title=\"Lightning\">Lightning</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Wizard\" title=\"Electro Wizard\">Electro Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard_Trio\" title=\"Wizard Trio\">Wizard Trio</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblinstein\" title=\"Goblinstein\">Goblinstein</a>.\n<ul><li>In the French and Portuguese language settings of the game, the Zappies' name translates to \"Electrocutioners\". In the Spanish, Turkish, Korean, Russian, Chinese, Persian, Indonesian, Malaysian, and Vietnamese language settings of the game, the Zappies' name translates to \"Shockers\", \"Shock Union\", \"Electric Shockers\", \"Mini Generators\", \"Electric Car Squad\", \"Sparkers\", \"Electrocutters\", \"Zap-Zaps\", and \"Lightning Shock Robots\" respectively.</li></ul></li></ul></li>\n<li>The
