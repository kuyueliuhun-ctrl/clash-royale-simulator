class=\"mw-empty-elt\"></p><p><br />\n</p>\n<div class=\"quote-block\"><i><b>\u201cThree ruthless bone brothers with shields. Knock off their shields and all that's left are three ruthless bone brothers.\u201d</b></i></div>\n<p>The Guards is an <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Epic_Cards\" title=\"Category:Epic Cards\"><span style=\"color: #FF99FF; font-weight: bold;\">Epic</span></a> <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> that is unlocked from the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">P.E.K.K.A's Playhouse (Arena 6)</a>. It spawns three fast single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> with low hitpoints and low damage with shields in addition to their normal hitpoints. Their deployment is in a triangle formation. They appear to be <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a>, but are somewhat taller, wear black helmets with team-colored feathers, and carry spears and wooden shields with a crown emblem on them. A Guards card costs 3 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> to deploy.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Strategy\">Strategy</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Guards_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Guards Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>The Guards function similar to <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a> with triple the cost, but are more reliable defensively thanks to their shields improving their resistance against enemy <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spells</a> or any other powerful attack, as any excess damage is negated.\n<ul><li>If <a href=\"/wiki/Lightning\" title=\"Lightning\">Lightning</a> is used on Guards, only the shields will be destroyed. This is because the shield and the unit are both considered the same entity.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Guards are best used as a defensive <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, then used in a counterpush with something that can tank for them, such as a <a href=\"/wiki/Miner\" title=\"Miner\">Miner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Golem\" title=\"Ice Golem\">Ice Golem</a>, or a <a href=\"/wiki/Hog_Rider\" title=\"Hog Rider\">Hog Rider</a>.</li>\n<li>Their defensive potential marks them as a low-risk, high-reward option. They can eliminate ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> like groups of&#160;Skeletons&#160;and can distract air troops like&#160;<a href=\"/wiki/Minions\" title=\"Minions\">Minions</a>, making them a good pick against an incoming enemy push for gaining positive <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> trades. They don't have much damage or pushing power for 3 Elixir, so they should be rarely used on offense; they could even fail to take out tanky troops like the <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Giant\" title=\"Royal Giant\">Royal Giant</a> quickly.</li>\n<li>The Guards can be used effectively to counter a lone <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>. With their shield, they can withstand a blow from the Sparky and destroy her before she shoots a second time. For better results, surround her by spawning them directly on her so only one Guard gets hit with the blast. They will quickly destroy Sparky with the help of a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Crown_Towers\" title=\"Category:Crown Towers\">Crown Tower</a>, leaving the player two Guards with shields and one without.</li>\n<li>The vast majority of single-target troops or those with slow attacks can be easily countered by Guards, including <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"P.E.K.K.A.\">P.E.K.K.A</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Skeleton\" title=\"Giant Skeleton\">Giant Skeleton</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Boss_Bandit\" title=\"Boss Bandit\">Boss Bandit</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Mini_P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"Mini P.E.K.K.A.\">Mini P.E.K.K.A</a>. They are especially effective against ones that cannot destroy their shields in one hit, such as <a href=\"/wiki/Knight\" title=\"Knight\">Knight</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bandit\" title=\"Bandit\">Bandit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Healer\" title=\"Battle Healer\">Battle Healer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Archer_Queen\" title=\"Archer Queen\">Archer Queen</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Wizard\" title=\"Electro Wizard\">Electro Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Golden_Knight\" title=\"Golden Knight\">Golden Knight</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Fisherman\" title=\"Fisherman\">Fisherman</a>.</li>\n<li>Guards are able to counter <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a> if placed dirrectly on top of him circling his so he only hit one at a time. This must be done before his jump animation. Proper placement is mandatory and will not fully countered if not placed right.</li>\n<li>Despite having a shield, certain spells are still effective counters to Guards.\n<ul><li><a href=\"/wiki/The_Log\" title=\"The Log\">The Log</a> can make short work of the Guards since it knocks off the Guards' shields, making them weak enough for the Crown Tower to finish them off. An <a href=\"/wiki/Zap/Evolution\" title=\"Zap/Evolution\">Evolved Zap</a> can be used similarly.</li>\n<li><a href=\"/wiki/Poison\" title=\"Poison\">Poison</a> can be used to completely take them out, due to the 8 ticks of Poison being more than capable of taking their shield out first and finishing them off second. It takes 3 seconds for it to destroy the shields, while an additional one is required for the Guards themselves.</li>\n<li>If the Guards are alone, they can be destroyed entirely with the <a href=\"/wiki/Void\" title=\"Void\">Void</a> for a neutral Elixir trade. The shields will be taken down with 2 of the strikes, while the last one will finish off the Guards. The player can deploy any card that spawns 2 or more troops to save the Guards from the spell, even preventing their shield from being removed.</li>\n<li>While they cannot fully destroy them, <a href=\"/wiki/Arrows\" title=\"Arrows\">Arrows</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Delivery\" title=\"Royal Delivery\">Royal Delivery</a> have the same Elixir cost and enough damage to destroy the Guards' shields. The latter will also spawn a <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Recruits\" title=\"Royal Recruits\">Royal Recruit</a> to assist with further defense against them.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarian_Barrel\" title=\"Barbarian Barrel\">Barbarian Barrel</a> can be used similarly to the Royal Delivery and for 1 less Elixir. However, it is unable to destroy the shields by itself and will require the assistance of the <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarians\" title=\"Barbarians\">Barbarian</a> to do so.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Guards can be countered with area damage troops or <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">buildings</a>. Cards like <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber\" title=\"Bomber\">Bomber</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Ghost\" title=\"Royal Ghost\">Royal Ghost</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Wizard\" title=\"Ice Wizard\">Ice Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Firecracker\" title=\"Firecracker\">Firecracker</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Princess\" title=\"Princess\">Princess</a> are most effective, while the <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard\" title=\"Wizard\">Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Executioner\" title=\"Executioner\">Executioner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Dark_Prince\" title=\"Dark Prince\">Dark Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Archer\" title=\"Magic Archer\">Magic Archer</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bowler\" title=\"Bowler\">Bowler</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Demolisher\" title=\"Goblin Demolisher\">Goblin Demolisher</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_King\" title=\"Skeleton King\">Skeleton King</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Bomb_Tower\" title=\"Bomb Tower\">Bomb Tower</a> are also good, though expensive, alternative options.\n<ul><li>Although not dealing area damage, the <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Dragon\" title=\"Electro Dragon\">Electro Dragon</a> will be able to hit all three of the Guards with one attack. It takes it 3 attacks to fully destroy the Guards. Although it is a negative Elixir trade, it will have full hitpoints for a counterattack.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a>'s attack can bypass the shields allowing him to deal with the Guards easily. His first attack will be able to fully destroy 2 Guards, and the next one will finish off the last Guard. After that, he will be ready for a counterattack.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Guards cannot target air units, so cheap air troops like <a href=\"/wiki/Bats\" title=\"Bats\">Bats</a> or Minions can quickly defeat them. The Baby Dragon and Skeleton Dragons are especially effective due to being able to hit multiple Guards at once.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Fire_Spirit\" title=\"Fire Spirit\">Fire Spirit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Spirit\" title=\"Ice Spirit\">Ice Spirit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Heal_Spirit\" title=\"Heal Spirit\">Heal Spirit</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Spirit\" title=\"Electro Spirit\">Electro Spirit</a> do not deal enough damage to take out the Guards' shields but they do deal enough damage to finish off the Guards themselves. Knowing this, you can combine either of them with The Log to fully counter the Guards for a neutral Elixir trade, however that requires precise timing.\n<ul><li>Ice Spirit can be used to counter them, freezing them and allowing your Crown Tower to finish them off.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Guards are great for defending against the <a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a>, since they are more durable than regular Skeletons and have a longer range. For similar reasons, they are also effective at countering the Miner, capable of tanking multiple hits from him.</li>\n<li>The Guards can finish off a weakened <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golem</a> reliably. Their shields will allow them survive the Golem's death damage, and they will quickly destroy the Golemites. However, they will likely be defeated rather quickly if the Golem is supported by other cards, and the Guards will be destroyed entirely after dealing with the Golemites.</li>\n<li>The Guards are a great support card for an <a href=\"/wiki/X-Bow\" title=\"X-Bow\">X-Bow</a> or a <a href=\"/wiki/Mortar\" title=\"Mortar\">Mortar</a>. Their shields allow them to distract enemy troops long enough, buying for time for the siege building to deal damage to its target.</li>\n<li>When the <a href=\"/wiki/Three_Musketeers\" title=\"Three Musketeers\">Three Musketeers</a> are coming to a Crown Tower without support, once the player's Crown Tower targets the Three Musketeers, they can do a quick drop of Guards and then Ice Spirit. The first Guard can negate three simultaneous hits and then the Ice Spirit will freeze them for 1.2 seconds which is enough time for the 2 remaining Guards and Crown Tower to finish off the Musketeers.</li>\n<li>An very powerful defensive combination is the Guards paired with the <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Wizard\" title=\"Ice Wizard\">Ice Wizard</a>. The Ice Wizard slows down enemy troops while chipping them down, while the Guards distract and damage the troops, benefitting immensely from the Ice Wizard\u2019s slow improving their survivability. This combo can be further enhanced by high-DPS ranged units like the <a href=\"/wiki/Cannon\" title=\"Cannon\">Cannon</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a>, who will appreciate the amount of free time generated from the combo.</li>\n<li>Guards are actually able to slightly outrange some troops&#160;because of their spears, similar to the Prince. Because of this, they can poke at a distracted Valkyrie without taking any damage, setting you up for a counterpush.</li>\n<li>The Guards' shields allows them to be a great meatshield against an <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Tower\" title=\"Inferno Tower\">Inferno Tower</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Dragon\" title=\"Inferno Dragon\">Inferno Dragon</a>, or a <a href=\"/wiki/Mighty_Miner\" title=\"Mighty Miner\">Mighty Miner</a>. These three cards will reset their charge after they destroy a shield, being distracted for longer.</li>\n<li>Depending on the player's needs in a <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">deck</a>, the Guards can be replaced with other similar cards.\n<ul><li>The Guards are a good alternative to Barbarians if the player needs to keep their deck cheap as they cost 2 less Elixir. The trade-off is that the Guards have all-around lower statistics except for speed.</li>\n<li>For a lower Elixir cost, Skeletons and <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a> can work as a replacement for Guards, with the latter also being better than Guards offensively due to their higher movement speed. However, the lack of shields on both units makes them worse defensively, especially against spells.</li>\n<li><a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Army\" title=\"Skeleton Army\">Skeleton Army</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Gang\" title=\"Goblin Gang\">Goblin Gang</a> offer significantly higher damage compared to Guards for the same Elixir cost. However, much like the previous cards, they are easier to counter with spells, due to their lack of shields.</li>\n<li>If the player can afford to defend without a counterpush afterwards, they can use the <a href=\"/wiki/Tombstone\" title=\"Tombstone\">Tombstone</a> as a replacemet for the Guards for the same Elixir cost. While weak to spells, it can also distract building-targeting troops like the <a href=\"/wiki/Giant\" title=\"Giant\">Giant</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ram_Rider\" title=\"Ram Rider\">Ram Rider</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Wall_Breakers\" title=\"Wall Breakers\">Wall Breakers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Golem\" title=\"Elixir Golem\">Elixir Golem</a>, and Hog Rider.</li></ul></li></ul>\n<center>\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\">\n<h2 style=\"display: block; text-align: center; font-size: 1.6rem; line-height: 1.6rem;
