It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, ground [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] with moderate hitpoints and low damage, and a single target, building-targeting, melee, ground troop with high hitpoints and low damage. The Goblinstein’s attacks will stun a targeted unit for 0.5 seconds. Being a Champion card, they will stay out of the player's card cycle, and only return once they are defeated. The Goblinstein's ability has him shoot an electric spark towards an antenna that is typically located on the Monster's head. The Goblinstein Doctor resembles a [[Goblins|Goblin]] with white hair, wearing a silver-rimmed team-colored goggles with glowing orange lens, a satellite dish on his head with a team-colored strap, a white coat, tan gloves, and brown boots, and wields a controller. The Monster is a large, green Goblin with a square head with an antenna on top, with some of its body being darker and red and having gold stitches and bolts on them, and wears team-colored pants. A Goblinstein card costs 5 [[Elixir]] to deploy and his ability costs an additional 2 Elixir to activate.\n\n==Ability: Lightning Link==\n{{Quote|Electrifies the link, damaging any enemies that get near. Shocking!}}\n{{Champion Ability}}\nThe Goblinstein has a special ability, accessible from the rightmost side of the screen just above the player's [[Cards|card]] slots in [[Basics of Battle|battle]]. After a 1-second delay, the Goblinstein activates his “Lightning Link”, creating an electric link between him and Monster. It damages by shocking nearby targets every 0.5 seconds up to 2 tiles away. This ability deals reduced damage to the [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|Crown Tower]]. If the Monster has already been defeated, it will drop an antenna on the ground which is what the Goblinstein's electric link will target when the ability is activated. The Lightning Link ability costs 2 [[Elixir]] to activate. \n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Goblinstein Card}}\n*For the purposes of determining [[Elixir]] trades, the Goblinstein and his activated Ability count as 7 Elixir, as the player must play the 5 Elixir Goblinstein and then spend a further 2 on his Ability.\n*The Doctor always spawns 1 tile in the opposite direction of the side of the [[Arenas|Arena]] in which the Monster is placed in. This allows for the Doctor and the Monster to be split into both lanes if played in the middle.\n*The Doctor can stun units like the [[Miner]] to get him to retarget to the Monster, saving hp on the tower.\n*Since the Doctor is the specific Champion, killing him would remove the 3 card cycle, and the ability.\n**However, when the Monster is defeated, an antenna is dropped in the Arena, what will be connected if the ability is used. This allows the ability to be used after the Monster is initially defeated.\n*Similarly to cards with area damage, the Lightning Link ability can hit infinite targets, and will hit everything within a 2 tile radius. Thus, fragile swarms like [[Skeleton Army]], [[Minion Horde]], [[Bats/Evolution|Evolved Bats]] and [[Goblin Gang]] will be quickly wiped out if they come within its range. While bulkier swarms such as [[Barbarians/Evolution|Evolved Barbarians]], [[Royal Recruits/Evolution|Evolved Royal Recruits]], [[Three Musketeers]], [[Zappies]] and [[Royal Hogs]] can withstand a few hits before they are completely obliterated.\n*The [[Fisherman]] and [[Tornado]] can be a good way to puling the Monster quickly away, so the ability won't get too much damage on for example, the tower.\n*The Goblinstein can be placed as a defender in [[Clan Wars|Boat Battles]]. However, much like other {{Rarity|Champion}} cards, he is unable to use his ability.
