Card Evolution
6
SAVE
EDIT

Card Evolution is a feature that allows specific Cards placed in the Evolution slots to become more powerful. Card Evolution and the Evolution slot are both unlocked at Arena 3, with the Wild slot, that lets you choose if you want to activate an Evolution, or a Hero version of a Card at Arena 10. A card evolves after a certain number of Cycles. Cycles are the amount of times the original card must be deployed into the Arena to obtain the Card Evolution. For Instance, if a card requires 2 Cycles to evolve, every 3rd of that card would be its Card Evolution. This can be tracked as once you deploy the card, a diamond above the card art will be filled in the next time it comes into your hand. Every card with an Evolution takes 6 Evolution Shards or Wild Shards to be used in battle. When a Card Evolution is deployed, a special deploy sound will play while a purple light temporarily shines down on them. When you obtain a Card Evolution in a match, a guitar riff will play. All Card Evolutions have a special ability that the original card doesn't have. In addition to the ability, some Card Evolutions have increased stats to compensate for their appearance in battle.

Obtaining Evolutions
Evolution Shards

Evolution Shards can be obtained from Shop offers, Community Events, Special Challenges, Pass Royale, 5-Star Lucky Chests, Pass Royale, Evolution Boxes, and every other Level Up Chest past King Level 50. 5-Star Lucky Chests have a 4.3% chance to drop a full Evolution, while events contain 3 Evolution Shards of an Evolution that appeared four months prior, and Pass royale unlocks the current season's Evolution in the paid side of tier 1. If you obtain an Evolution Shard via Shop Offers, Pass Royale or Community Events for an Evolution you already have, they'll convert into a significant amount of extra cards for the regular form of the Evolution. This amount varies based on the card’s rarity, with the conversion rates being 1,000 Common cards, 250 Rare cards, 40 Epic cards, and 2 Legendary cards. If an Evolution Shard is obtained for a card that the player does not own, the card is unlocked for them automatically.

Wild Shards

Wild Shards can be obtained from Shop Offers, Special Challenges, Pass Royale, and 4-Star Lucky Chests. The Pass Royale will always contain 2 Wild Shards and 4-Star Lucky Drops have a 3% chance to drop one. Unlike Evolution Shards, Wild Shards are capable of being used on any card with an Evolution, and go beyond the player's limit.

Trivia
The number of possible decks that can be created with 2 evolutions is 28, assuming that all 8 cards in the deck can evolve.
The Ice Spirit and Bats are the only evolutions without a purple diamond being represented on their card renders.
The Battle Ram is the only evolution to spawn another evolved troop as a part of its mechanics.
The Skeletons and Ice Spirit are the cheapest evolutions and require the least amount Elixir to fully cycle, costing 1 Elixir alone and 3 Elixir when fully cycled (excluding Elixir spent from playing other cards).
The P.E.K.K.A., Royal Recruits, and Mega Knight are the costliest evolutions, costing 7 Elixir alone.
The Royal Hogs require the most amount of Elixir to fully cycle, costing 15 Elixir when fully cycled (excluding Elixir spent from playing other cards).
The Barbarians were the first evolution to be fully revealed.
Some troops and buildings resemble Super Troops from Clash of Clans. More specifically:
The Evolved Barbarians resemble the Super Barbarian.
The Evolved Cannon resembles the Eagle Artillery.
The Evolved Valkyrie resembles the Super Valkyrie.
The Evolved Witch resembles the Super Witch.
The Evolved Wizard resembles the Super Wizard.
The Evolved Bomber's ability is the same mechanic as the one used by the Bomber from the Clash of Clans Builder Base.
The P.E.K.K.A. and Mega Knight's abilities resemble old upgrades from Clash Mini.
The Elite Barbarians' abilities are based on the the common modifier in C.H.A.O.S..
Card Evolutions do not have alternative skins that take Star Points. This can be seen with the Evolved Skeleton Barrel; the Skeletons spawned on death will not show a Star Level.
All Card Evolutions are 3-D models when deployed into the arena. This is different from the 2-D sprites used in their regular forms.
The only exception to this rule is the Ice Spirit, which re-uses its base form's sprite, except that it now has a purple tint.
