“All fired up! When it’s hot and attacking, Evolved Furnace summons Fire Spirits even faster from hat to hearth.”

The Furnace's Evolution is a Rare card that is unlocked with 6 Evolution Shards. It spawns a Furnace, with identical stats to the original. When the Furnace attacks, the spawn speed is decreased to 2.4sec, as well as spawn Fire Spirits to the side, instead of the front. It appears to be an amethyst cast-iron steam locomotive armor with golden spikes, limbs, and a Card Evolution symbol in the center, wearing purple gloves ane cape with golden linings, team-colored chainmail, a cracked metal witch-like hat on top with a golden band in the middle, and a golden grate in the center. It also holds a wooden staff with a cauldron placed on top that has a golden handle filled with bubbling magenta fluid in it, which resembles Elixir, along with a large amethyst knob on the bottom with golden spikes on it. The Furnace's Evolution costs 4 Elixir and requires 2 cycles to deploy.

Strategy
Make sure that the Furnace attacks as much as possible, so that it spawns as many as possible.
The Fire Spirit from "Hot Spawns" ability spawn first to the left, then to the right, then to the left again, and so on.
This can be used to lure units from the other side of the arena.
The Rage works really well, to spawn even more Fire Spirits.

History

2025

The Furnace Evolution card was added to the game on 4/8/2025, in the August 2025 Update
On 7/8/2025, a maintenance break reduced the Furnace's hitpoints by 19% and increased the spawn time for its Fire Spirits to 7 seconds (from 5 seconds).
On 1/9/2025, a Balance Update, increased the Evolved Furnace's Hot Spawn Fire Spirit Spawn Period to 2.4 seconds (from 1.8 seconds).
On 6/10/2025, a Balance Update increased Furnace's Movement Speed to Medium (from Slow).

2026

On 12/1/2026, a Balance Update increased Furnace's attack speed to 1.7 (from 1.8)
On 1/6/2026, a Balance Update, decreased the Furnace's range to 5.5 tiles (from 6 tiles)
On 4/8/2026, a Balance Update, decreased the Furnace's spawn duration to 5 seconds (from 7 seconds)

Trivia
In the German, Turkish, Japanese and Finnish language settings of the game, the Furnace's name translates to "Oven". In the Norwegian, Korean, and Chinese language settings of the game, the Furnace's name translates to "Fireplace", "Blast Furnace", and "Furnace of Flames" respectively.
The Furnace Evolution is the subject of multiple Emotes.
This Evolution was obtainable for free during New Year's.

A card or a feature with a + next to it denotes something that is only available in temporary challenges (has a timer on it, and the event may still come back every once in a while). Merge Tactics cards are purple, while Evolutions are pink, and Heroes are orange.
