{"parse":{"title":"Princess Towers","pageid":281,"wikitext":"{{Protection}}{{MagicWords}}{{RemovedContent}}\n[[File:Princesstower.png|center|x200px]]\n\n==General Information==\n*The Princess Tower is a ranged [[:Category:Crown Towers|Tower]] that attacks opposing [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]] and can attack both air and ground units.\n**Princess Towers are classified as [[:Category:Building Cards|buildings]] and because of this, they will be directly targeted by troops like [[Giant]]s, [[Hog Rider]]s, [[Golem]]s, and [[Wall Breakers]].\n**Each player has a pair of 3×3 size Princess Towers on their respective side of the map, one on the left and one on the right of the [[King's Tower]].\n*Destroying an opposing Princess Tower awards a player a Crown and grants the player additional territory across the river (commonly called a “pocket”) where [[cards]] can be cast upon (the [[Royal Recruits]] card needs both Princess Towers to be destroyed to be deployed in the pocket)\n*There are certain [[cards]] that will deal reduced damage to Crown Towers. More specifically:\n**[[Arrows]], [[Zap]], [[Giant Snowball]], [[Fireball]], [[Lightning]], [[Freeze]], [[Poison]], and [[Rage]] will deal 30% of their full damage.\n***Due to spawning a Rage after death, the [[Lumberjack]]'s death Rage will also deal 30% of its full damage Crown Towers.\n***[[Goblin Drill]] will also deal 30% of their full damage to Crown Towers.\n***The [[Phoenix]]'s Death Damage and the [[Firecracker/Evolution|Evolved Firecracker]]'s Spark Damage will also deal 30% of their full damage to Crown Towers.\n**Rocket will deal 25% of its full damage.\n***[[Miner]] will also deal 25% of his full damage to Crown Towers.\n**[[Tornado]] will deal 35% of its full damage.\n**[[The Log]] will deal 20% of its full damage.\n**[[Earthquake]] will deal 65% of its full troop damage.\n**The [[Electro Giant]]'s reflected damage will deal 66% of its full damage.\n**On the contrary, the [[Giant Skeleton]]'s death bomb will deal double damage to Crown Towers.\n*The Princess Tower's hitpoints and damage are increased by roughly +8% per level when upgrading from level 1 to level 9 and roughly +10% per level from level 9 to level 13.\n**Since cards' damage, hitpoints and healing increase by +10% per level, max level gameplay is practically identical to tournament standard gameplay. At lower levels, Princess Towers are slightly stronger compared to cards.\n** For example, a Princess Tower will two-shot a certain same level [[Fire Spirit]] at Level 8 or below, but not at Level 9 or above. Also, a Level 3 [[Rocket]] will take 17.2% of the health of a Level 3 Princess Tower while a maxed Rocket will take 19.5% of the health of a maxed Princess Tower.\n*The Princess Towers' attack speed can be increased by a Rage [[:Category:Spell Cards|spell]] like all other offensive buildings and troops.\n*The Princess Towers have an attack range that is 0.5 tiles longer and a hit speed that is 0.2 seconds faster than the King's Tower, however, their projectiles are slightly slower.\n\n==History==\n2016\n*The Arena Towers were added with Clash Royale's game launch on 4/1/2016.\n*On 3/5/2016, the [[Version History#May 2016 Update (3/5/2016)|May 2016 Update]], increased the Arena Tower Level cap to 13 (from 12) and made it so that the Arena Tower's hitpoints and damage would increase by 10% with each Level at Levels 9-12 (from 8%). The update also decreased the Arena Tower's range to 7.5 tiles (from 8.5 tiles), but its effective range was unchanged.\n*On 1/11/2016, the [[Version History#November 2016 Update (1/11/2016)|November 2016 Update]], slightly modified the shooting animation of the [[Princess]] atop the Princess Tower.
