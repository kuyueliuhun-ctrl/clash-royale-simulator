It spawns a Dart Goblin with identical stats to the original. When attacking a target, he will leave an area of effect poison, becoming stronger after multiple attacks. He resembles a [[Goblins|Goblin]], but wears a voodoo mask with purple eyes, team-colored face paint and a rope behind, leaves with circles and purple tips, and a [[Card Evolution]] symbol on top, purple pants, team-colored bands on his arm, and brown shoes, carries a purple potion, and holds a blowgun with purple paint. The Dart Goblin's Evolution costs 3 [[Elixir]] and requires 2 cycles to deploy.\n\n==Strategy==\n*Even if the Evolved Dart Goblin is defeated, the poison will stay until that unit is defeated. So shooting high-hitpoint units like the [[Golem]], [[Giant]], [[P.E.K.K.A.|P.E.K.K.A]], [[Giant Skeleton]], [[Electro Giant]], [[Mega Knight]], [[Goblin Giant]], [[Royal Giant]], or [[Lava Hound]] will make sure that a lot of poison will be made for a long time.\n*Against Crown Towers, the poison will only remain for 1 second, and he will only deal 25% of its damage.\n*Troops that spawn other troops after death will not carry over the poison to their sub troops if the parent troop is poisoned.\n*The poison effect does not stack. For example, if two Evolved Dart Goblins attack the same troop, the second one will not increase the poison damage of the first one.
