“These skyward skellies were never satisfied while six feet under! Aeri and her skeleton crew are known for their semi-well-crafted wings which allow them to dive-bomb without breaking any bones.”

The Heroic Balloon is a Epic card that is unlocked with 200 . It spawns a single-target, building-targeting, melee, air troop with high hitpoints and very high damage. When defeated, it drops a bomb which explodes after 3 seconds, dealing moderate damage. It is a hot air balloon with a team-colored envelope and a wooden basket with sacks held together by patches and ropes, and is piloted by a Bomber. A Heroic Balloon card costs 5  to deploy.

Ability: Coffin Cadets
“A Skeletrooper soars to the nearest enemy on the ground, dealing landing damage.”
File:Ability-Coffin Cadets.png

The Hero Knight has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. Once pressed, a skeletropper will fly towards the closest ground unit within 6 tiles, deling spawn damage. The skeletropper will the attack, dealing less damage to the crown tower. The Coffin Cadets ability costs 2 Elixir to activate.

Strategy
The ability can be used to kill units like Musketeer and Wizard, or buildings.
When defending, put troops like Skeletons closer, so that they can take the agro of the ability.

History

2026

On 6/4/2026, with the April 2026 Update, the Hero Balloon was added in the game, along with Evo Minion Horde.
On 1/6/2026, a Balance Update, decreased the Skeletrooper's Landing Damage by 14%.
On 6/7/2026, a Balance Update, decreased the Skeletrooper's Attack Damage by 20% and increased his hit speed to 1.1 seconds (from 0.9 seconds).
On 4/8/2026, a Balance Update, made the ability single use (from 12sec cooldown).

Trivia
The first use of hot air balloons in warfare was in 1849, where the Austrian imperial forces besieging Venice attempted to float some 200 paper hot air balloons each carrying an 11–14 kg bomb that was to be dropped from the balloon with a time fuse over the besieged city. Before that, Balloons were used in wars exclusive for reconnaissance purposes, the first instance being in the Battle of Fleurus in 1794, used by the French against the Austrians.
In the Italian, Finnish, and Vietnamese language settings of the game, the Balloon's name translates to "Hot Air Balloon". In the Spanish, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese language settings of the game, the Balloon's name translates to "Bombastic Balloon", "Air Balloon", "Skull Airship", and "Balloon Soldier" respectively.
