“They see him rollin', they hatin'! No one clears the path like Hero Barbarian Barrel, who willingly gave up his rock music career for the resilience of a second roll.”

The Heroic Barbarian is a Epic card that is unlocked with 200 . It is an area-damage, ground-targeting spell with a short rectangular area and moderate damage. It can only be deployed on the player's own side. Once the spell reaches its destination, it spawns a single Barbarian. It is a spiked barrel with a Barbarian inside it.A Heroic Barbarian Barrel card costs 2  to deploy.

Ability: Rowdy Reroll
“Barrels down the lane a second time for maximum impact.”
Rowdy Rerollpx

The Hero Knight has a special ability accessible from whatever side of the screen was put in, just above the player's card slots in battle. When activatet, the Barbarian Barrel will roll for a second time, while healling the barbarian for 50% of the damage. The Rowdy Reroll ability costs 1 Elixir to activate.

Strategy
The ability can get the barbarian closer to the crown tower while healing some hp, forcing more elixir on defense.
The roll can also kill more swarms, or get behind troops.

History
On 2/3/2026, along with the March 2026 Update the Hero Barbarian Barrel was added into the game, along with Hero Magic Archer.
On 4/5/2026, a Balance Update, decreased the reroll range to 3 tiles (from 4 tiles).
On 4/8/2026, a Balance Update increased the barbarian's hitpoint by 4%.

Trivia
This is the only hero, that is a spell
The word "Barbarian" comes from the Greek "βάρβαρος" (bárbaros), meaning foreign. Dating back to the ancient Greek times, the Greeks referred to non-Greek people as barbarians because the language they spoke sounded to Greeks like gibberish represented by the sounds "bar..bar..;".
In the Japanese and Thai language setting of the game, the Barbarian Barrel's name translates to "Rolling Barbarian" and "Barbarian Tank" respectively.
The Barbarian Barrel is 1 of 6 cards associated with Barbarians, with the others being Elite Barbarians, Barbarian Launcher, Battle Ram, Barbarian Hut, and of course, Barbarians.
It is the only Barbarian-related card to be Epic.
It is the only Barbarian-related card to be a spell.
It is the only Barbarian-related card to spawn only 1 Barbarian instead of more than 1.
According to a Clash-A-Rama comic strip, the Barbarians follow a career path, with the one in the Barbarian Barrel being the lowest-ranking one.
