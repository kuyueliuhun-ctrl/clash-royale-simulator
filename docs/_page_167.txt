{"parse":{"title":"Basics of Battle","pageid":316,"wikitext":"{{Protection}}{{MagicWords}}\n{{CardImg|[[File:BasicsOfBattle.png|500px|center|link=]]}}\n\n==Summary==\n*Players win a battle by obtaining more Crowns than the enemy. This is done by destroying the opponent's [[:Category:Crown Towers|Crown Towers]].\n*If the player destroys the opponent's [[King's Tower]], they get 3 Crowns, as the other 2 Crown Towers will be automatically destroyed as well, resulting in an automatic win.\n*The battle ends when:\n**A player gets 3 Crowns. This can be done instantly by destroying the opponent's King's Tower.\n**A player has more Crowns than their opponent at the end of the normal 3 minute period.\n**A Crown Tower is destroyed in Overtime (with certain exceptions [[#Winning|below]]).\n**The time limit (Battle and Overtime) runs out, and none of the above has occurred. This results in a tiebreaker. A tiebreaker is when all Crown Towers rapidly lose health until the one with the lowest health is destroyed.\n***If both players' towers have identical health, the tiebreaker would be skipped and the match would be counted as a draw. In this condition, neither side wins or loses trophies.\n***Once a single [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|Crown Tower]] is destroyed, the game ends with the player who has the most Crowns as the winner.\n***In [[Clan Wars]], if a Duel ends in a stalemate (both players have the same amount of Tower hitpoints after a tiebreaker), a coin flip will then decide the winner.\n*Winning multiplayer battles earns the player [[Trophies]], while losing battles loses trophies. Trophies allow the player to unlock new [[Arenas]] and reach higher Leagues.\n**New Arenas unlock new [[Cards]] and have increasing [[Chests|Chest]] and Trophy Road rewards, Victory Gold, and [[Clans|Donation]] & request limits.\n**In alternative Trophy Roads, other kind of trophies can grant other Trophy Road rewards.\n**In [[Tournament|Victory Challenges]], Global Tournaments and [[Tournament/Special Event Challenges|Special Event Challenges]], winning a certain number of times can also allow players to obtain one-time rewards. Challenges have a maximum limit of victories that can be achieved, and if reached, the challenge is completed, while Global Tournaments continue infinitely.\n\n==Battle==\n*Players square off in live competition on maps dotted by 6 [[:Category:Building Cards|buildings]]; 3 for each player: 2 [[Princess Towers|Crown Towers]] and 1 [[King's Tower]].\n*The objective of the battle is for the player to destroy the other player’s Crown Towers (the large towers with Crowns on them) while protecting their own.\n*A player may do this by deploying [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]], buildings, and [[:Category:Spell Cards|spells]] from a [[Battle Decks|deck]] of 8 [[Cards]] (drawn from a Battle Deck that they've created or drafted in [[Tournament/Special Event Challenges|Draft]] [[:Category:Modes|modes]]) anywhere within an allowable area on the field in their territory.\n**The player's territory is the non-red space on the map shown when hovering a card onto the arena, which is almost always the bottom half of the screen stopping at the bridge (most spells and certain Cards are exempt from this).\n**In the event a player takes down an enemy's Crown Tower, a small rectangle of territory on the opponent's annexed side beyond the bridge becomes available for the player to deploy their [[:Category:Cards|Cards]] on. However, the opponent can still play Cards in captured areas.\n***[[Royal Recruits]] require both opposing Crown Towers to be destroyed in order to be placed on the opponent's side.\n*More Cards are collected by unlocking [[Chests]] won in battle or bought in the [[Shop]], which in turn will unlock new Cards that the player can add to their Battle Deck and/or level up the Cards they already have.\n*Each card requires a certain amount of [[Elixir]] to deploy, but the player's Elixir regenerates, so they are only moments away from deploying the next troop or spell.\n*While battling, the player can communicate through [[Emotes]], small messages that can be accessed by the speech bubble icon next to the deck. These include text messages like \"Thanks!\" or \"Wow!\", and emoji-like pictures such as a raging or laughing King face. Next to the Emotes, there is also a button to mute them. Muting emotes will prevent the player from seeing any Emotes that their opponent or spectators send, but they will also not be able to send any Emotes back unless the player unmutes them.\n*Using [[Elixir]] wisely is key to success. When a player has more Elixir than the other, this is called an \"Elixir advantage\". Having this advantage will give the player more flexibility when attacking or defending. However, this advantage can shift very quickly if the advantaged player \"overcommits\" on Elixir and allows the disadvantaged player to defend for a \"positive Elixir trade\" by using up more Elixir to attack than their opponent spends to defend with.\n**Assuming perfect play, the player defending will almost ''always'' have an advantage with Elixir trades because their Crown Towers are helping them defend for free. To get around this, one can either build up a push so strong their opponent can't defend no matter what they play, or play in such a way that their opponent doesn't have any counters to defend with in their current hand when the player starts attacking. Nevertheless, it's usually better to have a positive Elixir trade first before deciding to go on offense (with plenty of exceptions).\n\n==Battle Deck==\n*The [[Battle Decks|Battle Deck]] is the rotation of [[Cards]] the player has in battle.[[File:A Battle Deck.jpg|thumb|220x220px]]\n*To change these, the player must tap the Card they wish to add into their Battle Deck and tap, 'Use', before tapping the Card that the player would like removed from their Battle Deck. After tapping 'Use', they can also rearrange the Cards in the Deck by dragging them around like apps on a home screen. This can be used to arrange Cards by rarity or [[Elixir]] cost if one wishes so.\n*Under the cards in the Deck, it will display the average Elixir cost of all the cards. Occasionally, this can come in handy when deciding if a Deck is suitable. For example, a Deck with an average of 3.5 Elixir that is semi-powerful will allow for the deployment of cards more often to counter enemy offences and proceed with an offensive strategy, as compared to a Deck with an average cost of 4.7 Elixir. However, the 3.5 Deck may be less powerful than the 4.7 Deck. A Deck with an average cost of below 3.5 Elixir is a very flexible deck; the player will need more planning.\n*The Battle Deck consists of 8 Cards, so choose only the ones that will be used, otherwise the redundant Card will waste a Card slot. Be sure to choose Cards that can work together and will cover each other's weaknesses, like including powerful single-target units like the [[Prince]], [[Mighty Miner]], [[P.E.K.K.A.|P.E.K.K.A]] and [[Mini P.E.K.K.A]], area damage Cards like the [[Bomber]], [[Wizard]], [[Executioner]] and [[Bowler]], and air-attacking [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]] like the [[Musketeer]], [[Hunter]], [[Dart Goblin]] and [[Magic Archer]].\n*Each Battle Deck also has a 9th Card on it. This slot is reserved for the Tower Troop that is being used. The two Crown Towers will use the selected Tower Troop and inherit its stats. Be sure to use a Tower Troop whose weaknesses are covered by the remaining Cards, such as the single-target [[Cannoneer]] being used with multiple area damage Cards.\n*The first Card in a Battle Deck will be able to evolve in battle, provided that the player has collected all Evolution Shards required to unlock them.\n*The second Cards in a Battle Deck will be able to turn heroic in battle, provided that the player has collected all Hero Shards required to unlock them.\n*The third Card in a Battle Deck is a Wild Card, which means that it can be either able to evolve or turn heroic in battle. If it is a card that can have both, then the player can select whether they want to use its evolved or heroic counterpart.\n*In a Battle Deck, up to 2 {{Rarity|Champion}} cards can be used at a time. They can only be used in the Heroic and Wild Card slots.\n*In battle, 4 of the 8 Cards in the player's Battle Deck will be available to play in most cases. The first 4 Cards will be randomly selected at the start of the battle. In addition to the 4 Cards that are readily available to play, there is a fifth Card in the \"Next\" slot. Once the player has played a Card, the card in the \"Next\" slot will replace the Card that was just played. In turn, another Card will take the place in the \"Next\" slot. The player should consider not only the 4 Cards in their hand but also the 5th Card in the \"Next\" slot when planning an attack.\n**An exception to this are the [[Elixir Collector]] and [[Mirror]] cards, which will never appear at the start of the battle.\n*If multiple Cards are played in quick succession, new Cards won't immediately fill the hand. When a new card enters the \"Next\" slot, it will have a 2-second cool down before it can enter the hand (this cooldown is reduced to 1 second when Elixir production is doubled).\n*Once a Card is played, it will not reappear until 4 Cards from the player's Battle Deck have been drawn. In practice, this means that the player will redraw each Card after playing exactly any 3 other Cards after it. Some Decks carry many low-cost Cards in order to rotate to others more quickly, giving them more control over the tempo of a match when used correctly.\n*Cards with an evolution will spawn their evolved counterparts after a certain cycles threshold is met. One cycle is gained for every time the regular card is played. Once the threshold is fulfilled, the evolved card can be played on the field instead of its regular counterpart. Using Mirror on the regular card has no effect on the cycles.\n*Multiple {{Rarity|Champion}} or Heroic cards can be deployed on the match. Both of them can use their abilities, but only one of each can use them in case the player deploys them again. Deploying a {{Rarity|Champion}} or Heroic card while the previous one's ability is on cooldown will refresh the timer.\n*The player can save up to 3 Battle Decks using the numbered buttons in the top right corner of the battle deck interface, up to 5 if they are Level 7 or above, and up to 10 if they are Level 17 or above. The selected Deck is the one that will be taken into battle, so check it out before starting a match.\n\n==Combat and Damage==\n*[[:Category:Troop Cards|Troops]] will automatically engage the nearest enemy of their target category if any such enemies are in its sight range. The sight range varies by troop and is not revealed in the troop's official stats. Once engaged, the troop will automatically follow and attack that target as long as possible. If no target is found, they will advance towards the nearest enemy Crown Tower.\n*[[:Category:Building Cards|Buildings]] function just like troops do, but they are stationary and have a lifetime. This means they will gradually lose health after their deploy time is finished, even when not being attacked.\n*If a card does Area Damage, it can affect multiple targets at once. \"Point\" damage (known simply as \"Damage\" in the card's info screen) is dealt to a single target. Other forms of damage are also available, such as Chain Damage, which is dealt to a single target, but will hit additional ones afterwards.\n*Any negative effect the player deals (damage, slowing down, immobilization, pushback, dragging in) will not affect their own troops or buildings and will affect both opponents in [[2v2]] mode. Because of this, firing ranged weapons, bombs, or spells into a melee fight can be a very powerful and effective strategy. Similarly, any positive effect the player deals (enraging, cloning, healing) will not affect the opponent's troops or buildings and will affect their teammate's troops or buildings as well.\n*It is entirely possible that two opposing troops defeat each other simultaneously. This usually happens when two of the same troop, both with the same level, encounter each other.\n*Some spells deal damage when they are played. They deal reduced damage to Crown Towers, and the amount of damage depends on the spell. The [[Miner]] also deals reduced damage to Crown Towers and is the only troop that does so. Similarly, the [[Goblin Drill]] deals reduced damage to Crown Towers and is the only building that does so. Certain cards like the [[Electro Giant]] and [[Monk]] will also do reduced damage to Crown Towers with their alternative forms of damage, but their regular attacks will still deal full damage.\n*When playing a spell, the player must aim it correctly. Most spells have a cast time before they hit, so hitting moving targets can get tricky.\n*Crown Towers will attack enemies; the area they cover is different depending on where they are in the Arena. This gives the defender an additional edge in the fighting.\n**The Crown Towers have ranges that extend up to a point just before the river in the middle of the Arena. They are active from the start of the match until they are destroyed.\n**Depending on how the player wants their Crown Towers to attack, they may set it to have a specific tower troop in their deck.\n**The player's [[King's Tower|King Tower]] will also attack enemies, but only after it is damaged, or a Crown Tower is destroyed. It takes several seconds for the King to pull out his cannon and start shooting. Despite having a cannon, it has shorter range than the Crown Towers, but more hitpoints.\n\n==Winning==\n*In order to win, the player must destroy more Crown Towers than their opponent.\n*If the opponent's [[King's Tower|King Tower]] is destroyed, the player wins automatically.\n**It is entirely possible, but very rare, for the player and the opponent to destroy each other's King's Tower at exactly the same time, resulting in a 3-Crown draw.\n**Usually, the player must destroy at least 1 of the Crown Towers in order to proceed to damaging the King Tower.\n***This doesn't happen if two [[:Category:Building Cards|buildings]] are placed in front of the King's Tower, as a building targeting troop such as a [[Balloon]] will target the King's Tower after destroying these two buildings. This puts the opponent at a disadvantage as the Death Damage of a [[Balloon]] or a hit landed by a [[Hog Rider]] can activate the King's Tower, once again giving a significant advantage.\n***The player can also directly target the King's Tower with the [[Rocket]] (or any other [[:Category:Spell Cards|spell]] that is able to deal damage to the King Tower, or with a [[Miner]] or [[Goblin Drill]]), though this usually won't destroy it. The spells do reduced damage to Crown Towers.\n*The game will continue for 3 minutes. When the timer reaches 0, then the player who has destroyed more Crown Towers wins. Under normal circumstances, [[Elixir]] generation is doubled after the first two minutes.\n**If both players have destroyed an equal number of Crown Towers or none of the Crown Towers have been destroyed at the 3-minute mark, a two-minute overtime with sudden death starts, with the last minute using triple Elixir rate. In overtime, the first player to destroy any Tower immediately wins.\n**It sometimes (although extremely rarely) happens that both players destroy a Crown Tower at exactly the same moment in overtime. In this case, the overtime continues.\n**If the overtime also runs out, the game will end with a tiebreaker. In the tiebreaker, all troops immediately disappear from the Arena, and all towers rapidly lose health until one is destroyed, meaning that the player owning the Crown Tower with the least amount of health loses the game.\n**If both players' towers have identical health when overtime ends, the tiebreaker is skipped and the match is counted as a draw, except in [[Clan Wars]] Duels. Neither player will gain or lose any of their [[Trophies]], nor will they receive any [[Chests]] or [[Gold]] for the game. In Clan Wars Duels, a coin flip will randomly decide the winner to break this type of tie.\n*After a multiplayer battle, the Crowns earned go towards [[Pass Royale]], which needs 5, 10 or, 15 Crowns to obtain each reward.\n*The winner of a multiplayer battle will receive Trophies, Gold, and a Lucky Chest. If the player has already won 20 times throughout the course of the day, they will not receive Gold for subsequent wins until the next day. Similarly, if the player has already won 4 times throughout the course of the day, they will not receive a Lucky Chest for subsequent wins until the next day.\n**In a [[Tournament]], the winner will receive Gold, but will not receive a Lucky Chest.\n**In a Victory Challenge or [[Tournament/Special Event Challenges|Special Event Challenges]], the winner might be able to claim a one-time reward.\n**In Party Mode, the winners do not receive any Trophies, but they will still receive a Lucky Chest and Gold if applicable.\n**In Clan Wars, the winner will receive double the Gold than the loser, as well as Medals to progress in the River Race. Medals can only be gained during Battle Days, while Gold can be obtained at any day.\n\n==History==\n2016\n*{{Balance|Add}} Battles and [[Battle Decks|Decks]] have been in the game since Clash Royale's game launch on 4/1/2016.\n*{{Balance|Remove}} On 2/2/2016, the [[Version History#February 2016 Update (2/2/2016)|February 2016 Update]], removed the [[Gold]] battle cost.\n*{{Balance|Add}} On 29/2/2016, the [[Version History#March 2016 Update (29/2/2016)|March 2016 Update]], introduced the Deck Slots allowing the players to save up to 3 Decks.\n*{{Balance|Add}} On 3/5/2016, the [[Version History#May 2016 Update (3/5/2016)|May 2016 Update]], allowed users to rearrange [[cards]] in their Battle Decks by dragging them around after tapping the 'Use' button on a card not in the deck.\n*On 4/7/2016, the [[Version History#Tournaments Update (4/7/2016)|Tournaments Update]], allowed players to sort their card collection by [[Elixir]], [[Arenas|Arena]], or Rarity{{Balance|Add}} .
