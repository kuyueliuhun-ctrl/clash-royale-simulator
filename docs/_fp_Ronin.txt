{"parse":{"title":"Ronin","pageid":280603,"wikitext":"{{DISPLAYTITLE: <span class=\"text-legendary\";\">Ronin</span>}}\n{{Protection}}{{MagicWords}}{{Quote|A wandering warrior with nowhere to be and no one he can’t defeat. Ronin parries one melee attack every few seconds, reflecting even more damage than he blocked. 1v1 him, we triple hog dare you!}}{{Card Infobox|Cost=5|Rarity=Legendary|Type=Troop|Arena=Serenity Peak|ReleaseDate=6 July 2026}}The Ronin is a {{Rarity|Legendary}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from the [[Arenas|Serenity Peak (Arena 14)]]. It spawns one single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] with high hit points and high damage. The Ronin can block the attack of opposing melee troops and deal double the damage to them every 3.5 seconds. He appears as a man wearing a viridian-colored robe with a beige trim and patterns, a straw hat with a team-colored tag on the side supported by a gold ring, which covers his eyes, a team-colored sash around his waist, and flip flops, has dark blue hair and a clean-shaved beard, carries two long scabbards attached to his hips, and wields two katanas. A Ronin card costs 5 [[Elixir]] to deploy.\n\n== Strategy ==\n''For [[Battle Decks]] with this [[Cards|card]], click [[:Category:Battle Decks using the Ronin Card|here]].''\n* Thanks to the parry effect, the Ronin excels against any ground melee attacker. This makes him an effective counter to strong melee attackers, like the [[P.E.K.K.A.|P.E.K.K.A]], [[Mega Knight]], Giant Skeleton, [[Prince]], [[Mini P.E.K.K.A.|Mini P.E.K.K.A]],  and [[Boss Bandit]].\n* The parry has no effect on ranged troops, so most high damage ranged troops like the Musketeer, Bowler, Wizard, and Sparky can defeat him with little resistance. However, he can counter these units on defense if placed on top of them.\n* Since all defensive buildings are ranged, they will be able to bypass the parry of the Ronin and counter him on defense. Some like the Cannon are also a positive Elixir trade.\n* The Ronin is helpless against air troops, as neither himself nor the parry can hit them. This makes air troops like the Minions, Mega Minion, and Flying Machine good counters against him. Interestingly enough, the Bats and the Phoenix are immune to his parry, despite being melee. As such, they can counter him unharmed, and for a positive Elixir trade.\n* Due to activating every 3.5 seconds and only affecting one unit at a time, swarms are effective counters to him, making the parry less effective against them, or completely ineffective if the swarm is ranged.\n* The parry is dependent on the enemy troop's damage. Troops that attack slowly with high damage such as the [[Giant Skeleton]], P.E.K.K.A, Mini P.E.K.K.A, [[Royal Ghost]], [[Elite Barbarians]], Prince, or [[Night Witch]] are easily decimated from the parry, while rapid-hitting troops like [[Lumberjack]], [[Golden Knight]], [[Monk]], or [[Berserker]] will suffer much less, since they will damage him down more quickly, reducing the number of times he parries their attacks.\n\n<center><div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\">{{Statistics}}{{StatusEffects}}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width: 100%; text-align: center;\"\n!
