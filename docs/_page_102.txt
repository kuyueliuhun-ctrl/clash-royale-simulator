{"parse":{"title":"Ram Rider","pageid":238874,"wikitext":{"*":"{{DISPLAYTITLE: <span class=\"text-legendary\";\">Ram Rider</span>}}\n{{Protection}}{{MagicWords}}{{ClashofClansLink}}\n\n{{Card Infobox|Cost=5|Rarity=Legendary|Type=Troop|Arena=Electro Valley|ReleaseDate=24 December 2018}}\n{{Quote|Together they charge through the Arena; snaring enemies, knocking down towers... and chewing grass!?}}\n\nThe Ram Rider is a {{Rarity|Legendary}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from the [[Arenas|Electro Valley (Arena 11)]]. It spawns a single-target, building-targeting, melee, ground [[:Category:Troop Cards|troop]] with high hitpoints and high damage. After she travels 2 tiles, she will begin to charge towards her target, and deals double damage if she hits the target whilst charging. She is able to jump over the river. She carries a Rider on her back that can attack independently of the Ram. The Rider's attacks only affect troops, but she can snare them, slowing down their movement speed by 70%. The Ram Rider appears as a dark-skinned woman who has light brown eyes, black hair and eyebrows, and white spots on her face, wears a sandal dress, a team-colored belt, and a golden band on her neck and right arm, wield bolas, and rides a ram that wears a team-colored headband, has a golden piercing in its left ear, and carries a team-colored scroll on its back. A Ram Rider card costs 5 [[Elixir]] to deploy.\n\n==Modifiers==\n===[[File:Ram Rider Common Modifier.png|45x45px]] Common: CHAAAARGE!===\n{{Quote|+100% charge damage}}\nRam charge deals double the damage.\n\n===[[File:Ram Rider Rare Modifier.png|45x45px]] Rare: Tie Me Down===\n{{Quote|Hunter net attack}}\nGives the Ram Rider an [[Hunter/Evolution|Evolved Hunter]] net attack.\n\n===[[File:Ram Rider Epic Modifier.png|45x45px]] Epic: Battering Net===\n{{Quote|Battle Ram & Hunter net abilities}}\nHas both Evolved Hunter and [[Battle Ram/Evolution|Evolved Battle Ram]] attributes.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Ram Rider Card}}\n*The Ram Rider herself has a snare which reduces movement speed by 70%. It will slow wind-ups and charges such as [[Bandit]] and [[Prince]]. However, the snare does not stun, so the [[Sparky]] and [[Inferno Dragon]] will still harm her.\n**The Ram Rider's snare does not prevent [[:Category:Troop Cards|troops]] from attacking. This makes ranged troops that have slowing, stun, or knockback effects like the Ice Wizard and [[Electro Wizard]] strong counters to Ram Rider. However, short-range troops like [[Minions]] and [[Mega Minion]] will not attack the Ram Rider until she runs into range, or until they slowly reach her. Snared melee troops can still attack her, although she can easily run past them after charging. This can be prevented by placing the troop near the Ram Rider's destination.\n***The snare does not affect attack speed like the [[Ice Wizard]]'s attack, so if she is in the range of an enemy unit, they will attack at full speed. It also does not affect the spawn speed of the [[Witch]] and [[Night Witch]].\n***When using the [[Hunter]] to counter the Ram Rider, he should be placed similar to a melee troop. This will prevent more damage to the [[:Category:Tower Troop Cards|Crown Tower]] than if he was placed like a ranged troop.\n**It also does not completely stop units from moving, so melee units with a high movement speed like the [[Lumberjack]], [[Mini P.E.K.K.A.|Mini P.E.K.K.A]], [[Prince]], [[Berserker]], or the [[Fire Spirit]] can still inch closer to her as she attacks.\n*A Ram Rider is extremely good at kiting troops. Wait for the enemy to cross the bridge and place the Ram Rider near the river. The Ram Rider will jump across the river, pulling the enemy while snaring them. The Crown Tower will also chip away at the enemy while it is snared.\n**Against [[Elite Barbarians]], it is best to kite them without her jumping over the river. This will keep her in range for longer, doing more damage and snaring them for longer. This is enough to stop them for a +1 [[Elixir]] trade.\n*Just like any other ranged unit, the Ram Rider will target the nearest valid target. However, she is unique in that she will prioritize non-snared troops over snared ones; even if they are on low health.\n*Due to the Ram Rider's 5.5 tile range, any ranged troop with a higher range than that can easily attack without being attacked in return. Try to use such ranged units from a high distance so that they take no damage from her, while attacking her, saving all of their hitpoints for a counterpush.\n*The Ram Rider can easily shut down slow, tanky troops such as [[P.E.K.K.A.|P.E.K.K.A]], [[Mighty Miner]], [[Valkyrie]], [[Skeleton King]], and [[Giant Skeleton]]. The Rider snares them, rendering them almost completely immobile. This also works very well when these troops are placed in the back, as they will not be able to reach her in time to defend against her.\n*[[:Category:Building Cards|Buildings]] are a great way to stop the Ram Rider from charging. With the help of the Crown Towers, they can stop her for a positive or neutral Elixir trade.\n**Active buildings should be placed in a similar way to how they are used against other building-targeting troops. This will allow them to utilize their ranged attacks, as well as lure the Ram Rider deep enough for the second Crown Tower to attack her. The Furnace and Goblin Hut can be placed in a similar way, due to them spawning ranged troops that would still be in range even if she snares her way past them.\n**Passive buildings that spawn melee troops like the [[Tombstone]] and [[Goblin Cage]] are better to be placed in front of the Crown Tower. This will prevent the Ram Rider from keeping their spawned troops out of range, allowing them to reach her and defeat her for a positive Elixir trade.\n**She can be used alongside another building-targeting card in the same [[Battle Decks|deck]]. One of them baits buildings, for the other to be able to attack afterwards without resistance. This works especially well with the [[Balloon]], as the Ram Rider can also attack air troops used to counter the Balloon.\n**Unlike the [[Hog Rider]], the sight range of the Ram Rider is shorter. This means that an [[Elixir Collector]] placed in front of the [[King's Tower]] will not distract her, and as such she should not be used with the intention to destroy the Elixir Collector.\n*In a pinch, [[:Category:Spell Cards|spells]] with knockback or stun effects like the [[Giant Snowball]], [[Fireball]], [[Lightning]], and [[Zap]] can be used to stop the Ram Rider's charge, preventing a lot of would-be damage from being dealt to the Crown Tower. However, only use them as a last resort.\n**While the [[Rocket]] has the same attributes as these aforementioned spells, its damage is high enough to completely prevent the Ram Rider from connecting to the Crown Tower. However, this is still a negative Elixir trade.\n*Her charge can be utilized with other troops in order to push them to the Tower faster. This works well with slow mini tanks like the [[Valkyrie]] and [[Battle Healer]], creating a great synergy; Ram Rider pushes them closer to the Tower while protecting them from air troops, and the mini tank distracts the Crown Tower and troops used to counter the Ram Rider.\n*The [[Skeleton Army]] and [[Minion Horde]] can completely stop a Ram Rider with quick reaction time. The P.E.K.K.A. and [[Mega Knight]] are also great counters because of their high health and high damage, with the latter also knocking the Ram Rider back when he spawns, giving more time to destroy them. Place them near the Ram Rider, or she'll snare them, immobilizing them and dealing massive damage to the Tower.\n*The [[Barbarians]] and Elite Barbarians occupy large space when placed down. Their high mass will obstruct the Ram Rider's pathing, forcing her to take a longer path, which will allow them to defeat her. Since they are swarm cards, the damage caused by the Rider on them will also be split, meaning that they have taken pretty minimal damage from her, setting up for a counterattack.\n*The [[Bowler]] and [[Zappies]] are very effective counters to the Ram Rider. The Bowler will take minimal damage from the Rider, while the Zappies will have her damage split into all three of them. In addition, their stuns and knockback will prevent her from charging and doing heavy damage to the Crown Tower, as well as lower any damage that the troops themselves would take. Note that the latter can be weakened or outright destroyed with spells like [[Fireball]] and [[Poison]].\n*When placing the Ram Rider at the bridge, it is most recommended to place her two tiles to the side of the bridge instead of directly at the center of the bridge. This will allow her to jump over the river, similar to the Hog Rider. Not only will she be further away from building distractions, allowing her to bypass them more easily, but it will also let her connect to the Crown Tower faster compared to the center bridge placement.\n*The Ram Rider is very viable on defense, given the situation. She can almost completely immobilize single troops, such as a [[Golem]], [[Giant]], [[Lava Hound]], [[Prince]], [[Monk]], or [[Mini P.E.K.K.A.]]. This makes her a great counter to most building-targeting units, especially the Hog Rider, Balloon, and herself since she can slow them down heavily and prevent a lot of damage from being dealt. She won't move fast enough to allow the unit to be freed until it has taken a lot of damage. It is a good idea to combine her with cheap swarms like [[Goblins]], [[Skeletons]], [[Guards]], [[Archers]], or [[Spear Goblins]] to stop these troops completely, while also setting up for a counterpush. \n**Deploying her in front of the building-targeting unit will cause her to collides with them and obstruct their pathing, keeping them from reaching the Tower for slightly longer.\n*She is quite decent against [[X-Bow]] and [[Mortar]] setups. The Ram Rider will be attacking the building, while the Rider attacks troops that are used to protect the building.\n**On the contrary, the player can also use the Mortar to lure her to the center of the river. The Crown Tower will keep attacking her while the Mortar will deal damage to the Crown Tower.\n*The Ram Rider can be used with Minions or [[Bats]]. These air troops can help protect the Ram Rider from counters like Goblins, Skeletons, Guards, Archers, or [[Goblin Gang]].\n*Certain spells work particularly well with the Ram Rider, due to her snare attack and ability to attack troops while she also attacks a building simultaneously.\n**The Giant Snowball pairs very well with the Ram Rider, especially on offense. The Giant Snowball will knock melee defenders away from her, while her snare will keep them where they are for the most part. Against certain swarms like Minions, the spell will bring their health low enough to be OHKOed by the Rider's attack.\n**Zap does slightly more damage than Giant Snowball, and this allows the Ram Rider to OHKO the same swarms that Giant Snowball can alongside the Ram Rider's attack. \n**[[Rage]] does similar damage to the Zap, allowing the Ram Rider to OHKO the same swarms, as well as allow her to start charging faster. The Rage will be able to affect both the Ram Rider and the Rider, allowing the spell to benefit multiple troops without the risk that comes from using it on frail swarms.\n**Similarly, the Fireball synergizes well with the Ram Rider due to the Ram Rider being able to finish off defenders like [[Musketeer]], [[Little Prince]], [[Goblinstein]], [[Wizard]], or [[Electro Wizard]], allowing the Ram Rider to deal more damage. The Fireball's knockback will also knock bulkier troops away from the Ram Rider, buying more time for her to deal more damage.\n**Due to the Ram Rider's weakness to buildings, she pairs quite well with the [[Earthquake]]. The Earthquake will destroy buildings for the Ram Rider to continue pursuing towards the Crown Tower. The Rider can also finish off troops that can survive a lone Earthquake such as a [[Dart Goblin]], [[Firecracker]], [[Bomber]], [[Archers]], or [[Princess]].\n**The Ram Rider in conjunction with the [[Freeze]] can surprise the opponent and allow the Ram Rider to deal much more damage than anticipated, especially if the opponent's go-to counter is a swarm, or swarms are their only effective counter to her. Skeletons and Bats will immediately be defeated by the spell, while Spear Goblins, Goblins, and Minions will be at low enough health to be defeated by the Ram Rider.\n***Against non-swarm troops, it can deal a lot of damage during the freeze time, but this can allow the opponent to set up a massive counterpush. For this reason, players should either only go for a Ram Rider + Freeze push when they have other units backing it up from a counterattack, or if the match is about to end and they need to deal as much damage as possible.\n*The Ram Rider can easily stop a [[Cannon Cart]]. Her attack will slowly kill the first form while her charge will deal massive damage to the second form of the Cannon Cart.\n*She is good when used against a [[Princess]] at the bridge. Whether she distracts the Princess or not, she will deal damage to her and immediately start a push on top of that, forcing the opponent to spend additional Elixir on her.\n*The Ram Rider can be countered with the [[Monk]] for a slight negative Elixir trade. Activating the ability will cause the Ram Rider's snare to be used against her. Even after the ability ends, his combo attack will knockback the Ram Rider, stopping her charge completely.\n*A [[Fisherman]] can pull the Ram Rider towards the King's Tower and cause an activation, further weakening future pushes. However, the charge will still hit the Crown Tower, so try to stop it first with a card that can inflict a stun or knockback first before the Fisherman pulls her.\n*The Ram Rider pairs well with the [[Graveyard]]. Both cards will deal damage to the Crown Towers in two different positions, and the Ram Rider will assist against Graveyard counters. However, this is an expensive push so do this as a counterattack.\n\n<center>\n<div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\">{{Statistics}}\n{{StatusEffects}}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Ram Attributes}}\n{| class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width: 100%; text-align: center;\"\n!
