class=\"quote-block\"><i><b>\u201cThe King of the undead himself. He sometimes feels lonely (could be due to his non flattering features) and will summon friends to join him in the battle even after death. Tough guys have feelings too!\u201d</b></i></div>\n<p>The Skeleton King is a <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Champion_Cards\" title=\"Category:Champion Cards\"><span class=\"text-champion\" style=\"font-weight: bold;\">Champion</span></a> <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> that is unlocked from the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Executioner's Kitchen (Arena 16)</a>. It spawns an area-damage, ground-targeting, melee, ground <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troop</a> with high hitpoints and moderate damage. The Skeleton King's ability spawns <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a> in a radius around him. While the Skeleton King is on the field, any troops which die will be turned into \u201csouls\u201d which are collected by him. The number of souls that the Skeleton King collects determines the number of Skeletons that the ability will spawn. The Skeleton King appears as a massive Skeleton wearing a menacingly large dark chestplate and gauntlets, a gray chain mail, a crown with team-colored jewels, a team-colored belt and cape, and brown sandals, and carries a giant club. A Skeleton King card costs 4 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> to deploy, and his ability costs an additional 2 Elixir to activate.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Ability:_Soul_Summoning\">Ability: Soul Summoning</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a title=\"Sign in to edit\" class=\"mw-editsection-visualeditor\" data-tracking-label=\"log-in-edit-section\" data-action=\"edit-section\" href=\"https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fclashroyale.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkeleton_King%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en&amp;metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section\" data-testid=\"log-in-edit-section\" aria-label=\"Sign in to edit\"><svg class=\"wds-icon wds-icon-tiny\" aria-hidden=\"true\" focusable=\"false\"><use xlink:href=\"#wds-icons-pencil-tiny\"></use></svg></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<div class=\"quote-block\"><i><b>\u201cCollect souls of the fallen and bring them back from the dead!\u201d</b></i></div>\n<div style=\"float: right;\"><span typeof=\"mw:File\"><span><img alt=\"Ability-Skeleton King\" src=\"data:image/gif;base64,R0lGODlhAQABAIABAAAAAP///yH5BAEAAAEALAAAAAABAAEAQAICTAEAOw%3D%3D\" decoding=\"async\" loading=\"lazy\" width=\"64\" height=\"62\" class=\"mw-file-element lazyload\" data-image-name=\"Ability-Skeleton King.png\" data-image-key=\"Ability-Skeleton_King.png\" data-relevant=\"0\" data-src=\"https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/clashroyale/images/b/b6/Ability-Skeleton_King.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/64?cb=20231027122518\" /></span></span></div>\n<p>The Skeleton King has a special ability, accessible from the rightmost side of the screen just above the player's card slots in <a href=\"/wiki/Basics_of_Battle\" title=\"Basics of Battle\">battle</a>. After a 1-second delay, the Skeleton King activates his \u201cSoul Summoning\u201d, stopping in place momentarily before summoning a varied amount of <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a> in a 4-tile radius around himself. The Skeletons spawn 1 at a time at random positions in the circle every 0.25 seconds. The number of Skeletons spawned is based on how many <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> die (either from the player or the opponent) while he is deployed. It continues, even if the Skeleton King dies.\n</p><p>When a troop is vanquished while the Skeleton King is in the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Arena</a>, the Skeleton King gains a \u201csoul\u201d, which spawns an additional Skeleton when his ability is used. With no souls, the Skeleton King will spawn 6 Skeletons, but with a maximum of 10 souls, he can summon 16 Skeletons. The amount of souls the Skeleton King has can be seen in a blue bar above his hitpoint bar. The ability goes on a 20 second cooldown after the duration ends. The Soul Summoning ability costs 2 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> to activate.\n</p><p>The Skeletons he summons have a bluish tint and behave identically to <a href=\"/wiki/Clone\" title=\"Clone\">cloned</a> Skeletons, which means you can't clone them further with said <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, and they only have 1 hitpoint. Cloned troops do not count as souls to him when they are defeated, and neither do the Skeletons summoned from the ability.\n</p><p>It is also important to note that cards that make \u201csub-troops\u201d (e.g., <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Golem\" title=\"Elixir Golem\">Elixir Golem</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golem</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Lava_Hound\" title=\"Lava Hound\">Lava Hound</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a>) do not count as a soul to it, but only the final forms of the troop, with <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Giant\" title=\"Goblin Giant\">Goblin Giant</a> as an exception. <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">Buildings</a> also do not count as souls when vanquished, making <a href=\"/wiki/Cannon_Cart\" title=\"Cannon Cart\">Cannon Cart</a> the only troop that cannot count as a soul. \u201cKamikaze cards\u201d such as Spirits, <a href=\"/wiki/Wall_Breakers\" title=\"Wall Breakers\">Wall Breakers</a>, and the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Demolisher\" title=\"Goblin Demolisher\">Goblin Demolisher</a> however do count as souls when they defeat themselves.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Strategy\">Strategy</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a title=\"Sign in to edit\" class=\"mw-editsection-visualeditor\" data-tracking-label=\"log-in-edit-section\" data-action=\"edit-section\" href=\"https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fclashroyale.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSkeleton_King%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&amp;uselang=en&amp;metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section\" data-testid=\"log-in-edit-section\" aria-label=\"Sign in to edit\"><svg class=\"wds-icon wds-icon-tiny\" aria-hidden=\"true\" focusable=\"false\"><use xlink:href=\"#wds-icons-pencil-tiny\"></use></svg></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Skeleton_King_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Skeleton King Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>For the purposes of determining <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> trades, the Skeleton King and his activated Ability count as 6 Elixir, as the player must play the 4 Elixir Skeleton King and then spend a further 2 Elixir on his Ability.</li>\n<li>Since friendly <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> will count as a soul to him, you can place a swarm such as a <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Army\" title=\"Skeleton Army\">Skeleton Army</a> while he is around and punish whenever the army gets countered with a small <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spell</a>. This works well in swarm-heavy <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">decks</a>.</li>\n<li>To counter, simply place a small spell to defeat the Skeletons, and place a ranged or air troop to counter the Skeleton King. A decently tough splash card such as <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>, and Bowler can also be used.</li>\n<li>A good way of countering the Skeleton King's activated ability is by using a combination of a cheap splash and single-target unit, such as a <a href=\"/wiki/Firecracker\" title=\"Firecracker\">Firecracker</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Princess\" title=\"Princess\">Princess</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Wizard\" title=\"Ice Wizard\">Ice Wizard</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber\" title=\"Bomber\">Bomber</a> combined with a <a href=\"/wiki/Knight\" title=\"Knight\">Knight</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Berserker\" title=\"Berserker\">Berserker</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Fisherman\" title=\"Fisherman\">Fisherman</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bandit\" title=\"Bandit\">Bandit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Miner\" title=\"Miner\">Miner</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Minion\" title=\"Mega Minion\">Mega Minion</a>. Any combination of units up to 6 Elixir or less will achieve this for a neutral or positive trade.</li>\n<li>He has a suspiciously large hitbox, and can bodyblock the <a href=\"/wiki/Hog_Rider\" title=\"Hog Rider\">Hog Rider</a> more easily than other tanks.</li>\n<li>He also has a suspiciously high mass, and he can even push large troops such as a <a href=\"/wiki/Giant\" title=\"Giant\">Giant</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Giant\" title=\"Electro Giant\">Electro Giant</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golem</a>. You can even use this to your advantage by using him with ranged win conditions like the <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Giant\" title=\"Royal Giant\">Royal Giant</a> to push him away from buildings.</li>\n<li>He and his <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a> are weak to air troops. Beware of anti-air spells and support troops that may be paired with the Skeleton King.</li>\n<li>The Skeleton King is an excellent defensive <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, owing to his splash damage, good HP, and ability to get stronger when opposing units fall. Place him behind other friendly units on defense (preferably a swarm), then cast Soul Summoning once those units are defeated to summon a second defensive layer out of nowhere. This is especially strong if the enemy's units lock on to the Skeleton King, as he will naturally tank for his summoned Skeletons and any other defending units the player has down.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Bowler\" title=\"Bowler\">Bowler</a> or Valkyrie is an excellent counter to the Skeleton King, as their relatively high health allow them to tank the Skeleton King as his attack damage is rather low. Their large splash damage radius takes out the spawning Skeletons while damaging the Skeleton King and is a positive Elixir trade either way.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Mother_Witch\" title=\"Mother Witch\">Mother Witch</a> can take out his Skeletons easily, distracting him with a flow of Cursed Hogs, allowing you to gather Elixir for a better counter card. Beware, the Mother Witch will also make them a cloned Cursed Hog with 1 Hitpoint.</li>\n<li>The Skeleton King pairs really well with the <a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a>, as after the opponent defeats the Graveyard, you can summon another \"graveyard\" by activating his ability for 2 Elixir.\n<ul><li>The Skeleton King can tank for the Graveyard, allow the Skeletons to quickly deal massive damage to the <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Tower_Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Tower Troop Cards\">Crown Tower.</a></li>\n<li>The Graveyard could also be tanking for the Skeleton King, the Skeletons will be eliminated faster, and the Skeleton King will summon more Skeletons and tank for them.</li>\n<li>On the contrary, Skeleton King is also great against the Graveyard, as he can summon a full flock of 16 Skeletons as a threatening counter push.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Skeleton King can fully counter a <a href=\"/wiki/P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"P.E.K.K.A.\">P.E.K.K.A.</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Elite_Barbarians\" title=\"Elite Barbarians\">Elite Barbarians</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mighty_Miner\" title=\"Mighty Miner\">Mighty Miner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Skeleton\" title=\"Giant Skeleton\">Giant Skeleton</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Boss_Bandit\" title=\"Boss Bandit\">Boss Bandit</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Mini_P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"Mini P.E.K.K.A.\">Mini P.E.K.K.A.</a> with the ability active, as the Skeletons will constantly distract the slow-hitting troops and be chipped down by your Crown Tower with the Skeleton King, regardless of soul amount.</li>\n<li>The Skeleton King can also fully counter a <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a> up to 1 Level higher if the Skeleton King is placed near the center so both Towers can chip the Mega Knight. The Skeleton King has high enough health to tank the jump attack from the Mega Knight, and his regular attacks.</li>\n<li>Do not use the ability, unless you have something tanking for the leftover Skeletons from the ability in the lane they are going to go in, otherwise it is a waste of 2 Elixir.</li>\n<li>The Skeleton King can be placed as a defender in <a href=\"/wiki/Clan_Wars\" title=\"Clan Wars\">Boat Battles</a>. However, much like other <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Champion_Cards\" title=\"Category:Champion Cards\"><span class=\"text-champion\" style=\"font-weight: bold;\">Champion</span></a> cards, he is unable to use his ability. As such, he should not be set for Defense Towers in Clan Wars, and it is better to seek for other alternatives.</li></ul>\n<center><div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\"><h2 style=\"display: This also made it so that players at any <a href=\"/wiki/Experience\" title=\"Experience\">King Level</a> could unlock the Skeleton King, as opposed to only Level 14.
