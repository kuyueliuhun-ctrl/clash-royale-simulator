Summon her as a hard-hitting ground troop for 3 Elixir, or atop her majestic spirit dragon for 6 Elixir.}}\n\nThe Spirit Empress is a {{Rarity|Legendary}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from the [[Arenas|Miner's Mine (Arena 15)]]. It spawns a single-target, air-targeting, medium-ranged, air troop with high hitpoints and high damage, or a single-target, ground-targeting, melee, ground troop with moderate hitpoints and high damage, depending on its [[Elixir]] cost. She appears as a woman who bears light blonde eyebrows and hair with two buns tied with team-colored knots, has heterochromic eyes, one golden and one blue, wears golden wristbands, an armor similar to the [[Battle Healer]]'s, a team-colored cape and boots, and wields a scimitar with a light blue glow and a blue handle. She also appears to ride a blue dragon that looks similar to an [[Electro Dragon]], except that it is paler and has glowing eyes. A Spirit Empress card costs 6 Elixir to deploy if the player has 6 or more Elixir stored, and 3 Elixir to deploy otherwise.\n\n== Strategies ==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Spirit Empress Card}}\n*Like the [[Mega Minion]], the Spirit Empress deals high damage per attack in both forms, while having slightly higher hitpoints in both forms. This allows her to have a great matchup against the vast majority of troops in the game, while also being mostly resistant to spells.\n*Using [[Mirror]] on the Spirit Empress will copy the form which the previous Spirit Empress was used. For example: if the player used the 3-Elixir version of the Spirit Empress prior to using the Mirror, then the mirrored version will cost 4 [[Elixir]] and will have the attributes of the 3-Elixir form at one Level higher. The cost of the mirrored version will not increase even if it reaches the threshold required to play the more expensive form.\n*Since she only targets one troop at a time, swarms such as [[Goblins]], [[Barbarians]], [[Royal Recruits]], [[Guards]], and [[Skeletons/Evolution|Evolved Skeletons]] make great counters to the ground form of the Spirit Empress. For her flying form, swarms that can target air troops like [[Spear Goblins]], [[Minions]], [[Bats]], and [[Archers]] are more recommended to stop her.\n*A [[Rocket]] can OHKO the Spirit Empress in both of her forms, making it a good counter to her. In the case of her flying form, it is also a neutral Elixir trade.\n**[[Lightning]] does not deal enough damage to OHKO the Spirit Empress, so it should not be used against her\n**Unlike other cards with similar hitpoints, she has enough hitpoints to survive any combination of [[Fireball]] or [[Poison]] with any 2-Elixir spell, so they should not be used against her.\n*The Spirit Empress works best with cards like [[Golem]], [[Giant]], [[Royal Giant]], and [[Lava Hound]]. Using the cheap ground version for emergency defense, while using the air card for offense.\n\n<center><div id=\"unit-statistics\" class=\"table-back\">{{Statistics}}\n{{StatusEffects}}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Spirit Empress Attributes}}\n{|class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table\" style=\"width: 100%; text-align: center;\"\n!scope=\"col\" |Deploy Time<br/>{{Icon|I=Deploy Time}}\n!scope=\"col\" |Count<br />{{Icon|I=Troop Count}}\n!scope=\"col\" |Type<br/>{{Icon|I=Type}}\n!scope=\"col\" |Rarity<br />{{Icon|I=Rarity}}\n|-\n|1 sec||1||[[:Category:Troop Cards|Troop]]||{{Rarity|Legendary}}\n|}\n\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Air Form Attributes}}\n{|class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table-secondary\" style=\"width:100%;text-align:center;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |Cost<br />{{Icon|I=Elixir}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |First Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n!scope=\"col\" |Speed<br/>{{Icon|I=Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Range<br />{{Icon|I=Range}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Target<br />{{Icon|I=Targets}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Transport<br />{{Icon|I=Transport}}\n|-\n|6||1.6 sec||0.6 sec||Medium (60)||5||Air & Ground||Air\n|}\n\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Ground Form Attributes}}\n{|class=\"wikitable\" id=\"unit-attributes-table-tertiary\" style=\"width:100%;text-align:center;\"\n! scope=\"col\" |Cost<br />{{Icon|I=Elixir}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |First Hit Speed<br />{{Icon|I=Damage Speed}}\n!scope=\"col\" |Speed<br/>{{Icon|I=Speed}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Range<br />{{Icon|I=Range}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Target<br />{{Icon|I=Targets}}\n! scope=\"col\" |Transport<br />{{Icon|I=Transport}}\n|-\n|3||1.2 sec||0.3 sec||Fast (90)||Melee: Medium (1.2)||Ground||Ground\n|}\n{{StatisticsSubheader|Card Statistics}}\n<!--\nthis will not be shown\n================================================================\nATTENTION EDITORS: DO NOT MANUALLY UPDATE THE TABLE CELLS!
