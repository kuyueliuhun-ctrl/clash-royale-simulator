If the troop is in the air, they'll ground them. Don't worry, they'll grow on you!}}\n\nThe Vines is an {{Rarity|Epic}} [[Cards|card]] that is unlocked from the [[Arenas|Frozen Peak (Arena 8)]]. It is a multi-damage, air-targeting [[:Category:Spell Cards|spell]] with a small radius and high damage per second. When used, it will freeze the troop in place and render it unable to move or attack, and allow ground units to hit the trapped unit for 2.5 seconds. The three troops that are targeted are always the three that have the highest hitpoints within its radius. It appears as an aquamarine-colored potion encased in a large, diamond-shaped vial, that is strangled with a curling vine with thorns. It also has a lid which is made of wood with a thick, large thorn on top of it. A Vines card costs 3 [[Elixir]] to deploy.\n\n==Modifiers==\n===[[File:Vines Common Modifier.png|45x45px]] Common: All Tangled Up===\n{{Quote|+100% radius & x2 targets}}\nVines radius is doubled and can affect twice the amount of units at once.\n\n===[[File:Vines Rare Modifier.png|45x45px]] Rare: Jungle Fever===\n{{Quote|+1 Suspicious Bush}}\nSends in a [[Suspicious Bush]] when deployed.\n\n===[[File:Vines Epic Modifier.png|45x45px]] Epic: Entangling Endeavor===\n{{Quote|Infinite roots}}\nVines duration is heavily extended.\n\n==Strategy==\n{{Battle Deck with Card|:Category:Battle Decks using the Vines Card}}\n*The Vines deal slightly less damage than Arrows for the same [[Elixir]] cost, allowing them to take out any unit up to [[Wall Breakers]] or [[Bomber]] within its duration.\n*This card, along with [[Arrows]], is the only two cards able to fully counter an [[Skeleton Army/Evolution|Evolved Skeleton Army]] for an equal Elixir trade. However for Vines, you need perfect placement and timing to pull it off, therefore making Arrows a better option.\n*The Vines act as a cheaper alternative to the [[Freeze]] spell, costing 1 less Elixir but with the drawback of only being able to hold down 3 targets, within a smaller radius, and having a shorter duration.
