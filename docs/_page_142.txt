Everybody chill. Reduced damage to Crown Towers.\u201d</b></i></div>\n<p>The Freeze is an <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Epic_Cards\" title=\"Category:Epic Cards\"><span style=\"color: #FF99FF; font-weight: bold;\">Epic</span></a> <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> that is unlocked from the <a href=\"/wiki/Arenas\" title=\"Arenas\">Frozen Peak (Arena 8)</a>. It is an area-damage, air-targeting <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spell</a> with a medium radius and low damage. When used, it can stun enemies in its radius for 4 seconds. It appears as a cyan liquid bottled inside a transparent jar. A Freeze&#160;card costs 4&#160;<a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> to deploy.\n</p>\n<h2><span class=\"mw-headline\" id=\"Strategy\">Strategy</span><span class=\"mw-editsection\"><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">[</span><a title=\"Sign in to edit\" class=\"mw-editsection-visualeditor\" data-tracking-label=\"log-in-edit-section\" data-action=\"edit-section\" href=\"https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fclashroyale.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FFreeze%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&amp;uselang=en&amp;metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section\" data-testid=\"log-in-edit-section\" aria-label=\"Sign in to edit\"><svg class=\"wds-icon wds-icon-tiny\" aria-hidden=\"true\" focusable=\"false\"><use xlink:href=\"#wds-icons-pencil-tiny\"></use></svg></a><span class=\"mw-editsection-bracket\">]</span></span></h2>\n<p><i>For <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">Battle Decks</a> with this <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a>, click <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Battle_Decks_using_the_Freeze_Card\" title=\"Category:Battle Decks using the Freeze Card\">here</a>.</i>\n</p>\n<ul><li>The Freeze is most effective when it is timed to freeze enemy units right before one or more of the player's units is defeated. If the player has not yet revealed the Freeze <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Spell_Cards\" title=\"Category:Spell Cards\">spell</a> to their opponent, it can&#160;turn what may have seemed like a clean defense from the opponent into massive damage for the Freeze user.</li>\n<li>While the Freeze can be deadly,&#160;it&#160;comes with a high risk. Due to the one-second delay before a <a href=\"/wiki/Cards\" title=\"Cards\">card</a> is placed, it is easy to mistime and cast it after either the player's <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Troop_Cards\" title=\"Category:Troop Cards\">troops</a> or the opponent's troops are already defeated, resulting in a waste of 4 <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a>. This will leave the player fairly vulnerable to a counterattack.</li>\n<li>The Freeze's effect is identical to <a href=\"/wiki/Zap\" title=\"Zap\">Zap</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Lightning\" title=\"Lightning\">Lightning</a>; the attack animation of any affected units will be paused, and resume when the effect wears off. It is not like <a href=\"/wiki/The_Log\" title=\"The Log\">The Log</a>'s effect, where the attack animation of affected units will be completely restarted.\n<ul><li>Freeze is effective at stopping <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Tower\" title=\"Inferno Tower\">Inferno Towers</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mighty_Miner\" title=\"Mighty Miner\">Mighty Miners</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Dragon\" title=\"Inferno Dragon\">Inferno Dragons</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Little_Prince\" title=\"Little Prince\">Little Princes</a> since it can reset their damage or attack speed ramp-up and disable them for a few seconds. Similarly, effects like dashing (<a href=\"/wiki/Bandit\" title=\"Bandit\">Bandit</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Dark_Prince\" title=\"Dark Prince\">Dark Prince</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Royal_Recruits/Evolution\" title=\"Royal Recruits/Evolution\">Evolved Royal Recruits</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Boss_Bandit\" title=\"Boss Bandit\">Boss Bandit</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Ram_Rider\" title=\"Ram Rider\">Ram Rider</a>), the attack charge up (<a href=\"/wiki/Sparky\" title=\"Sparky\">Sparky</a>), jumping (<a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Knight\" title=\"Mega Knight\">Mega Knight</a>), and grabbing (<a href=\"/wiki/Fisherman\" title=\"Fisherman\">Fisherman</a>) will be reset after the Freeze wears off.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Always separate defending troops and <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Building_Cards\" title=\"Category:Building Cards\">buildings</a> from each other, to prevent them from having all of them frozen. Alternatively, the player can use a weaker counter to the opposing card to bait the Freeze, so that their stronger counter can be used without having to worry about being frozen.\n<ul><li>Ranged troops placed on the bridge or one tile away from the bridge will be far enough from the <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Crown_Towers\" title=\"Category:Crown Towers\">Crown Tower</a> that the Freeze will not be able to affect both them and the Crown Tower. Keep this in mind when defending against an attacking troop that is going to be supported with Freeze.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The Freeze relies a lot on the surprise factor to be able to be utilized effectively. Because of this, it is more effective to switch up the cards that is used alongside, otherwise the opponent will be able to predict the spell and defend the attacking troops accordingly.</li>\n<li>The Freeze's damage output is low, and is only enough to fully defeat <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bats\" title=\"Bats\">Bats</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Suspicious_Bush\" title=\"Suspicious Bush\">Suspicious Bushes</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Barrel/Evolution\" title=\"Goblin Barrel/Evolution\">Decoy Goblins</a>. However, when combined with another low cost spell, like Zap, it can defeat some bulkier troops such as <a href=\"/wiki/Goblins\" title=\"Goblins\">Goblins</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Spear_Goblins\" title=\"Spear Goblins\">Spear Goblins</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Minions\" title=\"Minions\">Minions</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Rascals\" title=\"Rascals\">Rascal Girls</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Princess\" title=\"Princess\">Princesses</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Dart_Goblin\" title=\"Dart Goblin\">Dart Goblins</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Archers\" title=\"Archers\">Archers</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Firecracker\" title=\"Firecracker\">Firecrackers</a>.\n<ul><li>Unlike regular Bats, the <a href=\"/wiki/Bats/Evolution\" title=\"Bats/Evolution\">Evolved Bats</a> are able to survive a Freeze, even if they have not healed themselves at all. As such the spell should not be used the Freeze on Evolved Bats, as this is a -2 Elixir trade.</li></ul></li>\n<li>If a troop or building which spawns&#160;other units after death is defeated while frozen, such as an <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Golem\" title=\"Elixir Golem\">Elixir Golem</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Tombstone\" title=\"Tombstone\">Tombstone</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Lava_Hound\" title=\"Lava Hound\">Lava Hound</a>, or a <a href=\"/wiki/Golem\" title=\"Golem\">Golem</a>, the spawned troops will remain frozen until the duration of the Freeze ends. This does not apply to troops with a staggered deployment after death, like the Spear Goblins from the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Giant\" title=\"Goblin Giant\">Goblin Giant</a> or the <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a>'s <a href=\"/wiki/Barbarians\" title=\"Barbarians\">Barbarians</a>, making Freeze less effective against them.</li>\n<li>Any&#160;lifetimers of frozen buildings will still count down while frozen. This can be useful against the <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Collector\" title=\"Elixir Collector\">Elixir Collector</a> and spawner buildings, though it is usually not a valuable play unless other threatening units are near the building and the player is currently attacking in the same lane as the building.\n<ul><li>However, an Elixir Collector that was already full will still generate its stored Elixir even if it is frozen. As such, it is not a good idea to use Freeze against an Elixir Collector on a player who is leaking Elixir.</li>\n<li>Unlike most other spells, the Freeze is able to affect the <a href=\"/wiki/Tesla\" title=\"Tesla\">Tesla</a> even if it is underground. This allows fast-moving troops such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Ram\" title=\"Battle Ram\">Battle Ram</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Wall_Breakers\" title=\"Wall Breakers\">Wall Breakers</a> or longer-ranged troops like the Dart Goblin and Magic Archer to bypass a Tesla and head straight for the Crown Tower, as it can't be targeted while hidden underground. Alternatively, if it has not retreated, the player can use Freeze on it to allow those longer-ranged troops to attack it before it gets the chance to retreat again.</li></ul></li>\n<li>If a frozen troop is <a href=\"/wiki/Clone\" title=\"Clone\">Cloned</a>, the clone won't be frozen. This can be used to surprise an opponent and get the drop on an undefended Crown Tower.</li>\n<li>The Freeze card can be effectively used in combination with the <a href=\"/wiki/Balloon\" title=\"Balloon\">Balloon</a> to deal immense damage to buildings and Crown Towers. The Freeze should be cast once the Balloon approaches the Crown Tower, disabling the enemy Tower and also any troops deployed to defend it. On the contrary, Freeze can also help destroy a Balloon. By deploying the Freeze with high damage output air targeting units, such as the <a href=\"/wiki/Mega_Minion\" title=\"Mega Minion\">Mega Minion</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Hunter\" title=\"Hunter\">Hunter</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Wizard\" title=\"Wizard\">Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Executioner\" title=\"Executioner\">Executioner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Phoenix\" title=\"Phoenix\">Phoenix</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Musketeer\" title=\"Musketeer\">Musketeer</a>, a Balloon can easily be destroyed before it reaches a Crown Tower.</li>\n<li>The Freeze card pairs very well with <a href=\"/wiki/Graveyard\" title=\"Graveyard\">Graveyard</a>. Where normally a Graveyard push requires a tank to be effective, a Graveyard Freeze combo functions similarly without requiring a tank. This also disables any ground splashers and swarm deployed to stop the Graveyard, giving the spawned Skeletons plenty of time to take them out, thus weakening their counter push significantly. This is especially useful against players who defend against the Graveyard by using <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_Army\" title=\"Skeleton Army\">Skeleton Army</a> or Bats, as the Freeze is capable of defeating them without the help of the Skeletons. Keep in mind that a <a href=\"/wiki/Poison\" title=\"Poison\">Poison</a> spell will stop this combo for 4 Elixir, so the Poison should be baited out before performing this combo.\n<ul><li>The opponent might be able to predict the Freeze if the Graveyard is played without a tank. Try to use a tank to keep the surprise factor, while also allowing the tank to take advantage of the lack of counters.</li></ul></li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Hog_Rider\" title=\"Hog Rider\">Hog Rider</a> in conjunction with the Freeze can surprise the opponent and allow the Hog Rider to deal much more damage than anticipated, especially if the opponent's go-to counter is a swarm, or swarms are their only effective counter to him. Skeletons and Bats will immediately be defeated by the spell, while Spear Goblins, Goblins, and Minions will be at low enough health to be defeated by a follow up small spell.\n<ul><li>Against non-swarm troops, it can deal a lot of damage during the freeze time, but this can allow the opponent to set up a massive counterpush. For this reason, players should either only go for a Hog Rider + Freeze when they have other units backing it up from a counterattack, or if the match is about to end and they need to deal as much damage as possible.</li>\n<li>A building placed directly at the river is far enough that will lure the Hog Rider and prevent the Freeze from affecting both the Crown Tower and the building. However, this position is easily sniped by long-ranged support troops like the Musketeer, <a href=\"/wiki/Flying_Machine\" title=\"Flying Machine\">Flying Machine</a>, Firecracker, Dart Goblin, and <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Archer\" title=\"Magic Archer\">Magic Archer</a> or even shorter-ranged ones like the <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Dragon\" title=\"Electro Dragon\">Electro Dragon</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Baby_Dragon\" title=\"Baby Dragon\">Baby Dragon</a>.</li>\n<li>The <a href=\"/wiki/Ram_Rider\" title=\"Ram Rider\">Ram Rider</a> can be used with the Freeze in a similar way, although that is at a higher 9 Elixir cost. Unlike the Hog Rider, there is less risk for a threatening counterattack due to Ram Rider weakening them with her snare.</li></ul></li>\n<li>An extremely strong synergizer with the Freeze is the <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Dragon\" title=\"Inferno Dragon\">Inferno Dragon</a>. Freeze helps stop enemy units from attacking, letting the Inferno Dragon safely charge up its beam. If done fast enough, it could even play around the <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Wizard\" title=\"Electro Wizard\">Electro Wizard</a> and <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Dragon\" title=\"Electro Dragon\">Electro Dragon</a>.</li>\n<li>Freeze can be a situational synergy with the <a href=\"/wiki/Goblin_Giant/Evolution\" title=\"Goblin Giant/Evolution\">Evolved Goblin Giant</a> as its 4 second freeze duration allow the Spear Goblins to attack more of the opposing defenses while also giving extra time for the Goblins to spawn. Single target attackers, such as <a href=\"/wiki/Prince\" title=\"Prince\">Prince</a>, Mighty Miner, <a href=\"/wiki/P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"P.E.K.K.A.\">P.E.K.K.A</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Monk\" title=\"Monk\">Monk</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Mini_P.E.K.K.A.\" title=\"Mini P.E.K.K.A.\">Mini P.E.K.K.A</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Boss_Bandit\" title=\"Boss Bandit\">Boss Bandit</a>, and <a href=\"/wiki/Lumberjack\" title=\"Lumberjack\">Lumberjack</a> will be overwhelmed by the influx of Goblins if not responded to properly.</li>\n<li>The Freeze's radius is big enough to affect both the Crown Tower and the <a href=\"/wiki/King%27s_Tower\" title=\"King&#39;s Tower\">King's Tower</a> at once. The player can use this to their advantage if they have already destroyed one Crown Tower. When their troops come to destroy another, they can cast the Freeze in between the two Towers, disabling both of them.\n<ul><li>If the player has already lost a Crown Tower, they should deploy any defending troops away from the King Tower. This is to prevent the opponent from being able to use Freeze on the second Crown Tower, the King Tower and the defending troop, and force them to choose between freezing the defending troop or the King Tower.</li>\n<li>Avoid playing it near the enemy King's Tower as it may damage and activate it. The Freeze will not affect the King Tower's activation animation, so it will be ready to attack immediately after it thaws.</li>\n<li>Freeze can temporarily mitigate the issue of an activated King Tower, as it can be used to temporarily disable the King Tower, and the Crown Tower. However, it will limit where the player can deploy their spell, thus making it easier to counter any attacking troop with it.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Although situational, it can&#160;be used defensively to help stop incoming troops such as the P.E.K.K.A., Prince, <a href=\"/wiki/Giant_Skeleton\" title=\"Giant Skeleton\">Giant Skeleton</a>, Golem, Mini P.E.K.K.A., Mighty Miner, Inferno Dragon, Hog Rider, or <a href=\"/wiki/Elite_Barbarians\" title=\"Elite Barbarians\">Elite Barbarians</a>, which can be devastating if it reaches an Arena Tower. It is also good at simply stalling troops when both the player and the opponent are racing to take each other's tower in Sudden Death, granting the edge to the player.</li>\n<li>Certain cards can pair quite well with Freeze on defense and can quickly defend large pushes.\n<ul><li>Freeze + <a href=\"/wiki/Valkyrie\" title=\"Valkyrie\">Valkyrie</a> is a very effective combo. With the Valkyrie's moderately high splash damage, the player can use Freeze on the support troops behind a tank (Musketeer, Magic Archer, Wizard, <a href=\"/wiki/Ice_Wizard\" title=\"Ice Wizard\">Ice Wizard</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Wizard\" title=\"Electro Wizard\">Electro Wizard</a>, Hunter, <a href=\"/wiki/Witch\" title=\"Witch\">Witch</a>, Princess, Archers, Firecracker, Spear Goblins, <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber\" title=\"Bomber\">Bomber</a>, etc.) then use the Valkyrie to wipe them out.</li>\n<li>Similarly, Freeze + Skeleton Army is capable of destroying many ground based splash-damaging troops, like Valkyrie, Mega Knight, Dark Prince, and <a href=\"/wiki/Skeleton_King\" title=\"Skeleton King\">Skeleton King</a>, and for 1 less Elixir. However, it is very volatile, as the Skeleton Army is frail and vulnerable to spells. Freeze + Barbarians works in a similar way, but it costs 2 more Elixir and is still weak to stronger spells like <a href=\"/wiki/Fireball\" title=\"Fireball\">Fireball</a>.</li></ul></li>\n<li>In a pinch, it can be used on an <a href=\"/wiki/Electro_Giant\" title=\"Electro Giant\">Electro Giant</a> to freeze him. A frozen Electro Giant will not deflect back damage, which will allow your defending troops to defeat him easier, or for your attacking units to break through the defensive Electro Giant. However, for the latter, it is a heavy Elixir investment, so only do this if you are certain that the attacking troops will defeat him.\n<ul><li>Conversely, the Freeze should not be used with the Electro Giant, as freezing enemy troops will remove their ability to attack the Electro Giant, preventing him from hitting them with his Zap Pack. An exception can apply to cards placed out of his Zap Pack range, such as ranged troops.</li></ul></li>\n<li>Very rarely, it can be used to keep a troop in place, so that area-damage troops can deal damage to the Tower along with the troop. This works best with troops whose attack pierces through enemies like the Magic Archer, Firecracker, <a href=\"/wiki/Executioner\" title=\"Executioner\">Executioner</a>, <a href=\"/wiki/Bomber/Evolution\" title=\"Bomber/Evolution\">Evolved Bomber</a>, or <a href=\"/wiki/Bowler\" title=\"Bowler\">Bowler</a>, However, this is riskier compared to using a <a href=\"/wiki/Tornado\" title=\"Tornado\">Tornado</a> for the same purpose.</li>\n<li>The Freeze lasts longer than the <a href=\"/wiki/Archer_Queen\" title=\"Archer Queen\">Archer Queen</a>'s ability, which makes it a great way to neutralize the ability. However, this is a heavy Elixir investment for offense and will still require the use of another card on defense.</li>\n<li>Depending on whether the player wants to prioritize pure damage or the stun, the player can use the Lightning instead of the Freeze in their <a href=\"/wiki/Battle_Decks\" title=\"Battle Decks\">deck</a>. Lightning deals a lot more damage, being able to outright destroy several troops while also being able to stun anything that survives it, making it more useful against the Inferno Tower. However, it has a higher <a href=\"/wiki/Category:6-Elixir_Cards\" title=\"Category:6-Elixir Cards\">6-Elixir</a> cost as opposed to the <a href=\"/wiki/Category:4-Elixir_Cards\" title=\"Category:4-Elixir Cards\">4-Elixir</a> Freeze.</li>\n<li>The Freeze spell is very effective in the <a href=\"/wiki/Global_Spell#Rage\" title=\"Global Spell\">Rage</a> sub-mode of the <a href=\"/wiki/Global_Spell\" title=\"Global Spell\">Global Spell</a> game mode, as it can stun defending troops while your attacking troop take advantage of the Rage boost of the Arena. This works very well with non-building-targeting troops, because it may allow them to clear the way on offense, and allow themselves to connect to the Crown Tower.</li>\n<li>Using Freeze against the Safe in <a href=\"/wiki/Heist\" title=\"Heist\">Heist</a> is a waste because it cannot attack. This can allow the user to deploy their Freeze directly on the defending troops instead, without worrying about missing the Safe.</li>\n<li>Since Freeze's duration is identical at all Levels, and its damage is extremely low, upgrading it is generally wasteful if the player wishes to only stun enemy troops and buildings, At equal Levels, a Freeze spell can only OHKO Bats or <a href=\"/wiki/Skeletons\" title=\"Skeletons\">Skeletons</a>, and a Level 12 Freeze Spell can OHKO Level 15 Bats and Skeletons. Therefore, it is recommended that the player invest their <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Epic_Cards\" title=\"Category:Epic Cards\"><span style=\"color: #FF99FF; font-weight: bold;\">Epic</span></a> resources, (i.e., <a href=\"/wiki/Magic_Items\" title=\"Magic Items\">Wild Cards</a> or <a href=\"/wiki/Trade_Tokens\" title=\"Trade Tokens\">Trade Tokens</a>) into other <a href=\"/wiki/Category:Epic_Cards\" title=\"Category:Epic Cards\"><span style=\"color: #FF99FF; font-weight: bold;\">Epic</span></a> cards first. However, a Level 12 Freeze cannot OHKO <a href=\"/wiki/Mirror\" title=\"Mirror\">Mirrored</a> Level 16 Skeletons and Bats, so the player should consider levelling it to 13.</li></ul>\n<center>\n<div href=\"/wiki/Version_History#Tournaments_Update_(4/7/2016)\" title=\"Version History\">Tournaments Update</a>, allowed the Freeze to stop the <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir_Collector\" title=\"Elixir Collector\">Elixir Collector</a>'s <a href=\"/wiki/Elixir\" title=\"Elixir\">Elixir</a> generation and reset the <a href=\"/wiki/Inferno_Tower\" title=\"Inferno Tower\">Inferno Tower</a>'s damage
